# ParkFolio finding and "folio" name variants

Read-only research, Nov 16 2026. Not legal advice. "No brand found" means "not surfaced in top results". Nothing in the repo was modified and no .env file was read. Scratch files are in `%TEMP%\folioscreen`.

## 1. Answer first

**You can't use ParkFolio, and "it isn't theme-park related" does not rescue it.** Two separate things already use the name, and the app is in your App Store category.

1. **The iOS app "ParkFolio"** (verified via iTunes lookup, id 6759869900). It is a National Park Service visit journal and passport-stamp tracker.
   - Developer: Deepa Gupta, an individual. One other app, PadYatra. Bundle id `com.kyra.parkpulse.ParkFolio`.
   - Category: Travel (also Lifestyle). Price: free, with no ads or account.
   - Features: visit log with star ratings, who you went with and total cost; stamp collection; badges; a map; progress stats.
   - Released 2026-03-19. The current version release date is the same day (v1.0), so it has never been updated. It has 0 ratings. It requires iOS 26.2 or later.
   - Websites: deepa-gupta.github.io/parkfolio and parkfolio.deepagupta.me (verified).
   - Status: new, tiny, and apparently unmaintained for 8 months. It is still live and owns the exact name.
2. **"Parkfolio" is also a parking product.** Parkeon (now part of Flowbird) sells "Parkfolio", "Parkfolio Neo" and "Parkfolio Control" back-office software for pay-and-display parking terminals (parking.net press items, informer.com listing). Verified from search results. `parkfolio.com` is Parkeon's old site. Its homepage is a "select your country" page linking to parkeon.com. RDAP shows it was registered 2000-08-24, last changed 2026-10-22 and expires 2028-08-24, so it is held on a long cycle. Flowbird also sells "Smartfolio", so "-folio" is part of that company's naming. I found no "Parkfolio" trademark record in my search, but I could not search USPTO directly.

**Can you coexist?**
- App Store: **no**. Apple's guidance says a name already used by another app can't be reused. Support told a developer on the Apple forums that an app name must not be used by another app in the same localization (forum thread, user-reported). Apple's own help page says the same for names within your own account. Verified: those statements exist. Inferred: App Store Connect would reject the exact name "ParkFolio" and I expect "Park Folio" or "Parkfolio" to hit the same normalized match. Apple has reportedly accepted names that differ only by a space or punctuation, but a near-identical name in the same category would still hit your hard filter F3 and invite a dispute.
- Niche: it is a visit-log app in Travel with ratings, cost, stamps and badges. That overlaps your visit logging, Vault and expenses, so it is not a clearly different niche.
- Risk if the developer ever updates: low effort for them to file a name complaint against a lookalike. Parkeon/Flowbird is a second claimant in the parking class.
- Google Play: a web search for ParkFolio surfaced nothing. I could not check Play directly.
- Domains: `parkfolio.com` is held by Parkeon. `parkfolio.app` is unregistered (Google RDAP returns 404, verified), but that only helps if you accept the name conflict, which you shouldn't.

A side finding that helps the folio idea: at Disney, **"folio" is the itemized resort-bill receipt** that Disney emails at checkout (Disney's own plandisney Q&A pages). Fans know the word. That ties to your expenses feature, but the word also reads as "a bill" to Disney regulars.

## 2. Screen method and results

- 151 new names generated (after dropping duplicates and one name that was already in earlier reports: Park Folio).
- Exact match = text before any ':' / ' - ' / '|', non-alphanumerics removed, lowercase. Contains = the key appears in any other trackName. iTunes US, limit 100. Two-word forms were also searched with spaces removed.
- **123 clean, 22 exact App Store hits, 6 contains-only hits.**
- Dropped on exact hits (a sample): Keepfolio (3), Tripfolio (6), Pinfolio, Wishfolio (2), Playfolio (3), Trailfolio (2), Journeyfolio (a trip planner), Dayfolio, Kinfolio, Funfolio, Gofolio, Hopfolio, Passfolio, Roamfolio, Snapfolio, Stampfolio, Timefolio, Vaultfolio, Wristfolio, Fotofolio, Foliant, FolioDay.
- Contains-only hits dropped for caution: Foliobook (Folio book-club apps), Folyo (a Folyo expense tracker), Stub Folio (StubFolio paystub app), Voyafolio (Trips & Safebox), Fieldfolio.
- The "-folio" suffix is itself crowded: 22 of 151 invented compounds already exist as App Store names. Many are in Travel, Lifestyle and tracking.
- Survivors got second forms ("<name> app", "<name> travel", "<name> Disney"): all 0 exact and 0 contains after re-running the 13 queries that had errors.
- Web checks (brand search): Larkfolio is a live fiction-drafting tool (larkfolio.com). Pipfolio is a prop-trading dashboard (pip-folio.com). Foleo has four brands (creator platform, buyer's agents, foleo.me, foleo.app). Dropped. No brand found for the others below.
- RDAP: .com via Verisign, .app via Google. "free" = RDAP 404 (no record), "TAKEN" = a record exists.

## 4. Honest read

- **No folio name beats Magic Explorer, Moseybug, Keepsake Days or Pocket & Pip.** Moseyfolio is Moseybug with a longer, more abstract second half. Foliobug and Merryfolio are the best of this round, but they are an equal fit, not a better one.
- **The portfolio idea is the weak point, not the clearance.** "Folio" at Disney means the resort bill, outside Disney it means a leather padfolio or a book of pages (Etsy and Amazon are full of them). It doesn't hint at waits, crew or a Vault. And "-folio" is a generic suffix: 22 of 151 compounds already exist in the App Store, so your name would sit among Tripfolio, Dayfolio and Journeyfolio.
- If you still want the idea, Foliobug or Merryfolio are the ones I'd register first. Treat them as backups.
- Don't pursue Parkafolio. It is a phonetic copy of ParkFolio.

## 5. Not verified

- USPTO or any trademark database (needed for any pick, especially classes 9, 39, 41 and 42). I did not check whether Parkeon/Flowbird holds a US "PARKFOLIO" registration.
- Google Play directly. Only web-search snippets.
- Registrar availability and premium pricing. RDAP 404 means no record, not that the name is purchasable at standard price.
- Whether Apple would accept near-variants of ParkFolio. The Apple forum evidence is user-reported and dated.
- Whether the iTunes API under-reports. It can miss apps (it did once earlier). Other countries' stores and removed apps are not covered.
- Social handles, Etsy shops and small businesses outside top search results.
