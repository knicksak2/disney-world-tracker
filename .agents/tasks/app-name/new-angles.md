# New-angle name ideas: generation and screening
Read-only investigation. No repo source was read or changed and no .env file was opened. Not legal advice. Scratch scripts and CSVs live in `%TEMP%\naming`, outside the repo.
Method: 388 candidates across angles A-K (279 in wave 1, 109 coined/blend/"X & Y" names in wave 2). All went through the iTunes Search API (US, limit 100). 175 had zero exact App Store matches. The 131 I considered worth keeping got the three second-form queries (`<name> app`, joined, `<name> Disney`), plus DNS for `.com` and `.app`. About 25 web searches covered the most promising names. Nothing in the seven earlier reports is re-suggested.
"No brand found" means not surfaced in the top results. The web tool is not exact-phrase, and "<name> Disney" queries come back mostly as official Disney pages, so web evidence is weak for most names.

## 1. Summary answer
- No name from these angles clearly beats Park Sherpa, Park Gazette or Gate & Main. They fit the app and tie to Disney better.
- Best new options, in order: **Churro Crew**, **Mouseversary**, **Duskwalk**, **Skip Ahead**, **Whole Gang**, **Tack & Tell**, **Huzzah**, **Cork & Co**. Churro Crew has the most personality. Duskwalk matches the Twilight theme.
- **Mouse verdict: don't lead with "Mouse".** The App Store is nearly empty for coinages, but the web shows a dense field of direct competitors. The one worth keeping is **Mouseversary**, probably as a name for the visit-anniversary feature rather than the whole app.
- The App Store API under-reports. It missed an exact `Dusk and Dawn` app (4,032 ratings, found only by web search), so every "0 exact" below is a weak signal.
- Single common words are almost all taken (161 of 279 in wave 1 had an exact match). Survivors are mostly two-word or coined names.

## 2. Per-angle verdicts
- **A Mouse/ears:** very crowded on the web, nearly empty on the App Store. Fits the Disney fan identity but puts you next to direct competitors (see section 3).
- **B Time and light:** best fit for the Twilight theme. Real words are taken (Golden Hour, Afterglow, Lamplight, Daybreak, Sundown, Glimmer, Candlelit). Coinages survive (Duskwalk, Evenglow). Lamplighter has a game and a travel-agent collision.
- **C Fan food icons:** friendly and funny, but mostly map to the food-log feature only. Churro Crew is the exception because "crew" covers the trip crew. Drumstick (Nestle), Pretzel Day (The Office), Popcorn Bucket (13 chars) are weak.
- **D Getting around:** weak fit. Monorail, Trolley, Ferry and Stroller names describe a ride, not a planner. Gangway is taken (a group trip planner). Whistle Stop has 8 App Store contains hits and is a Disney-style restaurant name.
- **E Crew/friends:** the strongest fit with the app's "crew" feature. Single words (Troupe, Gang, Flock, Huddle, Tribe, Posse, Clan, Pack, Entourage, Caravan, Cohort, Famjam, Rally) are all taken. Two-word forms survive.
- **F Florida:** pleasant but generic and weak on Disney tie. Only the two-word names survive (Citrus Grove, Gator Gang). Gator may bring University of Florida associations.
- **G Wonder/joy:** crowded. WonderWaltz and Sunday & Wonder already sit in the Disney planner space. Huzzah and Wonderstruck survive.
- **H Shortcut:** names read as "skip the line", which is Genie/Lightning Lane territory. Skip Ahead survives. Beeline is taken (cycling app Beeline, plus an exact App Store hit).
- **I Collecting/board:** good fit for the Vault only. Cork & Co, Tack & Tell and Swapboard survive.
- **J Storybook/theatre:** already mined in earlier reports. Curtain Up survives with both domains registered. Marching Orders (15 chars) truncates.
- **K Extra angles:** carnival, band, steam-train, kite and weather words are mostly taken or only fit as decoration. Camp Canteen and Rain or Shine are the usable ones.

## 3. Mouse angle: how crowded
Fan businesses surfaced in web search (verified): MouseQueue (mousequeue.com, a Disney World trip planner), Mouse Trip Tools, Mouse Memo (iOS trip organizer), Mouse Life (iOS and Google Play checklist), Mouse Trivia, MousePlanning, Mouseways, Mouseketrips, MousePlanet, Mouse Fan Travel, Me and The Mouse, The Mouse for Less, MouseSavers, Mousecation (a travel agent's site, mousecation.com), the Mousecapades podcast and the Mousetopia podcast.
Coinages that cleared the App Store: Mouseversary, Mousecapade, Mouse Squad, Mouseling (all 0 exact in three forms, `.com` and `.app` show no records for these four).
- **Mousecapade:** also the name of Disney's licensed NES game "Mickey Mousecapade", so a Disney-title association exists.
- **Mouseplan/Mousewise/Mouseventure/Mousecation/Mousetopia/Mouse Crew/Mouse Mapper:** dropped. Direct collisions or registered `.com` plus a live same-niche brand.
- Avoid as Disney marks: Mickey, Minnie, Mouseketeer, Mouse Ears, House of Mouse, Mouseworks.

## 5. Honest top 8 and the comparison
1. **Churro Crew:** the friendliest and most memorable. Weakness: it leads with food.
2. **Mouseversary:** a real fan word, clean on the App Store, both domains show no records. Weakness: it sits in the crowded Mouse lane and I could not rule out Disney use of the word. Better as the visit-anniversary feature name.
3. **Duskwalk:** short, coined, fits the Twilight theme. Weakness: says nothing about trips.
4. **Skip Ahead:** says "save time" plainly. Weakness: generic, and theme-park wait apps use similar phrasing.
5. **Whole Gang:** warm, fits the crew feature. Weakness: hard to own.
6. **Tack & Tell:** both domains show no records. Weakness: needs explaining.
7. **Huzzah:** six characters, joyful. Weakness: both domains registered.
8. **Cork & Co:** fits the Vault. Weakness: narrow.
- **vs Park Sherpa / Park Gazette / Gate & Main:** none of these beats them. Gate & Main still has the best mix of fit, Disney tie and a clean result. Churro Crew is the only one I would put on the same shortlist, for personality rather than clearance.
- Lamplighter is the best meaning (Main Street lamps, the Twilight theme) but has a same-name Play game and a theme-park agent called Lamplight Travel Co.

## 6. Rejected, and why
- **Existing brands in the same lane (verified):** Gangway (group trip planner, beacon89.com), Beeline (App Store exact plus a cycling-route brand), Dusk & Dawn (exact iOS app, 4,032 ratings, plus a skincare brand), Act One (exact app), Kite Day (exact app), MousePlanning/Mouseplan, Mousecation (agent site), Mouseventure and Mousetopia (podcast and registered `.com`), Mouse Mapper, Mouse Crew.
- **Disney marks, characters or terms:** Mickey, Minnie, Mouseketeer, Mouse Ears, House of Mouse, Mouseworks, Boardwalk (Disney resort), Lamplight Lounge (Disney restaurant, near-name to Lamplighter), Tick & Tock (Peter Pan crocodile), Lampwick (Pinocchio), Updraft (Pixar "Up"), Wishes, Marvelous, Ohana, Timekeeper, Passport, Fast Lane, Dole Whip (a Dole mark).
- **Taken single words (exact App Store hits):** Postcard, Showtime, Spotlight, Backstage, Troupe, Gang, Flock, Huddle, Tribe, Posse, Clan, Pack, Entourage, Shortcut, Hustle, Bypass, Delight, Daydream, Reverie, Jubilee, Whimsy, Pinboard, Keepers, Trinket, Showcase, Carousel, Conductor, Kite and about 150 more.
- **Other brand or meme risk:** Drumstick (Nestle), Pretzel Day (The Office), Party of Five (TV show), Lamplighter (game), Early Lark and Lark & Lantern ("Lark" travel apps), Wonderstruck (book/film), Sawgrass (golf and mall brand).

## 7. Could not verify
- **USPTO and state trademark databases:** not searched for any name. Classes 9, 39, 41 and 42 should be checked for the finalists.
- **Google Play directly:** only through web search. It did surface a same-name game for Lamplighter, which shows the gap.
- **Registrar availability:** DNS only. "free?" means no NS or A records, not that the domain can be bought. Neither RDAP nor a registrar was queried.
- **Social handles, Etsy shops, travel agents and podcasts:** only whatever surfaced in the top web results. Names marked "Not individually searched" had no dedicated web query, only the App Store and DNS.
- **The App Store API under-reports:** it missed `Dusk and Dawn` (found by web), so every "0 exact" is a weak signal.

## 8. Recommendation
- Treat this round as a confirmation that Gate & Main, Park Sherpa and Park Gazette remain the strongest names. If you want a fun, non-Park name, choose Churro Crew or Duskwalk and run USPTO and registrar checks on that one name.
- Keep Mouseversary in mind as a feature label (visit anniversaries) rather than the app name.
- Nothing was implemented.
