# Wish and Fantasy name ideas: generation and screening
Read-only investigation. No repo source or .env file was read or changed. Not legal advice. Scratch scripts and CSVs are in `%TEMP%\naming-wf`, outside the repo.
Method: 186 candidates (wish side, fantasy side and neighbours: fancy, fable, storybook, wonder, whimsy, daydream, enchant, legend, myth). All went through the iTunes Search API (US, limit 100): 143 had zero exact matches and 43 were taken. The 99 survivors worth keeping got three more queries (`<name> app`, joined, `<name> Disney`) and DNS for `.com` and `.app`. About 20 web searches, plus RDAP for 28 `.com` names. None of the 8 earlier reports' names is re-suggested.
"No brand found" means not surfaced in top results. Web search is not exact-phrase and is swamped by Disney pages, so it is weak evidence.

## 1. Summary answer
- **Wish is the riskier word, Fantasy the less useful one.** Neither word is ownable by Disney alone, but Disney uses both in this exact space, and the risk shows up in different places for each.
- **Wish.** Disney Wish (cruise ship, Disney's own plandisney pages), *Wish* (2023 Walt Disney Animation film), Wishes (the Magic Kingdom fireworks, rejected earlier), "When You Wish Upon a Star", and the Disney Wish pin series (BoxLunch, shopDisney, Mercari "Wish Star" pin). That last one matters here because your Vault is a pin collection. Disney also runs a "Disney Week of Wishes" with Make-A-Wish, and "Wishmaker®" is a Make-A-Wish mark. Disney's own planner even has a "My Wish List" feature.
- **Fantasy.** Disney Fantasy (cruise ship), Fantasyland, Festival of Fantasy Parade, Fantasmic!, ESPN Fantasy (Disney-owned), and the App Store results for "fantasy" are fantasy-sports and fantasy-RPG apps. A name with "Fantasy" lands in that pile, and in Disney's ship name.
- **Prior art is dense.** Disney travel agents use these words heavily: Wish Upon a Star With Us (100+ agents), Wishes Family Travel, Wishing On A Star Travel, Wishdrawals Travel, Wishful Travel Co, EnchantAway Travel. Two direct app competitors use Wish too: **Wish Upon A Plan** (wishuponaplan.com, Disney and Universal trip planner) and **Wish & Wander** (Disney planner and checklist brand). The App Store is full of gift "wishlist" apps (Wishr, Wishfinity, Wishwave, Wishery, WishOwl, Wishful, Wishnest). The Wish shopping app (ContextLogic) owns the bare word.
- **Results:** of 96 candidates containing "wish", 24 had an exact App Store hit, and 20 of the 50 single-word "Wish___" coinages were taken. Of the 90 fantasy-side candidates, 19 were taken. Most plain words are gone; coinages and two-word names survive.
- **Best of the lot:** Fancylark, Wishful Crew, Dusk Wish, Wishing Penny, Storybook Crew. **None beats Gate & Main, Park Sherpa or Park Gazette.** The cleanest of them (Fancylark) is about level with Churro Crew and Duskwalk, and it says less about the app. The Wish names have the stronger Disney wink and also the risk of looking Disney-official.
- **Apple 5.2 (intellectual property) and looking official:** Apple rejects apps that use another party's trademark or trade dress without permission, and 4.1 (copycats) covers impersonation. A name that reads as a Disney product ("Disney Wish Planner", "Wish Cruise Tracker") is the problem. A generic word with no Disney term is not, but reviewers and Disney's brand team see the name next to the Disney Wish ship, so keep "Disney" and every Disney product name out of the name and the icon. Put "for Walt Disney World" in the subtitle as a descriptive use, add a non-affiliation line, and get a real trademark search before launch.

## 3. Rejected, and why
- **Disney marks or confusingly close:** Wishes (fireworks), Disney Wish, Wish Upon a Pin, Wish Upon a Star and "First Star Wish" (Jiminy Cricket's song), Wishstar (a star character is in the *Wish* film), Fantasia / Fantasia Day, Fantasyland, Fancy Free and Fable and Fancy (*Fun and Fancy Free*), Enchanted Day (*Enchanted*), Once Upon a Park (Disney's *Once Upon a Time*), Fantasy Main, Fantasy Gate, Fantasy Whistle (Fantasyland and Main Street), Wish Granted, Wishgiver (Disney "wish" language).
- **Make-A-Wish adjacent (registered charity marks):** Wishmaker (Wishmaker® is a Make-A-Wish mark and "WishMaker" is how Disney styles it), Wish Maker, Well Wishes. Dropped.
- **Direct competitors in the Disney planner space:** Wish Upon A Plan, Wish & Wander, Whimsy Trip (whimsytrip.com, a Disney World planner), Whimsey, Whimsly, Dream Day Assistant, MyMagicTrip. Wish Walk, Wish Trail and Wishful Trail sit next to the Wish planner brands.
- **Taken in the App Store (exact match):** Wishwell (3), Wishing Well, Wishful (2), Wishly (10), Wishbone (2), Wishbound, Wishcast, Wishlight, Wishkeep, Wishbook, Wishcraft, Wishtide, Wishery, Wishmap, Wishward, Wishling, Wishlet, Wishline, Wishpost, Wishpath, Wishday, Wish List (6), Starwish (2), Wishful Thinking, Fantasy Pass, Fancywalk, Phantasm, Fablewalk, Fabled, Fabulist, Fabula (3), Lorekeep, Storyday, Storywalk, Wonderly (2), Wondertrail, Reverly (2), Fabletale, Fantasma, Story Well (2), Wonderling, Dreamwell (3), Wonder Park.
- **Existing brands found on the web, same word:** Wishnest (Shopify wishlist app), Wishlyst, Wishr, Wishwave, Wishfinity, WishOwl, Wishwell (Google Play bucket-list app), Wish Vault (Google Play), Pennywish (appstor.io listing), Whimsical (0 exact but 11 contains hits; a known diagramming brand), Wishdrawals Travel, Wishing On A Star Travel, Wish Upon a Star With Us, Wishes Family Travel, Wishful Travel Co.
- **Other:** Wishcoin (crypto read), Fantasee (reads as Fanta, Coca-Cola), Fancify and Fancy That (Fancy.com and iFancy are in the same "Fancy" space). Fanciful, Fableborn and Fableling had zero exact hits but one contains hit each and were not pursued (dictionary word or weak fit).

## 4. Honest top 5
1. **Fancylark.** The cleanest result: zero exact in four query forms, `.com` unregistered, no brand found, no Disney term. Lark ties to the 6 AM reminders. Weakness: it says little about trips and needs a tagline.
2. **Wishful Crew.** Fits the crew feature and has an unregistered `.com`. Weakness: near-name Wishful Travel Co (a Disney agent) and the Wish word next to Disney's ship and film.
3. **Dusk Wish.** Short, fits the Twilight theme, `.com` unregistered. Weakness: the Wish proximity and a mood-only fit.
4. **Wishing Penny.** The best Disney fan wink (pressed pennies, wishing at the Cinderella fountain). Weakness: 13 characters truncate and it carries the Wish proximity.
5. **Storybook Crew.** Warm and fits the crew. Weakness: 14 characters and the Storybook Circus echo.
- **Versus the earlier finalists:** none of these beats Gate & Main, Park Sherpa or Park Gazette. The Wish names add Disney-mark exposure that those three avoid, and Fancylark gives up the app-meaning those three carry. Duskwalk is a safer pick than Dusk Wish for the same Twilight idea.
- **If you want to keep the wish idea:** use it as a feature name (a wish list inside the app) and keep the app name Disney-free. I would still avoid "Wish" in the store name.

## 5. Could not verify
- **USPTO and state trademark databases:** not searched. Check classes 9, 39 and 41 for any finalist; "Wish" and "Wishful" are likely to have crowded filings.
- **Google Play directly:** web search only. It surfaced Wishwell, Wish Vault and WishtrailPen, which the App Store API did not, so the API under-reports and every "0 exact" is a weak signal.
- **`.app` domains:** DNS only. Two DNS "free?" names were registered per RDAP, so treat `.app` "free?" as unconfirmed. No registrar was queried.
- **Social handles, Etsy, podcasts, other countries' stores:** only what web search surfaced. The Disney agent field is large and most small agencies would not appear.
- **Disney's own use of "Wish Upon a Pin", "Wishing" and "Fancy" in merchandise:** not checked beyond the results above.
- Nothing was implemented.
