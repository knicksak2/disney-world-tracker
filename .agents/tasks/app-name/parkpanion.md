## 2. Sound, spelling, fit

- **Pronunciation [I]:** most people will say "park-PAN-yun" (stress on PAN, rhymes with companion minus "com"). Some will say "park-PAN-ee-un" and some "park-pan-ion" (like onion). The mix itself is a cost: three readings, none certain.
- **Spelling from hearing [I]:** a listener writes "Park Companion", "Parkcompanion" or "Park Panion". The correct spelling needs the p-a-n-i-o-n jump from "com" to "pan". The blend reads as a typo or a dropped syllable for the first few seconds.
- **Length:** 9 letters. Fine for an icon label (limit about 12).
- **Meaning:** companion matches the Park Pal taste (a friend, not a tool). It is park-agnostic, so a Universal app later needs no rename.
- **Say it aloud:**
  - "We planned the whole trip on Parkpanion." It works, but the listener hears "Park Companion" and may think of a generic category.
  - "Join my Parkpanion." Weak. "Join my Parkpanion crew" is smoother, but the name alone has no noun feel (it is not a thing you can be "in").
- **Compared with the user's own ideas:** Park Pal is two common words, instantly spoken and spelled. Parkpanion adds a typo-feeling and loses the "pal" warmth.

## 4. Conclusions and recommendations
- Parkpanion is clean on the App Store and domains, but I would not pick it: its main risk is its sound and meaning against ParkCompanion: Wait Times (same niche, same feature set, active, 0 ratings so far) and the many apps that call themselves a "park companion".
- Of the 166 blends, the "panion" forms (Ropepanion, Crewpanion, Queuepanion) share Parkpanion's typo problem. The blends that read as one friendly spoken word are Parkgang and Parkchum. Parkgang fits the user's Churro Gang reaction; Parkchum is the warmest short single word.
- If the user wants "companion" meaning without the collision, the crew angle (Parkgang, Rope Crew, Lanyard Crew) is the safer route.
- Next step if one is chosen: web-check the shortlist under its spaced form, check Instagram/TikTok/X handles directly, run a USPTO search, and check a registrar for `.co`/`.io`/handles.
