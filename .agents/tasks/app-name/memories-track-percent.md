# Name clearance: Main Street Memories / Track the Magic / Park Percent

Read-only research, no repo source changed. Not legal advice. "Verified" means I saw it in an API or search result this session. "Inferred" means my judgment. "No brand found" means not surfaced in top results.
Environment dates are Nov 2026. Search content below is paraphrased.

## 1. Summary

| Name | Verdict | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| Main Street Memories | **No-go** | A Disney trip-planning business already uses the exact name, and "Main Street" is Disney's land name (inferred IP risk). |

## 2. Verdict per proposed name

### Main Street Memories (20 chars) - No-go
- **App Store (verified):** 0 exact or contains hits across 4 forms (name, "+ app", joined, "+ Disney"). Same for "MainStreet Memories" and "Memories on Main".
- **Same-space brand (verified):** [mymainstreetmemories.com](https://mymainstreetmemories.com/) is a Disney vacation planner that helps with dining reservations and park days. That is the same customer and the same activity. `mainstreetmemories.com` is also registered (RDAP).
- **Other users (verified):** Theme Park Press book "Main Street Memories: How I Made Dreams Come True at Walt Disney World" ([themeparkpress.com](https://themeparkpress.com/products/main-street-memories-how-i-made-dreams-come-true-at-walt-disney-world-as-the-magic-kingdoms-grand-marshal-coordinator)). An Etsy [Main Street Memories market](https://www.etsy.com/market/main_street_memories) of Disney prints, candles, wax melts, a Dept 56 collectible series and a puzzle. A 2025 documentary ([IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt35101620/)). A Walt Disney Family Museum talk titled "Main Street Memories with Eddie Sotto".
- **Disney IP (inferred):** "Main Street, U.S.A." is Disney's land name, and Disney's own pages use it that way. I could not confirm the USPTO registration. "Main Street Memories" next to a Disney app looks like official merchandise or a Disney program. That invites an Apple 5.2 (intellectual property) objection or a complaint. Generic "main street" alone would be fine, but the Disney context removes that cover.
- **Fit:** the memories half matches Vault, food, visit logs and photos. It says nothing about waits, crowds, reminders or expenses. At 20 chars iOS shows roughly "Main Street Me...".
- **Domains:** `.com` registered (A+NS, RDAP). `.app`, `.co`, `.io` showed no records (DNS) and `.app` is 404 in RDAP.
- **Variants:** "Memories on Main" is the already-rejected "Magic on Main" family. "Magic on Main Street" is a podcast and "Magic on Main Travel" is an agency (verified). Treat it as Caution at best. "MainStreet Memories" has the same collision.

### Track the Magic (15 chars) - No-go
- **App Store (verified):** 0 hits for the exact phrase, "Tracking the Magic", "TrackTheMagic" and "Track Magic". The phrase is clear, but the idea is not.
- **Direct competitor (verified):** "Magic Track: Theme Park Log" by Magic Digital, LLC (App Store, Travel, updated 2026-11-12). Its [site](https://www.magicdigitalapps.com/) describes a log of attractions, shows, characters, meals, resorts and transportation. "Track the Magic" and "Magic Track" differ by two words but sound the same and do the same job. Expect confusion in search and in reviews.
- **More trackers (verified, web):** [MagicalTracker](https://magicaltracker.com/) (Disney dining and snack checklist), MyMagicTracker (WDW trip organizer), [Magical Adventure Log](https://www.magicaladventurelab.com/), [Park Magic Live](https://parkmagiclive.com/) (planner, waits, spending and trip journal; closest to this app's scope), [MagicMapper](https://magicmapper.app/). Apple search shows "ParkMagic for Disney World" too.
- **"Magic" slot (verified/inferred):** Disney itself uses Magic Kingdom, MagicBand and MagicMobile (verified in Disney pages). The word is both crowded and Disney-owned in this category. This is the weakest part of the name.
- **Domains:** `trackthemagic.com` registered. `trackingthemagic.com` showed no records.
- **Fit:** says "tracker" only. Pins, crew, planning, crowds and reminders are not hinted. 15 chars shows as "Track the Ma...".

### Park Percent (12 chars) - Caution
- **App Store (verified):** 0 hits for "Park Percent", "Park Percentage", "ParkPercent" and "Parkpercent" across 4 forms.
- **Web (verified):** no brand found. Top results are percent calculators (Percentio, My Percent, Prozent), parking apps (ParkWhiz, Premium Parking) and ParkProgress (a ski tracker). A user searching the name would land on those.
- **Meaning (inferred):** it could be "crowd percentage", which matches the crowd calendar (the app's own scale is a 1-10 display level over a continuous ratio). But it reads as a stat, not a trip companion. It covers none of the planning, pins, food, expenses or crew features. It has no Disney feel.
- **Memorability:** "percent" next to "park" reads as parking-meter or calculator in speech. Moderate.
- **Domains:** `parkpercent.com` registered (A+NS, RDAP). `.app/.co/.io` showed no records. `parkpercentage.com` is available (RDAP 404).
- **Fit:** 12 chars sits at the iOS truncation edge. Fine, but not a name a fan would repeat.

## 5. Honest top 5

1. **Park Gazette:** friendly, easy to say, 12 chars, nothing found anywhere, `.com` and `.app` free. Gives a newspaper feel that suits "what is happening in my park day".
2. **Park Almanac:** best fit for crowd calendar plus history and planning. `.com` is taken, so use `.app`, or "getparkalmanac".
3. **Park Daybook (earlier clean):** same family as Gazette and Almanac. Pick one of the three, not several.
4. **Park Keepsakes:** best for the pins, memories and Vault side. Weaker on planning.
5. **Park Stubs:** shortest and cutest, fits the label, and a fan understands "ticket stub" at once. Risk is the parking-stub reading.

If a strong Disney tie matters most, Gate & Main (earlier) stays ahead of anything here. None of the names built on Main Street or Magic clear.
