## 1. Summary answer

- **Plain "Clubhouse" is blocked.** The exact title `Clubhouse` is Alpha Exploration Co., 792,147 ratings, updated 2026-11-19. The API now lists its primary genre as **News**, not Social Networking [V]. The same search has 4 more exact titles in Sports, Lifestyle and Health (Clubhouse - Racket Sports, Clubhouse: Golf with Friends, Club House, Clubhouse - Golf Stats GPS). "The Clubhouse" has 3 more exact titles. F3 fails.
- **Disney echo (stated once):** `Mickey Mouse Clubhouse` is a Disney Junior series (2006-2016), rebooted as `Mickey Mouse Clubhouse+` on Disney Jr. and Disney+ (premiered 2025-07-21), with "Clubhouse Day" as an episode party [V]. A name ending in "Clubhouse" reads as that title to Disney fans. The user counts a Disney feeling as a plus, so this is a risk to know, not a block. No Disney-owned app with Clubhouse in the title surfaced on the App Store API (0 Disney-seller hits in 4 searches). "Clubhouse" is also the Disney Vacation Club fan podcast `D-Brief by DVC Clubhouse` [V] and the name of a mental-health day-program model [V, minor].
- **Clubhouse compounds are workable but not the best route.** 73 `<flavor> Clubhouse` / `Clubhouse <word>` names were screened: only 1 had an exact hit (Pocket Clubhouse, a Sports app), 0 had 2+ contains, and the rest are clean on the App Store. Crew Clubhouse, Rope Clubhouse, Lanyard Clubhouse and Churro Clubhouse have both `.com` and `.app` unregistered. Their costs: 14-17 characters (the home-screen label truncates near 11-12), and the Clubhouse and Mickey echoes. **Merry Clubhouse** and **Clubhouse Days** are worse: "Merry Clubhouse Songs" is a Mickey Mouse Clubhouse book and "Clubhouse Day" is a show episode [V].
- **Better: keep the clubhouse feeling and drop the word.** The same warm hangout idea works with Lodge, Porch, Nook, Stoop, or with Troop (a crew synonym). These had no App Store or web conflict and free `.com`/`.app`. Best five: **Churro Troop, Lanyard Lodge, Rope Lodge, Churro Porch, Crew Clubhouse** (the best of the Clubhouse compounds).
- **Counts:** 482 unique names generated (a: 73, b: 292, c: 86, d: 24 after dedupe, plus 7 already-seen). 475 screened on the iTunes API (US, top 100). 24 had an exact hit, 3 had 2+ contains, **448 survivors**. 126 shortlisted names got `<name> app`, joined and `<name> Disney` queries (0 new exact hits except Crew Hub, which was already exact; "Pin Den" joined has 1 contains: Pindena). 47 names were web-checked one at a time. 126 RDAP pairs (.com and .app).
- **Caution on Rope + a social room.** Searches for "Rope Hangout", "Rope Lounge" and "Rope den" surfaced shibari/rope-bondage communities and events using exactly those names [V]. I do not recommend Rope Hangout, Rope Lounge or Rope Den. The other Rope place names (Lodge, Porch, Hideout, Stoop, Hut) showed no such hits, but "rope" plus a social-room word can attract that audience in search, so check social handles directly.
