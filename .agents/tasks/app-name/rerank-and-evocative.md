Read-only. No repo source or .env file touched. Scratch scripts and CSVs are in `%TEMP%\naming-rr`. Not legal advice. Dates are the Nov 2026 window of the tool results.
"No brand found" means "not surfaced in the top results". The web search tool is not exact-phrase and is dominated by Disney pages, so it is weak evidence. Scores 1-5 (5 best) for Sound (S), Fit (F), Feeling (W) and Clearance (C) are my judgment; C comes from recorded evidence (5 = no hit in four App Store query forms, no web brand, both domains unregistered; 3 = adjacent brand or taken `.com`; 2 = same-niche brand with the same lead word; 1 = F1/F3 block). Totals are S+F+W+C.

## 1. Summary answer

- **No block on Magic Explorer.** Four App Store query forms returned zero exact matches, no same-name brand surfaced, `magicexplorer.app` is unregistered, and `magicexplorer.com` is registered (Afternic resale). Its cost is crowding ("Magic Navigator" and "Magic Atlas" are live Disney planner brands, new finding), a 14-character label, and the adult "Magical Explorer" anime/game next door.
- **Do new names beat it?** On the numbers, yes for a few: **Keepsake Days** (17), **Penny & Pin** (16), **Merrywander** (15) beat Magic Explorer (14) on clearance and distinctiveness. On instant Disney recognition, no new name matches the word "Magic". The best fresh-feeling name overall is **Wishing Penny** from the earlier list (17). It is a taste call, so section 5 gives both sides.
- **Pressed pennies are the richest unused vein.** It is a real park ritual, it ties the Vault (collecting) to expenses, and "Penny" names survived every filter: Wishing Penny, Penny & Pin, Pins & Pennies, Pressed Penny.
- Of 378 new candidates, 98 had an exact App Store hit (F3) and 280 had none. 176 new names plus 26 re-checked old names got three more query forms (`<name> app`, joined, `<name> Disney`) and RDAP: only one additional exact hit appeared (Best Day Ever). The API still under-reports (it showed zero for Magic Navigator, which exists on the web).

## 2. Taste profile (from the user's reactions)

Positive: Park Pal, Headliner, Front Row, E ticket, Magic Explorer, Magic & Main, Queue the Magic, Park Pixie, wish/fantasy words, mouse.
- **Warm companion personas**: Pal, Pixie, Explorer. The name is a friend, not a tool.
- **Disney-insider vocabulary**: E ticket, Headliner, Main, Magic, Queue the Magic. Fan wink with a smile.
- **Short and easy to say**, usually one or two words with a nostalgic flavour.
- **Not wanted**: abstract utility words and neutral nouns. Sherpa, Gazette, Almanac, Daybook, Percent, Tally.
- **What the earlier finalists lacked**: a persona or a heart word. They were clean but read like software. The earlier reports also scored Disney ties as a risk; that is now reversed. The names that should rise are the ones that add feeling (penny, wishing, keepsake, merry, lantern) without needing a Disney mark.

## 7. What I could not verify

- **USPTO and state trademarks**: not searched. Disney mark status for "Mouseversary", "Starlit", "Penny" phrases and "Magic Explorer" is unchecked. Classes 9, 41, 42 and 39 need a real search.
- **Google Play directly**: only via web search. The App Store API and web both under-report; the API missed Magic Navigator.
- **Registrar status**: RDAP 404 means unregistered today; premium or reserved pricing is unchecked. `.app` RDAP hit a 429 rate limit for 42 names; I re-ran those at a slower pace and all returned results.
- **Web depth**: 20 targeted searches (the finalists), noisy and Disney-dominated. 9 of the 30 survivors had no dedicated web search ("not searched"). Instagram, TikTok, Etsy and podcasts are poorly covered.
- **Scores** are judgments. The Clearance column is the only one tied to recorded evidence.
