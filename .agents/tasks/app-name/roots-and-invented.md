"No brand found" means "not surfaced in top results". The web search tool is not exact-phrase and `OR` queries do not work, so web checks are weak evidence. Scores are 1-5 (5 best): Sound (S), Spelling (Sp), Warmth (W), Fit (F, feels like a park-day companion), Clearance (C). Only C is tied to recorded evidence. Total = S+Sp+W+F+C (max 25; note earlier reports used four scores, so subtract Sp to compare: Moseybug is 17 on that scale).
Step 0: all 11 earlier reports were read (I read `rerank-and-evocative.md` and `vague-brandable.md` in full and extracted every word from the other nine into a 2,794-word skip list). Every candidate below is absent from those reports.

## 1. Summary answer

- **The best answer to "a word we can add things to" is a two-part name: a friendly stem plus a small suffix.** Two suffix systems survived clearance. The **diminutive/companion** system (`-bug`, `-ling`, `-kin`, `-bean`, `-bird`) gives names that feel like a little friend, like Park Pal. The **place-name** system (`-vale`, `-wick`, `-ford`, `-stead`, `-dale`) gives a nostalgic storybook-town feel. Adding things later works either way: "Moseybug Crew", "Moseybug Vault", "Moseybug for Universal".
- **Top pick: Moseybug.** It is "mosey" (stroll slowly) plus "bug", which echoes the fan idiom of catching "the Disney bug" (my knowledge of the idiom, not checked here). Zero App Store matches in four query forms, `.com` and `.app` unregistered on 2026 RDAP, no brand found. It ties Keepsake Days and Pocket & Pip on clearance and beats Magic Explorer on distinctiveness. It does not beat them on proven fit, and **"bug" invites "has bugs" jokes in reviews**.
- **Pure from-scratch coinages (Part 2) mostly failed on taste, not clearance.** 479 of the 480 names screened on the App Store were clean, but the random ones are mostly gibberish (for example "fovop", "wuhul", "niwaloo"). The ones that sound good were almost always `.com`-registered: 70% of all 2,791 domain-checked names had a registered `.com`.
- **Verdict on the earlier five (Merrywander, Keepsake Days, Pocket & Pip, Glee & Go, Magic Explorer):** nothing here clearly beats them. Moseybug, Strollbird, Merryling and Tillykin are credible equals with a stronger "friend" persona than Merrywander and Keepsake Days. Section 4 explains.

## 4. Honest top 10

1. **Moseybug.** Companion persona plus the Disney-fan "bug" wink, no Disney word. Cost: "bug" can be mocked in reviews.
2. **Strollbird.** Highest score, friendly and easy to spell. Cost: 10 letters, no fan wink, not individually web-searched.
3. **Merryling.** Warmest of the set, cute "little merry thing". Cost: Christmas read.
4. **Larkbug.** Playful and easy to say. Cost: Lark travel brands, "bug".
5. **Tillykin.** Sweet pet-name feel. Cost: twee, close to the earlier Tilly & Tuck.
6. **Pipvale.** Storybook town. Cost: reads as a place, not a friend.
7. **Moseyling.** Same mosey idea without "bug". Cost: weaker identity.
8. **Gleebug.** Best sound (5). Cost: GleeBug debugger and Glow Bug apps, "bug".
9. **Tuckwick.** Ties to "tuck away" keepsakes. Cost: Twickenham and Tauck echoes.
10. **Moseyvale.** Gentle town feel. Cost: needs a persona word.

**Do any beat Merrywander, Keepsake Days, Pocket & Pip, Glee & Go, Magic Explorer?**
- **No clear win.** On the four-score scale used before, Moseybug scores 17 (S4+F4+W4+C5), the same as Keepsake Days and Pocket & Pip and above Merrywander (15) and Magic Explorer (14). That is a tie, not a win: my scores are judgments, and Moseybug has weak web evidence (one search).
- **Where they are better:** Moseybug and Merryling have a "friend" persona like Park Pal, which Merrywander and Keepsake Days lack. Both domains are free, like Merrywander and Pocket & Pip.
- **Where they are worse:** no proof yet for fit (Pocket & Pip and Keepsake Days name a feature), "bug" has the review-joke risk, and none says Disney as fast as "Magic" in Magic Explorer.
- **My read:** if you want a friend-name, test Moseybug beside Pocket & Pip. If you want the safest, keep Keepsake Days.

## 7. What I could not verify

- **USPTO and state trademarks**: not searched (classes 9, 41, 42, 39). "Moseybug", "Tillykin" and "Merryling" need a real search.
- **Google Play direct**: only via web search. "Glow Bug" and "Glowbug" Play apps exist near Gleebug.
- **Registrar status**: RDAP 404 means unregistered today, not that the price is standard. The `.app` RDAP rate-limited Nubako (429); I dropped it.

- **Languages**: checked from my own knowledge, not by a native speaker.
