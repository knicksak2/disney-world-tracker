# Name check: "Park Pixie" and Park + whimsical-word alternatives

Not legal advice. Searches ran in Nov 2026. "No brand found" means "not surfaced in top results", not proof of clearance.

## 1. Verdict: NO-GO on "Park Pixie"

1. **An established Disney World planning brand already uses this exact name.** "The Park Pixie" (theparkpixie.com, run by Meg Frost) publishes Disney World trip-planning guides, packing lists and monthly guides. It has Pinterest, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts under `the.park.pixie` / `theparkpixie`. Verified: web search for "ParkPixie" returned it as the first ten results. Your app does the same job for the same audience, so the overlap is direct and likely to cause confusion.
2. **The Pixie + Disney travel space is crowded.**
   - Pixie Parks Travel Co. (pixieparkstravel.com) is a Disney travel agency. Several of its agents run "Pixie Parks ..." social accounts.
   - Pixie Vacations (pixievacations.com) is an Authorized Disney Vacation Planner.
   - PixieWire: Dining Alerts (iOS, Travel, PixieWire Media LLC, released 2026-09) watches Disney World and Disneyland reservations.
   - Pixie Dust Planners (iOS, Travel, Ken Wallquist) sells Disney itinerary plans.
   - ParkSidePixie is a Disney World TikTok account.
   - All verified. Together they mean "Park Pixie" would look like one more of many near-identical names.
3. **App Store exact match found for the reversed form.** "Pixie Park" (iOS, Entertainment, San Francisco International School, a children's playground in Ross, CA) is an exact match. "Park Pixie" and "ParkPixie" returned no exact App Store match. Verified with 7 queries plus app-suffixed forms. The reversed-name app and the blog still make store search noisy.
4. **Disney association is strong.** Pixie is tied to Tinker Bell, Pixie Hollow, the Disney Fairies franchise and "pixie dust". Disney is known for enforcing Tinker Bell marks. A federal appeals court ruled in Disney's favor on "Teen Tink"/"Teen Tinker Bell" (Fed. Cir. 21-1056). Inferred, not verified for "Pixie" alone: "Pixie" is a common word and not a Disney mark by itself, so a claim is less likely than for "Tinker". It still raises the risk that fans, Disney or App Review read the app as affiliated. Apple's guideline 5.2 covers third-party IP and misleading affiliation, and "Park Pixie" in a Disney-planner category invites that question. I could not find a documented rejection for this name.
5. **Tone.** "Pixie" reads as a fairy or kids' product. Your features (crowd calendar, 6 AM booking reminders, expenses, crew trip planning, food logging) are adult/family planning tools. Inferred. The word also fits the Disney-fan-blog style rather than a utility.

Domains (DNS only, which suggests registration, not proof): parkpixie.com is parked on Afternic (for sale at a markup, likely). parkpixie.app resolves on Cloudflare (registered). pixiepark.com is registered. parkpixie.co, parkpixie.io and pixiepark.app have no A or NS records, so they may be free. theparkpixie.com is the blog's own domain.

## 4. What I could not verify

- **USPTO / trademark databases:** not searched. Park Wren and any pick needs a TESS/Trademark Search check (classes 9, 42, 41, 39) before commitment.
- **Google Play:** only via web search, not the Play Store directly. Web search is not exact-phrase and surfaces generic hits.
- **Registrar availability:** DNS with no A/NS records only suggests a domain is unregistered. It can still be reserved or premium. Check at a registrar. `.app` records for "free?" names were not verified for premium pricing.
- **iTunes Search API under-reports:** it returned at most 100 results per query and may miss exact matches (it did once before). Other countries' stores, unreleased apps and apps removed from the store would not appear.
- **Social handles, Instagram, TikTok, and trademarks pending:** not checked for the alternatives.
- **Apple review outcome:** no one can predict the 5.2 decision. My Disney-association risk read for "Park Pixie" is an inference.

## Recommendation
Drop "Park Pixie". If you want a Disney wink without the trademark risk, prefer Park Wren, Park Bobbin or Park Quill, and run a USPTO search and a registrar check on your top two before building brand assets. No repo files were modified.
