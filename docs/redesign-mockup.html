✨ The Winning Architecture: Magic FAB + Real-Time Wait Utility

Following in-depth UX evaluation, we have permanently committed to The Magic FAB Architecture (4 Content Tabs + Center In-Park Action FAB) and fully incorporated the Real-Time Live Wait Times & Queue Radar.

🪄 Why the FAB Model Wins
  • Lightning In-Park Speed: 1 thumb tap on the center gold FAB opens the fast logging sheet from any screen in the app.
  • Clean, Purposeful Tabs: The bottom bar stays 100% dedicated to prime destinations: Today, Explore, Trips, and Vault.
  • No Profile Clutter: Profile & Crew are elevated to the prominent Header Avatar Chip (the Apple Maps, Airbnb, Duolingo pattern).
  • Stable Muscle Memory: Zero dynamic bar shifting; buttons never move or disappear while walking through the park.
⏱️ Why Incorporating Live Waits is Essential
  • #1 In-Park Utility: Checking wait times is what guests do dozens of times per day. Putting it 1 tap away is a massive upgrade.
  • Fixes Kiro's Critical Flaw: Kiro made Waits a morphing bottom tab that only appeared in-park, broke muscle memory, and banished Explore to a hidden sheet.
  • Multi-Path Accessibility: Access Live Waits from Home Action Dock, Home Pulse pills, Explore banner, or the Center FAB.
  • Actionable Data: Filter by Walk-ons or Lightning Lane, and log rides directly from the wait list.
