What's the same in both

  • Info tags on every row + detail: category, land/area (new), dining price tier, and accessibility — rendered only when the data is present.
  • Collapsible land sections so a big park isn't one endless run.
  • Global search always available for the "I know what I want" case.
  • Detail page gains coordinates, accessibility, and land alongside the park it already shows.

The trade

  • Option A — calmer entry, each park is focused, best for "find a specific thing." Costs an extra tap and more screens.
  • Option B — no navigating in/out, quick park switching, but a big park still scrolls and there's no clean overview.
