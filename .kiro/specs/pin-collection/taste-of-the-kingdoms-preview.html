Art Highlights:
  • Instant Silhouette: Unmistakable room-service / fine-dining cloche recognizable from across the room.

Candidate 3 • Symbolic Cutlery
The Sovereign Tasting Fork
Ornate 4-Prong Gold Fork • 4 Park Cloisonné Insets • Heraldic Shield
Gold ✂️ Die-Cut Silhouette
The Sovereign Tasting Fork: A heraldic cutlery crest where the fork itself is the storytelling hero. Its 4 golden tines feature cloisonné channel insets for each park's heraldic color, superimposed over a four-quadrant royal dining shield.
Art Highlights:
  • Literal 4-Prong Concept: Clever metaphor representing 4 parks = 4 fork tines.
  • Regal Crest Styling: Combines heraldry with dining silverware; feels like a royal order or knighthood pin.
  • Strong Contrasts: Sparkling white knife blade contrasts against deep crimson & teal shield panels.
