Dark Ride Master — 5 Pin Candidates

Comparing your selected Game Icons galleon adaptations alongside the refined Living Storybook (pure tome, zero tracks, slimmed border). Inspect all 5 side-by-side at scale.

Adaptation 1 • Cloud Soarer
The Cloud Soarer
Pitched 12° Ascent • Billowing London Cloud Bank
Gold ✂️ Soaring Die-Cut
The Cloud Soarer: Rotates the galleon 12° upward into an ascending climb and seats its keel in a sculpted, billowing cloud bank (evoking the flight over London clouds) poured in porcelain cream and night plum enamel with stardust.
Why It Solves the Flat Look:
  • Ascending Pitch: Bow points dynamically up into the sky, immediately reading as flight rather than sailing.
  • Billowing Clouds: Puffy cloud lobes replace the thin flat wake, providing unmistakable aerial context.
  • Solid Pin Base: Broad cloud bank keeps the pin stable and snag-free.
Adaptation 2 • Cloisonné Sails
Full-Rigged Cloisonné
Game Icons Hull & Masts + Cream Enamel Sails
Gold ✂️ Fabricated Die-Cut
The Baseline Ship: Preserves the exact Game Icons hull and mast geometry with porcelain cream enamel sails poured into the yardarms in a classic upright nautical orientation.
Key Points:
  • Symmetrical Sails: High enamel ratio (62%) with genuine cloisonné depth.
  • Neutral Orientation: Flat water-line orientation without clouds or celestial elements.
Adaptation 3 • Moonlit Flight
The Moonlit Flight
Pitched 12° Ascent • Golden Crescent Moon Cradle
Gold ✂️ Celestial Die-Cut
The Moonlit Flight: The pitched galleon soars through the curve of a bold golden crescent moon. Directly references the iconic Disney ride moment of the pirate ship flying past the moon with trailing stardust.
Why It Conveys Flight:
  • Iconic Celestial Pairing: Galleon + crescent moon is universally synonymous with Peter Pan's Flight.
  • Dynamic Ascent: 12° upward pitch makes the ship look weightless and soaring.
  • Balanced Metalwork: Polished crescent moon provides structural integrity to the pin frame.
Adaptation 4 • Contained Disc
Flight Over London Medallion
Climbing Galleon • Big Ben Silhouette • Midnight Sky
Gold 🛡️ Contained (DISC)
Flight Over London: Places the galleon climbing at an 8° flight angle over the miniature gold silhouette of Big Ben and London rooftops against a deep midnight sky. Direct homage to the climax of the ride.
Key Highlights:
  • Unmistakable Narrative: Big Ben spire immediately establishes that the galleon is airborne over the city.
  • Original Badge Shape: Fulfills the Contained (DISC) catalog badge.
  • High Depth: Layered London cityscape + glowing celestial sky.
Candidate 5 • Storybook Tome
The Living Storybook
Pure Illuminated Tome • Gilded Edges • ZERO Tracks
Gold ✂️ Pure Die-Cut Tome
The Storybook Origin: Embodies the quintessential "storybook dark ride" history. Features an open illuminated tome with gilded page edges, crimson Drop-Cap 'D', and an illuminated night castle vignette.
Key Highlights:
  • Category Representation: Covers all dark rides (Pooh, Peter Pan, Snow White, Little Mermaid) as living stories.
  • Zero Tracks / Gimmicks: Pure, elegant jewelry aesthetic without any protruding elements.
  • Trimmed Border: Tight gilded rim (§1) without any heavy backplate slabs.
