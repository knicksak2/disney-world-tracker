Honoring all 8 Deluxe Resorts with authentic resort hospitality symbolism.
Each design incorporates 8 gold stars on a contained luxury plate (Pearl Tier specification).

Side-by-side product visual renders of the 4 flagship candidates: polished Pearl-White silver metal rims, deep Royal Palette cloisonné enamel, and 8 gold stars.
Milestone Scope: All 8 Deluxe Resorts Pearl Tier
Properties: Grand Floridian, Contemporary, Polynesian, Wilderness Lodge, Animal Kingdom Lodge, BoardWalk, Yacht Club, Beach Club

Why Contained Architectural & Heraldic Art Works

Because the 8 Deluxe resorts have opposite architectural themes (Victorian vs 70s A-frame vs Polynesian tiki vs rustic timber), no single prop represents them all. A grand courtyard fountain, crossed concierge keys, or an official resort estate crest with 8 stars honors all 8 properties as an elite collection.

Pearl Tier Art Direction

• Contained Plate Mode: Dilated Pearl metal rim (`shape: 'arch'` or `'crest'`).
• Deep Enamel Fill: 60%+ rich Royal Palette enamel wells (Crimson, Twilight Navy, Azure, Amber Gold).
• 8 Stars: Distinct, embossed 5-point stars for each of the 8 Deluxe Resorts.
• Zero Crown/Monorail: Clean hospitality imagery without generic crowns or monorail silhouettes.

The Courtyard Fountain
Proposed: Deluxe Haven / Grand Courtyard
Multi-Tier Water Fountain & 8 Stars
Evoking the peaceful elegance of Disney's luxury resort courtyards (Grand Floridian, Yacht & Beach Club, BoardWalk). An arched window framing an ornate multi-tiered stone fountain with cascading azure water, beneath an arc of 8 brilliant gold stars.

Candidate 2 • Les Clefs d'Or
The Golden Keys
Proposed: Les Clefs d'Or / The Grand Keys
Crossed Antique Keys & 8 Stars
Inspired by Les Clefs d'Or (the international hallmark of 5-star hotel concierges) and the "Keys to the Kingdom". An ornate midnight navy shield featuring two crossed antique Victorian skeleton keys tied with a crimson ribbon, crowned by an arc of 8 gold stars.
Shape: Crest
Enamel: 64%
Stars: 8
Candidate 3 • Grand Laurel

Proposed: Grand Deluxe Seal / Hotelier Crest

The timeless seal of hospitality mastery. A deep Grand Floridian crimson shield framed by a classical golden laurel wreath encircling a four-column resort portico, crowned with 8 gleaming gold stars along the upper rim.

Stars: 8
