How to read the number. Filled-area overlap after normalising both shapes into the same box — invariant to scale and position. Above 0.99 on a complex shape means the same artwork. On a simple shape it means nothing: any two rounded blocks score 0.94+, which is how an earlier pass of mine confidently matched a book to an oven and a suitcase to a garage. Each card says which case it is.

This search fixes three bugs that produced false negatives earlier: it searches by subject name rather than guessing filenames from our key, it always tries the union of a source icon's paths (a two-path coaster could never match one combined motif — that read 0.246 against art it matches at 0.998), and it applies no vertex-count prefilter.

For the simple ones there is no measurement that can ever settle this. Either you recall the source — that has worked 3 times out of 4, and verify/trace.js <key> <terms> verifies a guess in seconds — or the honest move is to replace the motif with a game-icons equivalent and stop paying for the uncertainty.
