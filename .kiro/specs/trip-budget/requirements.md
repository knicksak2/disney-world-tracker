# Requirements Document

## Introduction

Trip Budget gives a Trip a forward-looking plan for what the visit will cost: park tickets, Lightning Lane, restaurants, and anything else a group wants to price out, so the group can see how much money to save and can decide which add-ons are worth it. It sits next to the existing Trip Expenses ledger (`.kiro/specs/trip-expenses`) in the Trip hub but is a **separate data model**: a Budget is future and estimated, has no payer, no split and no settlement; an Expense is past and exact, with a payer, a split and a settlement. The two are presented together (a Budget tile beside the Expenses tile, an optional soft link from a Budget_Line to an Expense, and a budget-versus-actual comparison by shared category) and are never merged into one table.

The Budget is stored at its **finest grain**: Budget_Lines, each with per-person Budget_Allocations and an optional day and park. Every view (whole party, per person, per day, per park, per category) and the "save $X per week" figure is computed on read by a pure module from that single grain. Money is integer USD cents everywhere; rounding for display is a presentation concern and is never fed back into a calculation.

The product rule for prices is that **the user does not look prices up or maintain a price table**. Every Budget_Line carries a Price_Source and an As_Of date:

- **Park tickets** come from Disney's own public ticket price calendar, cached in Postgres (`disney_ticket_calendar`).
- **Restaurant meals** come from the already-cached Disney dining menus (`disney_menu`), where the user picks menu items or the prix fixe or buffet price.
- **Lightning Lane** comes from ThemeParks.wiki schedule purchases persisted by the sampling job (`themeparks_ll`).
- **Estimates** (`typical_estimate`) are the labelled exception, used only when no real price exists for that date; they always show the text "Estimate" and an as-of date.
- **User-entered prices** (`user_entered`, shown as "Your price") are always allowed and are never overwritten by a refresh.

Ticket prices are the one source with a policy risk. The project owner has explicitly decided to automate them from Disney's price calendar and has accepted the Terms-of-Use exposure (decision recorded 2026-10-25; the owner's message is quoted verbatim in Requirement 21.8, and the owner's written confirmation of the Terms-of-Use wording is a launch gate). The feature therefore ships behind a kill switch that defaults to off in production, refreshes **only on demand** (no cron, no worker, no scheduled job), and stops at the first sign of an edge block.

The feature also adds a **Menu Item Picker** that is shared with Trip Expenses (that spec's new Requirement 16): because the app already knows menu items and their (pre-tax) prices, an itemized Expense or a restaurant Budget_Line can be filled by choosing dishes instead of typing them.

This feature **depends on** `trips` (membership, roles, the permission matrix, the Trip hub, `trips.day_touring_hours`, `planned_items`), `trip-expenses` (the person key, allocation primitives, categories to map, the new picker requirement), `restaurant-menu-display` and `disney-facilities-catalog-source` (menu cache and projection, amended additively), `crowd-calendar` (owns migration `0020` and `samplingService`, amended additively), `disney-source-resilience` (Disney transport, WAF classification, amended additively), `account-deletion`, `admin-panel` and `legal-disclaimers-and-terms` (each amended additively). See the design's Cross-Spec Dependencies and Build Order.

## Glossary

- **Trip**, **Trip_Member**, **Trip_Role**, **Trip_Guest**, **Sponsoring_Member**: as defined by `trips` and `trip-expenses`.
- **Budget**: the set of Budget_Lines, Traveler_Profiles and Budget_Settings of one Trip. Nothing about it is "owed" or "paid".
- **Budget_Line**: one planned cost item: a category, a description, an optional day and park, a Price_Source, and one or more Budget_Allocations.
- **Budget_Category**: one of `tickets`, `lightning_lane`, `dining`, `lodging`, `transportation`, `shopping`, `other` (closed vocabulary, distinct from the Expense category vocabulary).
- **Budget_Allocation**: the pre-tax amount of one Budget_Line attributed to one Person_Key.
- **Person_Key**: exactly one of a Trip_Member id or a Trip_Guest id (the member-XOR-guest pattern of `trip-expenses`).
- **Traveler_Profile**: per-Trip attributes of one Person_Key: Age_Bracket, planned ticket product and length, Discount_Group, and Pass_Type.
- **Age_Bracket**: `adult` (ages 10 and over), `child` (ages 3 to 9), or `under3` (needs no ticket).
- **Ticket_Option**: `base`, `park_hopper`, `park_hopper_plus`, or `water_parks_sports`.
- **Discount_Group**: `std-gst` (all guests) or `fl-resident` (Florida resident).
- **Pass_Type**: `none`, `annual_pass`, `dvc`, or `other_discount`. Prices for any value other than `none` are user-entered.
- **Price_Source**: one of `disney_ticket_calendar`, `disney_menu`, `themeparks_ll`, `typical_estimate`, `user_entered`.
- **As_Of**: the instant a price was last obtained from its source (for a user-entered price, the instant the user saved it).
- **Price_Label_Kind**: one of `current`, `may_have_changed`, `estimate`, `add_your_price`, `your_price`; the machine form of the visible label text.
- **Ticket_Price_Cache**: the Postgres tables `ticket_prices`, `ticket_price_sets` and `ticket_price_control`.
- **Ticket_Client**: the dedicated HTTP client for Disney's token endpoint (T) and price calendar endpoint (P).
- **Ticket_Refresh_Service**: the in-process, single-flight, demand-driven service that runs a refresh through the Ticket_Client.
- **Horizon_Date**: `maxPricingCalendarDate` returned by endpoint P, the last date for which Disney publishes prices.
- **WDW_Today**: the current calendar date in `America/New_York`, from `wdwToday` in `services/trips/wdwClock.ts`.
- **LL_Price_Store**: the persisted Lightning Lane prices (columns on `park_schedule_signals` plus `lightning_lane_single_prices`).
- **Typical_LL_Price**: a labelled median of historical Lightning Lane prices for the same park and weekday, used beyond the real-data window.
- **Menu_Price_Parser**: the shared pure functions in `@dwt/shared` that turn menu price text into integer cents with labels.
- **Savings_Plan**: the "save $X per week until the trip starts" figure derived from the Budget total, the amount saved, and WDW_Today.
- **Suggestion**: a proposed Budget_Line derived from `planned_items`, `trips.day_touring_hours` and Traveler_Profiles that the user may accept or dismiss; never inserted silently.
- **Budget_Settings**: per-Trip tax rates (basis points per category), the dining tip rate, the saved amount and the optional savings target.
- **Kill_Switch**: the `TICKET_PRICES_ENABLED` environment flag.
- **Block_Now**: the admin action that sets `ticket_price_control.blocked_until` immediately (the runtime counterpart of the Kill_Switch, which needs a redeploy to change).
- **Ticket_Error_Code**: one of the closed set of short strings stored in `last_error` (`invalid_response`, `not_offered`, `start_date_rejected`, `row_count_drop`, `empty`, `invalid_price`, `unrecognized_fraction`, `network`, `timeout`, `waf_block`, `manual`, `consecutive_failures`, `horizon_changed`, or `http_status:<3 digits>`); upstream text is never stored.
- **Trip_Service**: the API-side service that serves a Trip's Budget, Traveler_Profiles and price quotes (`apps/api/src/services/tripBudget/` and its routes); as defined by `trips` for the surrounding Trip routes.
- **Mobile_App**: the React Native and Expo client in `apps/mobile`.
- **Admin_Panel**: the existing Basic-Auth operator HTML panel, as defined by `admin-panel`.
- **Trip_Detail_View**: the Trip hub screen (`TripDetailScreen.tsx`), as defined by `trips`.
- **Trip_Budget_Section**: the Budget tile (`HUB_SECTION_DEFS` entry `budget`) on the Trip_Detail_View.
- **Budget_Screen**: `TripBudgetScreen.tsx`, the screen the Trip_Budget_Section opens.
- **Balances_View** and **Expense_Ledger_Screen**: the Expense balances and ledger screens, as defined by `trip-expenses`.
- **Menu Item Picker**: the shared mobile component `apps/mobile/src/components/MenuItemPicker.tsx` that turns chosen menu items or a prix fixe tier into priced drafts; used by the Budget line form and the Expense form (`trip-expenses` Requirement 16).
- **Sampling_Job**: the existing wait-time sampling pass (`samplingService.ts`, run through `POST /internal/sampling/run`), as defined by `crowd-calendar`.
- **Typical estimate / Estimate**: a price that is not a stored real price; always labelled with the text "Estimate".

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Budget as a separate section presented with Expenses

**User Story:** As a Trip_Member, I want a Budget next to Expenses in the Trip hub, so that I can plan what the trip will cost and later compare it to what we actually spent, without the two ledgers getting mixed up.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Trip_Detail_View SHALL present a Trip_Budget_Section tile alongside the existing Expenses tile, which opens the Budget_Screen for that Trip.
2. THE Trip_Service SHALL store Budget data in tables separate from `expenses`, `expense_shares` and `settlements`, and SHALL NOT write a Budget_Line into, or read a Budget_Line from, any Expense table.
3. THE Budget SHALL carry no Payer, no Split_Mode, no Settlement and no Net_Balance; it SHALL NOT appear in the Balances_View, the Expense_Ledger_Screen, the Trip Feed or the Trip Summary.
4. THE Trip_Service MAY record an optional soft link from a Budget_Line to an Expense of the same Trip (Requirement 17), and a Budget_Line SHALL be valid and complete with no such link.
5. IF a User who is not a Trip_Member of a Trip requests any Budget read or write on that Trip, THEN THE Trip_Service SHALL reject the request with `trip_forbidden`, identically for an unknown Trip id.
6. WHEN a Trip is deleted, THE Trip_Service SHALL delete every Budget_Line, Budget_Allocation, Traveler_Profile, Budget_Settings row and dismissed Suggestion of that Trip through the same `ON DELETE CASCADE` path as other Trip-owned entities.

### Requirement 2: Budget lines

**User Story:** As a Trip_Member, I want to add, change and remove planned costs, so that the budget reflects what the group intends to do.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Trip_Member submits a Budget_Line with a Budget_Category, a Description of 1 to 200 characters after trimming, a Price_Source, and at least one Budget_Allocation, THE Trip_Service SHALL create the Budget_Line and return it including its identifier.
2. THE Trip_Service SHALL accept an optional `plannedDate` (a calendar date, no Trip-range restriction), an optional `park` (one of the seven canonical `experiences.park` values), an optional `experienceId` referencing a Catalog Experience, an optional boolean `applyTip` (default false), and an optional `linkedExpenseId` (Requirement 17).
3. THE Trip_Service SHALL reject with `trip_validation_failed` a Budget_Line whose Budget_Category or Price_Source is outside its closed vocabulary, whose Description is empty or over 200 characters, whose `experienceId` does not exist in the Catalog, which sets `applyTip` true on a Budget_Category other than `dining` (the only tip rate is the dining tip, Requirement 7.3), or which has zero Budget_Allocations.
4. THE Trip_Service SHALL cap a Trip at `BUDGET_LINE_LIMIT` (500) Budget_Lines using the same race-safe row-lock count pattern as the Expense cap, and SHALL reject the 501st with `trip_validation_failed`.
5. THE Trip_Service SHALL permit the Trip_Member who created a Budget_Line, or any Organizer of the Trip, to edit or remove it, and SHALL reject any other Trip_Member with `trip_forbidden`; any Trip_Member MAY add a Budget_Line.
6. WHEN a Budget_Line is edited, THE Trip_Service SHALL apply the same validation as creation, and SHALL replace its Budget_Allocations atomically with the submitted set.
7. WHEN a Budget_Line is removed, THE Trip_Service SHALL delete it together with its Budget_Allocations.
8. THE Trip_Service SHALL record, for every Budget_Line, the Trip_Member who created it, independent of any Person_Key it allocates to.
9. WHEN a Catalog Experience linked to a Budget_Line is later deactivated or removed, THE Trip_Service SHALL keep the Budget_Line and its stored amounts unchanged (the link is informational).

### Requirement 3: Per-person allocations

**User Story:** As a Trip_Member, I want each planned cost attributed to specific people, so that I can see what each person needs to bring.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Trip_Service SHALL store each Budget_Allocation against exactly one Person_Key (a Trip_Member of the Trip XOR a Trip_Guest of the Trip) and SHALL reject an allocation naming both, neither, a person of a different Trip, or the same Person_Key twice on one Budget_Line, with `trip_validation_failed`.
2. THE Trip_Service SHALL store each Budget_Allocation `amountCents` as an integer greater than or equal to zero and not greater than `EXPENSE_AMOUNT_MAX_CENTS`, and SHALL reject any other value with `trip_validation_failed`.
3. THE Trip_Service SHALL limit a Budget_Line to `BUDGET_ALLOCATIONS_PER_LINE_MAX` (50) Budget_Allocations.
4. WHERE a Budget_Line is a whole-party cost entered as one total (for example a rental car), THE Mobile_App SHALL offer an "everyone" control that splits that total across the chosen people using the shared pure helper `splitCentsEvenly(totalCents, count)` from `@dwt/shared` (`packages/shared/src/money.ts`; the same semantics as `splitEvenly` in `services/tripExpenses/allocation.ts`: floor share, remainder one cent at a time in list order, with a cross-check test that the two agree), so the allocations sum exactly to the entered total. The Mobile_App SHALL import its integer-cent helpers (`splitCentsEvenly`, `bpsOf`) from `@dwt/shared` only, never from `apps/api`.
5. THE Trip_Service SHALL NOT allow a Trip_Guest to be removed while any Budget_Allocation references it, and SHALL reject such a removal with `trip_validation_failed` (recorded in `trip-expenses` as the additive criterion 2.13, which extends 2.9); the `trip_budget_allocations.guest_id` foreign key is `ON DELETE CASCADE` like `expense_shares.guest_id` so a Trip deletion or a purge never fails on it, and the route-level rejection is the user-facing rule; a Traveler_Profile of that guest does not block removal and is deleted with it (Requirement 4.7).
6. WHEN a Budget_Allocation names a deleted or `pending_deletion` account, THE Trip_Service SHALL follow Requirement 18.5.
7. WHEN a Budget_Line is edited, THE Trip_Service SHALL apply the on-Trip, deleted-account and duplicate checks of criterion 1 and Requirement 18.5 only to Person_Keys that are not already allocated on that Budget_Line, so a Person_Key whose Budget_Allocation already exists on the line (for example a member who has since left the Trip, Requirement 4.7) remains valid on edit and its allocation is not forced out; a Person_Key newly added by the edit SHALL be fully validated.

### Requirement 4: Traveler attributes

**User Story:** As a Trip_Member, I want to say who is going and what kind of ticket each person needs, so that ticket prices are looked up automatically for the right age and product.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Trip_Service SHALL store Traveler_Profiles in a per-Trip table keyed by Person_Key (member XOR guest), at most one profile per Person_Key per Trip.
2. A Traveler_Profile SHALL carry `ageBracket` (`adult`, `child` or `under3`, required), and optional `ticketOption`, `ticketDays` (1 to 10), `ticketStartDate`, `discountGroup` (default `std-gst`) and `passType` (default `none`).
3. THE Trip_Service SHALL NOT add a birth date, an age, or any other new column to the `users` table, and SHALL NOT store a birth date anywhere.
4. WHERE `ageBracket` is `under3`, THE Budget SHALL NOT price a ticket for that person, and the ticket Suggestion SHALL omit that person.
5. WHERE `passType` is not `none`, THE Budget SHALL NOT quote a ticket price for that person from the Ticket_Price_Cache and SHALL require a `user_entered` price for that person's ticket allocation.
6. THE Trip_Service SHALL permit a Trip_Member to create or edit the profile of themselves or of a Trip_Guest they sponsor, and an Organizer to create or edit any profile; it SHALL reject any other caller with `trip_forbidden`.
7. WHEN a Traveler_Profile's Person_Key is a Trip_Guest that is removed or a Trip_Member who leaves the Trip or is removed from it, THE Trip_Service SHALL delete that profile in the same database transaction as the guest deletion or the membership deletion (`departMember` in `services/trips/repo.ts`), SHALL leave Budget_Allocations already recorded for that person unchanged, and SHALL stop proposing ticket Suggestions for that person.
8. THE Trip_Service SHALL reject with `trip_validation_failed` a Traveler_Profile write whose Person_Key names a Trip_Member who is not on the Trip or a Trip_Guest that belongs to another Trip (round 5 L13; the member check is the single pinned raw `trip_memberships` read of Requirement 18.8, and an account that is `pending_deletion` or purged is answered `trip_party_deleted` per Requirement 18.5 before the on-Trip check, exactly as for a Budget_Line).

### Requirement 5: Price provenance, labels and read-time resolution

**User Story:** As a Trip_Member, I want to know where each price came from and how fresh it is, so that I can trust the budget and know which numbers are estimates.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Trip_Service SHALL store a `priceSource` on every Budget_Line from the closed set `disney_ticket_calendar`, `disney_menu`, `themeparks_ll`, `typical_estimate` (vocabulary only; see criterion 10), `user_entered`, and an `asOf` instant (the time the price was obtained from that source, or saved by the user) that is always present.
2. THE Trip_Service SHALL return with every Budget_Line a derived `effectivePriceSource`, a `priceLabelKind` (a Price_Label_Kind), and an `asOf`, computed on read by the pure `resolveLinePrices` function (the stored `priceSource` records how the line was priced; the effective one reflects the data available now).
3. THE Mobile_App SHALL render the label for each Price_Label_Kind as text, never by colour alone: `current` as "Disney price, as of <date>" (or "Lightning Lane price, as of <date>" or "Disney menu price (before tax), as of <date>" per source), `may_have_changed` as "<same text> (may have changed)", `estimate` as "Estimate" followed by the as-of date, `add_your_price` as "Add your price", and `your_price` as "Your price".
4. WHERE a Budget_Line's `priceSource` is `user_entered`, THE Trip_Service SHALL return the stored amounts unchanged as the effective amounts and SHALL NOT replace them with any cached, refreshed or estimated price, under any refresh, repricing or schedule change.
5. WHERE a Budget_Line's `priceSource` is `disney_ticket_calendar` or `themeparks_ll`, THE Trip_Service SHALL resolve each Budget_Allocation's effective amount on read from the current Ticket_Price_Cache or LL_Price_Store using the allocation's stored `priceRef`, and SHALL fall back to the stored snapshot amount with the stored `asOf` when no current price resolves.
6. WHERE a Budget_Line's `priceSource` is `disney_menu`, THE Trip_Service SHALL use the stored snapshot amount (the menu selection's `priceCents`) as the effective amount and SHALL NOT re-look-up the menu on read.
7. WHEN a user types a price over a sourced one, THE Mobile_App SHALL set the line's `priceSource` to `user_entered` and display "Your price".
8. THE Trip_Service SHALL NOT write to `trip_budget_lines` or `trip_budget_allocations` as a side effect of a read, of a Ticket_Price_Cache refresh, or of a LL_Price_Store update.
9. THE `priceLabelKind` for a ticket price SHALL be a total function of its As_Of, WDW_Today, requested start date and Horizon_Date as defined in Requirement 14.
10. THE `typical_estimate` Price_Source SHALL be an EFFECTIVE source only: no Budget route SHALL accept it as input and no repo method SHALL store it. WHEN a Budget_Line is created or edited with Price_Source `themeparks_ll` for a date that has no stored Lightning Lane price, THE Trip_Service SHALL store the line with `priceSource = 'themeparks_ll'`, the `llPriceRef`, the Typical_LL_Price as the snapshot amount and the line `price_as_of` (and each allocation snapshot `asOf`) set to the `date` of the newest history sample used to compute that Typical_LL_Price (round 4 L4: the instant of the request is NOT used, so the "Estimate, as of <date>" label does not change every day; the label reads "Estimate" followed by that sample date, and a re-resolved typical estimate on read carries the same sample date; round 5 L5: a sample `date` is converted to the stored instant by the ONE function `dateToInstant(date)` in `ticketPrices/dates.ts` (12:00:00Z of that date, which falls on the same `America/New_York` calendar date in every season), so `wdwDateOf(dateToInstant(d)) === d` for every date and the stored `price_as_of` never renders one day early); on each later read THE Trip_Service SHALL re-resolve it, returning `effectivePriceSource = 'typical_estimate'` and label `estimate` while no stored price exists for that date, and returning the stored real price, `effectivePriceSource = 'themeparks_ll'` and label `current` (or `may_have_changed` per Requirement 15.7) once the Sampling_Job has stored one, without any write to the Budget_Line (Requirement 5.8).

### Requirement 6: Computed views

**User Story:** As a Trip_Member, I want to see the budget by whole party, by person, by day, by park and by category, so that I can decide what to cut or save for.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Trip_Service SHALL compute the Budget views on read from Budget_Lines, Budget_Allocations and Budget_Settings in the pure module `budgetViews.ts`, and SHALL NOT persist any total or view.
2. THE Budget response SHALL include the whole-party total and a breakdown by Person_Key, by day, by park and by Budget_Category.
3. FOR every Budget, the sum of the by-person buckets, the sum of the by-day buckets, the sum of the by-park buckets and the sum of the by-category buckets SHALL each equal the whole-party total exactly, in integer cents.
4. WHERE a Budget_Line has no `plannedDate`, THE by-day view SHALL place it in the bucket `unassigned`, labelled "Unassigned".
5. WHERE a Budget_Line has no `park`, THE by-park view SHALL place it in the bucket `other_resort`, labelled "Other (resort or multi-park)" (round 4 L3: the label was "Other (resort)"; it is widened because a multi-day or park-hopper ticket legitimately has no single park and must not read as a resort cost; the bucket id `other_resort` is unchanged; 143 of 448 Catalog restaurants had `park` NULL when counted on the local development database on 2026-10-25; production was not checked, so treat the figure as indicative; the point stands that resort and other dining legitimately has no theme park).
6. THE by-person view SHALL attribute each line's tax and tip to people in proportion to their pre-tax allocation using the Budget-local non-negative largest-remainder split `allocateShares` of the design (round 5 M2: NOT the Expense helper `allocateProportionally`, whose rounding can return a negative share, see Requirement 6.9), so the per-person totals sum to the line total exactly.
7. WHEN a Budget_Line has both a linked Experience with a non-null `park` and a `park` value, THE stored `park` SHALL prevail; WHEN a line is created from a Suggestion, THE `park` SHALL be the linked Experience's `park` when non-null, otherwise `trips.day_touring_hours[plannedDate].startingPark` when it equals (case-insensitive exact match) one of the seven canonical `PARKS` names, otherwise null (`startingPark` is a free string in `packages/shared/src/trips.ts`, so a value that is not canonical is treated as absent and a Suggestion can never carry a `park` that the form schema or the `trip_budget_lines.park` CHECK rejects).
8. THE Trip_Service SHALL present all money as integer cents and the Mobile_App SHALL round only for display.
9. FOR every Budget_Line, every per-person tax, tip and line amount SHALL be a non-negative integer, a Budget_Allocation of 0 cents SHALL receive exactly 0 tax and exactly 0 tip, and each person's tax share SHALL differ from the exact proportional value `tax * allocation / subtotal` by less than one cent (round 5 M2; the split is a pure largest-remainder allocation over integer cents, so no by-person bucket can show a negative amount).

### Requirement 7: Tax and gratuity

**User Story:** As a Trip_Member, I want the budget to include tax and tip, with rates I can change, so that I save the right amount and not just the menu prices.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Budget SHALL treat every sourced price as pre-tax (ticket `subtotal`, menu `withoutTax`, Lightning Lane price), and SHALL apply tax and gratuity on read from Budget_Settings.
2. THE Trip_Service SHALL compute, per Budget_Line, `tax = floor((subtotal * taxBps + 5000) / 10000)` and `tip = applyTip ? floor((subtotal * tipBps + 5000) / 10000) : 0`, both on the pre-tax `subtotal`, and `lineTotal = subtotal + tax + tip` (gratuity is not computed on tax).
3. THE Budget_Settings SHALL hold a tax rate in basis points per Budget_Category (integer 0 to 2500) and a dining tip rate in basis points (integer 0 to 5000), per Trip, with defaults from `BUDGET_DEFAULT_TAX_BPS` and `BUDGET_DEFAULT_DINING_TIP_BPS` in `@dwt/shared` when no override is stored.
4. THE default ticket tax rate SHALL be 650 basis points (6.5%, observed as `tax / subtotal` on every sampled Disney price row); the defaults for food, Lightning Lane, shopping and the dining tip SHALL be labelled in the UI as assumptions ("Assumed rate; change it if yours differs") because they are unverified.
5. THE Trip_Service SHALL permit only an Organizer to change Budget_Settings and SHALL reject any other caller with `trip_forbidden`.
6. THE Mobile_App SHALL show subtotal, tax and tip separately on each Budget_Line and in totals, and SHALL show the assumption labels of criterion 4.
7. FOR a fixed set of Budget_Lines, raising any tax or tip rate SHALL never decrease any line total or the whole-party total.
8. THE Trip_Service SHALL apply the tip only to a `dining` Budget_Line whose `applyTip` is true; a stored `tax_bps` value SHALL be a JSON object whose keys are Budget_Categories and whose values are integers 0 to 2500 (validated by the repo and backed by a database `CHECK (jsonb_typeof(tax_bps) = 'object')`).

### Requirement 8: Savings plan

**User Story:** As a Trip_Member, I want to know how much to put aside per week until the trip, so that the money is there on time.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Trip_Service SHALL compute the Savings_Plan with the pure function `computeSavingsPlan` from `targetCents` (the Budget_Settings `savingsTargetCents` when set, otherwise the whole-party total), `savedCents`, WDW_Today and the Trip's start date.
2. `remainingCents` SHALL equal `max(0, targetCents - savedCents)`; `daysUntilStart` SHALL equal the number of calendar days from WDW_Today to the Trip start date; `weeksRemaining` SHALL equal `ceil(daysUntilStart / 7)` when `daysUntilStart > 0`; `perWeekCents` SHALL equal `ceil(remainingCents / weeksRemaining)`.
3. WHERE `remainingCents` is 0, THE Savings_Plan SHALL report `perWeekCents` 0 and status `on_track`.
4. WHERE `remainingCents` is greater than 0 and `daysUntilStart` is 0 or negative, THE Savings_Plan SHALL report `perWeekCents` null, status `trip_started_or_past`, and the `remainingCents`.
5. THE Budget_Settings `savedCents` SHALL default to 0 and be settable by an Organizer (Requirement 7.5); it is a Trip-level figure, not per person.

### Requirement 9: Restaurant lines

**User Story:** As a Trip_Member, I want to pick what we will eat at a restaurant (or use the prix fixe or buffet price), so that the dining cost is based on real menu prices.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Budget_Line has Budget_Category `dining` and a linked restaurant Experience with a cached menu, THE Mobile_App SHALL offer the shared Menu Item Picker so each person's allocation can be built from selected menu items.
2. WHEN a person's allocation is built from selected items, THE Budget_Allocation `amountCents` SHALL equal the sum of the selected items' `priceCents` times their quantities, and THE allocation `priceRef` SHALL store the selection as `{ kind: 'menu', items: [{ name, priceCents, priceLabel, quantity, isAlcohol? }] }` (the `menuPriceRefSchema` of the design).
3. WHERE the restaurant has a prix fixe, buffet or per-person price offer parsed by the Menu_Price_Parser (for example "$49 per adult" and "$42 per child (Ages 3-9)"), THE Mobile_App SHALL offer it as a per-person tier choice (adult or child by the person's Age_Bracket) and store `{ kind: 'menu', tier: { name, priceCents } }` in the allocation `priceRef`.
4. WHERE a menu item has several prices (for example sizes "$12 / $18"), THE picker SHALL require the user to choose one price; WHERE an item has no parseable price, THE picker SHALL require the user to type a price and the line's `priceSource` SHALL become `user_entered`.
5. WHERE an item's group `type` is `Alcoholic Beverage` (case-insensitive), THE picker SHALL pre-check `isAlcohol` for that item.
6. ### Requirement 11: Ticket price calendar source

**User Story:** As a Trip_Member, I want ticket prices for my dates filled in automatically, so that I do not have to look up Disney prices.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Ticket_Client SHALL obtain prices from Disney endpoint P `GET /api/lexicon-view-assembler-service/wdw/tickets/product-types/theme-parks/prices` with parameters `storeId=wdw`, `discountGroup`, `startDate`, `endDate`, `numDays`, and header `Authorization: BEARER <token>`, where the token comes from endpoint T `GET /profile-api/authentication/get-client-token/` (response `{"access_token":..., "expires_in":1800}`), exactly as specified in the design's External Interfaces.
2. THE Ticket_Client SHALL request `startDate` no earlier than WDW_Today **[A: the timezone basis of Disney's "today" is unverified; only "earlier than today returns 400" was observed, so Disney may compare against the UTC date, which is ahead of `America/New_York` in the evening; the implementer MUST verify with the Diagnose action run once after 20:00 ET, Requirement 19.3]** and `endDate` no later than the Horizon_Date, `numDays` from 1 to 10, and `discountGroup` only from the configured `TICKET_PRICES_GROUPS`.
3. THE parser SHALL read a price only from `pricing[].subtotal` (decimal dollars as a string, pre-tax), converting it to integer cents by decimal-string arithmetic, and SHALL NOT use `pricePerDay`.
4. THE parser SHALL map each `pricing[].id` of the form `theme-park_{numDays}_{A|C}_{opt}_{res}_RF_AF_SOF_progenstr[_{park}]` to `age_group` (`A` adult, `C` child), `option_code` (`0` base, `P` park_hopper, `PHP` park_hopper_plus, `WPS` water_parks_sports), `discount_group` (`0` std-gst, `2` fl-resident) and `park` (`mk`, `ep`, `hs`, `ak` only on 1-day base rows, otherwise `any`), and SHALL cross-check the id's age token against `ageGroup`, its day token against the requested `numDays`, and its resident token against the requested `discountGroup`.
5. THE parser SHALL skip, and count as `skippedUnrecognized`, any pricing entry whose id does not match the pattern or fails the cross-checks, and SHALL skip and count as `skippedNotSellable` any entry with `stopSale` true, `availability` false or `enabled` false.
6. THE Ticket_Price_Cache SHALL store `ticket_prices(discount_group, num_days, age_group, option_code, park, start_date, price_cents, tax_cents, valid_end_date, product_id, changed_at)` with the 6-column primary key `(discount_group, num_days, age_group, option_code, park, start_date)`, and `ticket_price_sets(discount_group, num_days, as_of, horizon_date, row_count, window_days, last_attempt_at, last_error, blocked_until)` carrying freshness (`window_days` is the length of the fetch window that produced `row_count`, used by criterion 9), and `ticket_price_control` as a one-row table carrying the global cooldown, probe time, observed horizon, last diagnose time, in-progress claim and block state.
7. WHEN a fetched set is accepted, THE repo SHALL dedupe its rows by the 6-column key in a `Map` before each `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE` statement (Postgres error `21000`), SHALL upsert with `WHERE (price_cents, tax_cents, valid_end_date) IS DISTINCT FROM (EXCLUDED...)` so unchanged rows are not rewritten, and SHALL advance `as_of` only on the set row.
8. WHEN a fetched set is accepted, THE repo SHALL delete rows of that `(discount_group, num_days)` with `start_date BETWEEN <the startDate actually sent> AND <endDate>` that are absent from the fetched set (round 4 L6: the reconcile is scoped to the window actually requested, so after the one UTC-date start-date retry of Requirement 11.13 it can never delete the WDW_Today row the response did not cover), and SHALL delete all rows with `start_date < WDW_Today` of every set, so the table stays bounded.
9. THE Ticket_Refresh_Service SHALL reject a fetched set, keep the old rows, record `last_error`, and NOT advance `as_of` when the set has zero accepted rows, when any accepted entry has a non-numeric or non-positive `subtotal`, when its accepted rows per window day (`rows / days from startDate to endDate inclusive`) are below `TICKET_PRICES_SANITY_MIN_RATIO` (0.5, an **[A]** starting value) of the previous accepted set's rows per window day (`row_count / window_days`; the window shrinks by a day every day while the horizon is fixed, so raw counts are never compared), or when more than `TICKET_PRICES_MAX_UNRECOGNIZED_FRACTION` (0.1) of its entries were unrecognized.
10. THE Ticket_Client SHALL validate each response with a Zod schema whose fields are exactly the ones this design relies on and SHALL treat a validation failure as a rejected set (`invalid_response`), not as an empty success.
11. THE feature SHALL NOT price Passholder, DVC, Annual Pass or single-day water park tickets from this source; those are `user_entered`.
12. THE feature SHALL NOT call any Disney page other than endpoints T and P (no HTML pages, no `/passes/`).
13. IF endpoint P answers HTTP 400 to a call whose `startDate` is the WDW_Today date, THEN THE Ticket_Client SHALL retry that one call once with `startDate = max(WDW_Today, the UTC date)` (never an earlier date), and IF the retry also fails, THE Ticket_Refresh_Service SHALL record `last_error = 'start_date_rejected'` on the affected set (or on the control row for a probe or diagnose), SHALL keep the claim-time `last_attempt_at` (it SHALL NOT restore or rewind any cooldown stamp, so the 24-hour attempt cooldown is consumed), SHALL set `blocked_until = now() + TICKET_PRICES_FAILURE_COOLDOWN_MINUTES` with reason `start_date_rejected`, and SHALL NOT retry again in the same run; WHILE that block lasts, any number of further Budget reads or quotes SHALL start no run and cause no Disney call. The retry keeps the window valid: its endDate is the original endDate, or the retry startDate when that is later, so a single-day call (startDate = endDate, the horizon probe and Diagnose) stays a single-day call and no request is ever sent with startDate after endDate.
14. THE `last_error` columns of `ticket_price_sets` and `ticket_price_control`, and every block reason, SHALL hold only a Ticket_Error_Code from the closed set of the Glossary; the repo SHALL normalize any other value to `invalid_response`, SHALL NOT store, log at `info` or above, or render any upstream response text, and the Admin_Panel SHALL render these values HTML-escaped.

### Requirement 12: Demand-driven refresh and request hygiene

**User Story:** As the project owner, I want ticket prices refreshed only when someone needs them and only rarely, so that Disney's servers see almost no traffic from us.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE feature SHALL NOT add any cron job, scheduled job, background worker, queue or timer for ticket prices, and SHALL NOT add a `TICKET_PRICES_CRON_SECRET`.
2. WHEN a Budget read needs a ticket price and the `ticket_price_sets` row for the needed `(discount_group, num_days)` is missing or its `as_of` is older than `TICKET_PRICES_STALE_AFTER_DAYS` (30), THE Trip_Service SHALL serve the cached rows immediately and start at most one background refresh, without awaiting it.
3. THE Ticket_Refresh_Service SHALL run single-flight in-process, and SHALL claim a global cooldown in Postgres atomically (one `UPDATE ... RETURNING` that also sets `last_attempt_at = now()` at claim time) so at most one `full` refresh attempt starts per `TICKET_PRICES_MIN_ATTEMPT_INTERVAL_HOURS` (24) across all instances (except an explicit admin Refresh now, Requirement 19.2, which bypasses only this attempt cooldown and never the block, the kill switch or the in-flight check), so at most one `probe_only` attempt starts per `TICKET_PRICES_PROBE_MIN_INTERVAL_HOURS` (24) measured on `last_probe_at` (the claim takes a `mode` argument: a `probe_only` claim checks `last_probe_at` and sets it at claim time and the `probe_only` claim statement itself does not read or set `last_attempt_at` (only a successful `upgradeClaimToFull`, Requirement 12.4, stamps `last_attempt_at`, at that moment's claim time of a full attempt), so a failed full run cannot cause a probe re-attempt on every read), and so a claim older than 15 minutes with no completion is treated as abandoned.
4. WHEN a Budget read needs a start date later than the cached `horizon_date`, THE Ticket_Refresh_Service SHALL run only the horizon probe (token call plus one `numDays=1` call with `startDate=endDate=WDW_Today`), at most once per `TICKET_PRICES_PROBE_MIN_INTERVAL_HOURS` (24), and SHALL pull the sets again only if the returned `maxPricingCalendarDate` differs from the cached `horizon_date` AND the run also wins the full-refresh attempt cooldown of criterion 3 (a probe never pulls on its own authority); WHEN the attempt cooldown is active, THE Ticket_Refresh_Service SHALL pull nothing, SHALL store the observed horizon in `ticket_price_control.observed_horizon_date`, SHALL end with status `horizon_changed`, and the refresh planner SHALL then treat the demanded sets as stale so that the next demand after the cooldown runs a `full` refresh. No sequence of demand-driven runs SHALL start two set-pulling runs within `TICKET_PRICES_MIN_ATTEMPT_INTERVAL_HOURS`; the only exception is an explicit admin Refresh now (Requirement 19.2).
5. A full refresh run SHALL consist of one token call, one horizon probe, and one call per stale or missing `numDays` of each configured group, each call requesting `startDate=WDW_Today` through `endDate=Horizon_Date`, with the numDays that the triggering read needed fetched first.
6. THE Ticket_Client SHALL wait `TICKET_PRICES_REQUEST_SPACING_MS` (1500) between consecutive calls, reuse one token per run, and on HTTP 401 for a price call refetch the token once and retry that call once.
7. THE Ticket_Client SHALL send the User-Agent from `TICKET_PRICES_USER_AGENT`, an honest descriptive value, and SHALL NOT send a browser User-Agent.
8. THE Ticket_Client SHALL use a request timeout of `TICKET_PRICES_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_MS` (20000) and SHALL reject a response body larger than `TICKET_PRICES_MAX_BODY_BYTES` (5000000) as `invalid_response`.
9. A Budget read SHALL issue zero Redis commands and zero outbound HTTP requests on its own call path; the only outbound traffic is the background refresh of criteria 2 and 4.

### Requirement 13: Kill switch and circuit breaker

**User Story:** As the project owner, I want to be able to turn ticket fetching off instantly and have it stop itself if Disney pushes back, so that the app never hammers or gets the server blocked.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Kill_Switch `TICKET_PRICES_ENABLED` SHALL default to false in every environment; WHILE it is false, THE Ticket_Refresh_Service SHALL make no outbound request for a refresh or a probe, Budget reads SHALL continue to serve the cache, and the admin "refresh now" SHALL refuse with an explanatory message.
2. WHILE `TICKET_PRICES_ENABLED` is false, THE Admin_Panel ticket-price page SHALL display next to the kill-switch state the operator gate "Enable only after Diagnose has succeeded three times, at different times of day on different days, including once after 20:00 ET" (an operator procedure; the system records no count).
3. WHEN any response to endpoint T or P is HTTP 403 or 429, or any non-JSON body or non-2xx body contains an Akamai/edge marker from `DISNEY_WAF_BODY_MARKERS` (the Ticket_Client applies those markers itself to every non-JSON body and every non-2xx body regardless of status, so an Akamai challenge returned with HTTP 200 or an edge page returned with HTTP 503 is recognised; `classifyDisneyResponse` from `catalog/disney/classify.ts`, whose input also requires `target: 'web'`, is reused for the 403/429 decision only because it inspects markers only for those two statuses), THE Ticket_Refresh_Service SHALL stop the run immediately without retrying, and SHALL set `blocked_until = now() + TICKET_PRICES_BLOCK_COOLDOWN_MINUTES` (360) on `ticket_price_control`.
4. WHILE `blocked_until` is in the future, THE Ticket_Refresh_Service SHALL start no refresh, probe or diagnose, and SHALL report the block time.
5. WHEN an endpoint call fails with a network error, a timeout, an HTTP 5xx or an invalid response, THE Ticket_Refresh_Service SHALL record `last_error` on the affected set, leave its rows and `as_of` untouched, continue with the other sets of the run unless the failure was a WAF block or the consecutive-failure limit of criterion 9 is reached, and SHALL NOT retry that call in the same run.
6. WHEN the endpoint returns HTTP 404 for a `(fl-resident, numDays)` set, THE Ticket_Refresh_Service SHALL record `last_error = 'not_offered'` for that set and SHALL NOT treat it as a failure of the run; a set with `last_error = 'not_offered'` SHALL NOT be counted as stale by the refresh planner (it never has an `as_of`), so it cannot trigger a refresh by itself, and the planner SHALL only consider `(discount_group, numDays)` pairs that are permitted by `TICKET_PRICES_GROUPS` and, for `fl-resident`, listed in `TICKET_PRICES_FL_RESIDENT_NUMDAYS`.
7. THE Ticket_Refresh_Service SHALL never log or return the access token, and logs SHALL redact the `Authorization` header.
8. THE Ticket_Client SHALL be a separate static-data client wired in its own composition block; it SHALL NOT be added to the Disney live path in `composeServices.ts`, no module under `services/ticketPrices/` SHALL import `catalog/disney/transport.ts`, `catalog/disney/rateLimiter.ts`, an `ioredis` client, or any module of the retired live path (`catalog/disney/liveClient.ts`, `liveService.ts`), and `retirement.smoke.test.ts` SHALL keep passing unmodified.
9. WHEN `TICKET_PRICES_MAX_CONSECUTIVE_FAILURES` (default 2) consecutive calls of one run end in a network error, a timeout, an HTTP 5xx or an invalid response, THE Ticket_Refresh_Service SHALL stop the run and SHALL set `blocked_until = now() + TICKET_PRICES_FAILURE_COOLDOWN_MINUTES` (default 60) on `ticket_price_control`, so a degraded edge is called at most that many times in a run.
10. THE Admin_Panel SHALL provide a Block_Now action (`POST /admin/ticket-prices/block`, same-origin enforced) that sets `ticket_price_control.blocked_until = now() + TICKET_PRICES_BLOCK_COOLDOWN_MINUTES` with reason `manual`, taking effect on the next claim attempt of every instance without a redeploy and, for a run that is already in flight, before that run's next Disney call (Requirement 13.12); the page SHALL document it beside the Kill_Switch (the environment flag needs a redeploy to change).
11. THE Ticket_Refresh_Service SHALL send the horizon probe and the Diagnose price call with the `std-gst` Discount_Group, and `TICKET_PRICES_GROUPS` SHALL be required to contain `std-gst` (a boot-time validation error otherwise), because `fl-resident` with `numDays=1` is unverified and a 404 on a probe has no set to carry `not_offered`. An HTTP 404 on a `std-gst` call, and a second HTTP 401 for the same call after the one token refetch, SHALL be recorded as `http_status:404` and `http_status:401` respectively and SHALL count as failures toward `TICKET_PRICES_MAX_CONSECUTIVE_FAILURES` (round 5 L3).
12. BEFORE every Disney call of a run after the first, THE Ticket_Refresh_Service SHALL re-read `ticket_price_control.blocked_until` (one indexed single-row read) and, WHEN it is in the future, SHALL stop the run without making that call and release the claim with status `blocked`, so the Block_Now action stops an in-flight run after at most the one call already in progress (round 5 L4; at most 12 calls per run).

### Requirement 14: Ticket price lookup and freshness labels

**User Story:** As a Trip_Member, I want a ticket price for my dates even when the cache is old or my dates are far in the future, clearly labelled.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE pure function `resolveTicketPrice` SHALL take the requested `(discountGroup, numDays, ageGroup, optionCode, startDate, park?)`, the cached rows and set metadata, and WDW_Today, and return `{ priceCents | null, taxCents | null, resolvedStartDate, parkAssumed, labelKind, asOf }`.
2. WHERE an exact row exists for the requested key, THE function SHALL use it with `resolvedStartDate = startDate`.
3. WHERE `startDate` is after the set's `horizon_date`, THE function SHALL set `target = startDate - 364 days` (52 whole weeks, so the same weekday, using `ticketPrices/dates.ts`; round 4 L2: the latest cached date is always inside the holiday-peak last week of the horizon, so it would bias every beyond-horizon estimate high) and, WHEN `target` falls on or after WDW_Today and on or before the `horizon_date`, use the cached row of that key whose date has the same weekday (`wdwDayOfWeek`) as `startDate` and is nearest to `target` (ties resolve to the earlier date); OTHERWISE (the `target` is itself before WDW_Today or beyond the horizon, or no such row exists) use the row for the latest cached date on or before the `horizon_date` and on or after WDW_Today that has the same weekday as `startDate`; the label SHALL be `estimate` in both cases.
4. WHERE `startDate` is within the horizon but no exact row exists, THE function SHALL use the cached row with the same weekday nearest to `startDate` (ties resolve to the earlier date), and the label SHALL be `estimate`.
5. WHERE the requested key is a 1-day base ticket and `park` is null, THE function SHALL use the highest `price_cents` among `mk`, `ep`, `hs`, `ak` for that date, set `parkAssumed` true, and the label SHALL be `estimate`; WHERE `park` is one of `mk`, `ep`, `hs`, `ak`, THE function SHALL return exactly that park's row with `parkAssumed` false (a 1-day base ticket has a per-park price: the investigation verified that 1-day base rows exist per park, with 12 entries per date, and that the per-park peaks differ, for example the December 2026 peaks of Magic Kingdom $209, EPCOT $209, Hollywood Studios $204 and Animal Kingdom $184; round 5 L6: no single date with two different park prices was recorded, so none is asserted here).
6. THE age class SHALL be computed from whole days between the set's `as_of` date and WDW_Today: at most `TICKET_PRICES_LABEL_FRESH_DAYS` (45) is `current`; above 45 and at most `TICKET_PRICES_LABEL_ESTIMATE_AFTER_DAYS` (120) is `may_have_changed`; above 120 is `estimate`. THE final label SHALL be the more severe of the age class and any `estimate` condition of criteria 3 to 5.
7. WHERE no row resolves (no cache at all, no set for that length, or no same-weekday date), THE function SHALL return `priceCents` null and `labelKind` `add_your_price`.
8. THE Florida-resident Discount_Group SHALL be reported unavailable ONLY for a pair that will never be fetched (round 5 M3): THE quote response SHALL carry `flUnavailable: true` and `estimateReason: 'fl_unavailable'` with a null price if and only if `discountGroup` is `fl-resident` AND (the `(fl-resident, numDays)` pair is not permitted by the configuration (`fl-resident` is not in `TICKET_PRICES_GROUPS`, or `numDays` is not listed in `TICKET_PRICES_FL_RESIDENT_NUMDAYS`; the same `allowedNumDaysByGroup` map the refresh planner uses, passed to the resolver as `pairPermitted`) OR the set's `last_error` is `not_offered`), and THE Mobile_App SHALL show "Florida resident price unavailable for N days; enter your price" from that flag (the app has no access to the configuration lists, so it never decides this itself). A permitted pair that has simply not been fetched yet (no set row, or a set with `as_of` null and no `not_offered`) SHALL carry `flUnavailable: false`; its state is reported by `cacheState` (Requirement 14.12), so the first Florida-resident quote after the switch is turned on says "being fetched" and not "unavailable".
9. THE Trip_Service SHALL, WHERE a ticket `priceRef` for a 1-day base ticket on a Budget_Line create, edit or read-time resolution omits `park` and the Budget_Line has a `park` that maps through `TICKET_PARK_BY_CANONICAL_PARK` (`Magic Kingdom`→`mk`, `EPCOT`→`ep`, `Hollywood Studios`→`hs`, `Animal Kingdom`→`ak`), apply that mapped park on the server, so the same default holds for creates and read-time resolution. A price quote (`POST /trips/:id/budget/quotes`) carries no Budget_Line, so the server SHALL apply no park default to a quote: the Mobile_App's ticket picker SHALL default `priceRef.park` client-side from the line's `park` and send it, and a quote without `park` for a 1-day base ticket resolves as in criterion 5.
10. WHERE `startDate` is earlier than WDW_Today (a past trip viewed later; past dates are pruned from the cache), THE function SHALL NOT substitute a future same-weekday date; it SHALL return `priceCents` null, `labelKind` `add_your_price` and `estimateReason` `none`, and the stored snapshot fallback of Requirement 5.5 SHALL apply to such a Budget_Line.
11. THE quote response of the Trip_Service SHALL carry `estimateReason` (one of `beyond_horizon`, `no_exact_date`, `park_unset`, `typical`, `stale`, `fl_unavailable`, `none`) and, for a ticket quote, the `resolvedStartDate` and `parkAssumed` values, so that the Mobile_App renders the text of the reason without recomputing it.
12. THE quote response for a ticket SHALL carry `cacheState` (`disabled`, `warming`, `failed` or `ready`), the output of ONE total pure function `ticketCacheState` of `(enabled, now, blockedUntil, inProgressSince, lastAttemptAt, attemptIntervalHours, claimTimeoutMinutes, pairPermitted, set)` where `set` is `null` or `{ asOf, lastError }` (round 5 M3 and M4), evaluated in this order, first match wins: (1) `disabled` iff `!enabled || blockedUntil > now` (where `enabled` is `TICKET_PRICES_ENABLED`); (2) `ready` iff the pair needs no fetch, that is `!pairPermitted`, or `set.lastError === 'not_offered'`, or `set.asOf != null` (rows exist; an older failed refresh keeps serving them with their aged label); (3) `failed` iff the pair is permitted, `set` is null or has `asOf == null`, and `set.lastError != null` (the last attempt to fetch this set failed, so "try again in a minute" would be false); (4) `warming` iff the pair is permitted, never fetched, with no recorded error, AND a fetch is under way or about to start (`inProgressSince` is within the claim timeout, or `lastAttemptAt` is null or at least `attemptIntervalHours` ago, so the demand of this very read starts a `full` run); (5) otherwise `failed` (never fetched, no recorded error, nothing in flight and the attempt cooldown still active, which happens when an earlier run died before reaching this set; no run can start for up to the cooldown, so the truthful state is "not available now"). A set that will never be fetched (pair not permitted, or `not_offered`) is therefore `ready`, so it never shows a "being fetched" text, and a failed or cooling-down set never shows one either. **Precedence: WHEN the quote's `flUnavailable` is true (Requirement 14.8), THE Mobile_App SHALL render the Florida-resident text and SHALL ignore `cacheState`.** The Mobile_App SHALL render the `cacheState` text only when the quote's `priceCents` is null and `flUnavailable` is false (a quote that has a price shows its price label whatever the state). It SHALL be derived from the control and set rows the quote already reads plus the configuration map (no additional I/O). WHEN a ticket quote has a null price, THE Mobile_App SHALL render from `cacheState`, as text with an accessibility label: for `warming` "Prices are being fetched, try again in a minute", for `disabled` "Automatic ticket prices are unavailable right now; enter your price", for `failed` "Automatic ticket prices could not be fetched; enter your price", and for `ready` the existing "Add your price" or Florida-resident text, so a cold cache is never presented as "no price exists" and a failed fetch is never presented as "try again in a minute".

### Requirement 15: Lightning Lane prices

**User Story:** As a Trip_Member, I want Lightning Lane costs for my dates, so that I can decide whether to buy it.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE sampling job SHALL extract Lightning Lane prices from the park schedule response it already fetches for every park in its existing per-park loop (`liveClient.getEntitySchedule(park.id)` in `samplingService.ts`, which resolves to `GET {THEMEPARKS_BASE_URL}/entity/{parkGuid}/schedule`), keyed by the `canonicalPark` that loop already computes, with zero additional HTTP requests, and SHALL persist Multi Pass, Premier Pass and per-attraction Single Pass prices and `available` flags. (The WDW destination-level schedule call `GET /entity/e957da41-3552-4cf6-b636-5babc5cbc4e5/schedule`, which returns all six parks in one request, is deliberately NOT used: the sampler already holds each park's schedule, the destination call would be a net extra request, and its `parks[]` entries cannot be mapped to a `canonicalPark` without the per-park live data. The env var `THEMEPARKS_WDW_DESTINATION_ID` is therefore not introduced.)
2. THE extractor SHALL classify a purchase by `type === 'PACKAGE'` and `id` prefix `lightninglanemultipass_` (Multi Pass) or `premierpass_` (Premier Pass), falling back to the exact `name` only for those two package names, and `type === 'ATTRACTION'` with `id` prefix `lightninglane_` (Single Pass); every other purchase is ignored.
3. THE extractor SHALL read `price.amount` as integer cents (never multiplied or divided) and ignore a purchase whose amount is missing, non-integer or not greater than zero.
4. THE LL_Price_Store SHALL be additive and have ONE authoritative writer for the park-level columns (round 4 L1: besides `ll_prices_captured_at` the table SHALL carry `ll_multipass_captured_at` and `ll_premier_captured_at`, derived in the same statement from the one incoming timestamp and advancing only with a non-null incoming price of their own kind, so a stored Premier Pass price is labelled by its own age and not by a later Multi Pass capture; `StoredLlRow.capturedAt` is read from the column of its own pass type): the existing `ScheduleSignalRow` type and `IntelligenceRepo.upsertParkScheduleSignals` are extended with the new columns `ll_multipass_available`, `ll_premier_price_cents`, `ll_premier_available`, `ll_prices_captured_at`, and the row builder in `samplingService.ts` fills `ll_multipass_price_cents` with the id-prefix classifier of criterion 2 (instead of the exact name match), so one statement per park per pass writes every park-level column and no second writer can overwrite another's value; the statement is NON-DESTRUCTIVE on null for every Lightning Lane column (the four new ones AND `ll_multipass_price_cents`): each is assigned `COALESCE(EXCLUDED.col, park_schedule_signals.col)`, so a pass whose extraction failed or whose schedule carried no purchases for a date leaves the previously stored Multi Pass price, Premier Pass price and availability untouched, and every consumer of `ll_multipass_price_cents` is unchanged except that a transient empty extraction no longer erases it; `ll_prices_captured_at` is assigned `CASE WHEN EXCLUDED.ll_multipass_price_cents IS NOT NULL OR EXCLUDED.ll_premier_price_cents IS NOT NULL THEN EXCLUDED.ll_prices_captured_at ELSE park_schedule_signals.ll_prices_captured_at END`, so it advances only when the row carries a Lightning Lane price from the current pass and otherwise keeps the timestamp of the stored values (NULL when none was ever stored), which means it always expresses the age of the stored price and the `may_have_changed` label appears once `LL_PRICES_STALE_HOURS` elapse without a fresh price; and a new table `lightning_lane_single_prices(park, date, purchase_id, experience_id, attraction_name, price_cents, available, captured_at)` keyed `(park, date, purchase_id)`.
5. THE persistence SHALL dedupe rows by their conflict key in a `Map` before each batch upsert; the `lightning_lane_single_prices` writes and its prune (the only extra write volume, up to a few thousand rows per pass) SHALL run at most once per `LL_PRICES_MIN_REFRESH_HOURS` (12), judged from `max(captured_at)` of that table at the start of the pass, while the park-level columns ride the existing per-park statement on every pass; and it SHALL ride the existing sampling endpoint (no new endpoint, cron, worker or Redis use).
6. THE single-pass `experience_id` SHALL be resolved by an EXACT, type-qualified match: the numeric suffix `N` of `purchase_id` (extracted in TypeScript by `/_(\d+)$/`; no match means no lookup) is matched to `experiences.upstream_entity_id = N || ';entityType=Attraction'`, which is unique because `experiences_upstream_entity_id_key` is a unique index (round 5 M5: the earlier `split_part(upstream_entity_id, ';', 1)` match is NOT unique, because one numeric id is shared across entity types (for example `80010856` by a `Dinner-Show` and an `Entertainment` Experience on the local development database), so it could return two rows for one input and raise Postgres error `21000` in the `ON CONFLICT` batch); an Experience of any other entity type never matches; when no Attraction Experience matches, the row SHALL be stored with `experience_id` NULL; and the join SHALL return at most one row per input row (the number of rows written equals the number of distinct `park|date|purchase_id` keys sent).
7. THE LL price for a requested `(passType, park, date[, experienceId])` SHALL be the stored price when the date has a stored price; its label is `current` when `captured_at` is within `LL_PRICES_STALE_HOURS` (72) of now and `may_have_changed` otherwise, and an `available` of false SHALL be reported as "sold out" while still returning the price.
8. WHERE no stored price exists for the date, THE function `typicalLlPrice` SHALL return the median of stored prices for the same park (and for Single Pass the same attraction), same weekday and same month over the last `LL_TYPICAL_LOOKBACK_DAYS` (400) when at least `LL_TYPICAL_MIN_SAMPLES` (4) exist, otherwise the median for the same park and weekday over the same lookback when at least 4 exist, otherwise `no_estimate`; the label SHALL be `estimate`.
9. THE typical price SHALL always lie within the minimum and maximum of the samples it was computed from.
10. THE LL_Price_Store SHALL be pruned of rows with `date` older than `LL_PRICES_RETENTION_DAYS` (400) for `lightning_lane_single_prices`, and the implementer SHALL NOT shorten any existing retention of `park_schedule_signals` (verify its current retention first).

## Out of Scope

The following are deliberately deferred; none may be implemented without a spec amendment:

- Annual Pass prices (HTML-only source, four values, changes about yearly), Passholder, DVC and other discounted ticket prices, and single-day water park tickets (all `user_entered`).
- Prices from authorized resellers, discount-ticket sellers, military or special offers.
- Hotel and lodging pricing, parking, and merchandise catalogs (lodging, transportation and shopping lines are manual or generic).
- Sharing a Budget beyond the Trip's existing members, and any Budget export (Requirement 21.6).
- Any scheduled or cron-driven ticket refresh, and any admin-maintained price table.
- Payment, money movement, per-person "saved so far" tracking, and multi-currency.
- Fetching menus for restaurants that have no cached menu from inside the picker beyond the existing `GET /catalog/:id` lazy retrieval.

## Assumptions

- The ThemeParks.wiki schedule carries purchases only for the four theme parks today (the Blizzard Beach schedule has none); `price.amount` is in cents (verified on 2026-10-25).
- Disney endpoints T and P behave as documented in the design's External Interfaces on 2026-10-25 from a residential IP; behaviour from a Render egress IP is unverified, which is why the Kill_Switch defaults to false and the diagnose action exists.
- The 6.5% ticket tax is observed; the rates for food, Lightning Lane and shopping, and the dining tip rate, are assumptions and are configurable.
- The values of the upstream `serviceStyle` field beyond `Buffet` are unverified; Requirement 9.7 names the constant `TIP_BEARING_SERVICE_STYLES` to correct once the distinct values are queried.
- **[A]** Whether Disney validates `startDate >= today` against the UTC date or the New York date is unverified (Requirement 11.2); the 400 retry of Requirement 11.13 and the after-20:00-ET Diagnose run exist because of it.
- **[A]** The Single Pass `purchase_id` numeric suffix `N` maps to the Experience whose `upstream_entity_id` is exactly `N;entityType=Attraction` (verified for Seven Dwarfs Mine Train and TRON only; the type-qualified match of Requirement 15.6 is used because the bare numeric prefix is shared across entity types); unmatched ids store `experience_id` NULL.
- **[A]** The production Sampling_Job is scheduled (`POST /internal/sampling/run` is called by the external cron) and `park_schedule_signals.ll_prices_captured_at` will advance; locally `park_schedule_signals` was empty when investigated and production was not checked. Without it every Lightning Lane price is `add_your_price` (Requirement 20.7, operator launch gate).
- **[A]** `TICKET_PRICES_SANITY_MIN_RATIO` (0.5) and `TICKET_PRICES_MAX_UNRECOGNIZED_FRACTION` (0.1) are starting values taken from the investigation report, not measured thresholds; they are configurable and compared per window day (Requirement 11.9).
- The canonical value of `trips.day_touring_hours[date].startingPark` is assumed to equal an `experiences.park` string; the implementer must verify.
