# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The Photo Uploads feature lets people attach photos to two things in the app: **food photos** on a food-item log (and shown on the dish / restaurant pages), and **trip photos** (a Trip album, plus photos posted in the Trip feed). Both share one pipeline. A client resizes the photo, asks the API for a presigned URL, uploads the bytes straight to object storage, and then confirms. The API never carries image bytes. Every photo is checked by an automated harm check before anyone else can see it, and food photos also pass a vision "food screen". Photos can be reported, uploaders can be blocked, and an operator can review and remove content.

The pipeline is **generic**. A Photo has a polymorphic target (`target_kind` + `target_id`) so more attach points can be added later without rework. Only `food_log` and `trip` are implemented in this spec.

**Why this exists now and how it fits the hosting reality.** The app runs on free tiers (see `docs/hosting.md`): Render sleeps after ~15 minutes idle and has 512 MB RAM, Neon is ~1 GB, Upstash is ~500k commands per month, and there is no always-on worker. So the design uploads directly to Cloudflare R2, runs moderation asynchronously in-process after a fast `202`, and drives housekeeping from the external cron (cron-job.org) exactly like the Trip booking reminders. Photo bytes never go into Postgres.

**Visibility model (decided with the user).**

- Food photos are public to every signed-in user on the dish and restaurant pages. There is no private state and no share toggle for food photos. A public food photo must never leak the private `note` (or any other field) of the food log it is attached to.
- Trip photos are visible only to Trip members, inside the app. The Trip summary (Trip Recap) screen (member-only) gets a photos section with a highlights strip and a link to the Album (Requirement 26). A member can also send the recap to Friends through the in-app Sharing service; the Friend opens a read-only recap that includes the recap highlight photos drawn from every crew member's approved photos (there is no per-photo opt-out, D23), for as long as the two are still Friends (Requirement 15, decisions D1 and D25). The recipient can also open a read-only album of all approved Trip photos behind the same gate (decision D35). Today's Trip summary "Share" button is a plain-text OS share that cannot carry photos, and it stays exactly as it is. There are no links and no public URLs.
- A public food photo does not name its uploader to strangers: the name and avatar are shown only to the uploader's Friends (D8). Report and block work from the photo id alone.
- Until all required checks pass, a photo is visible only to its uploader.

**Dependencies.** This feature depends on `trips` (permissions matrix, feed, membership), `food-item-logging` (food logs), `friend-food-ratings` (the no-leak rules it reuses), `admin-panel` (the review queue extends it), and the existing Sharing service (a new `tripSummary` share kind). See the "Relationship to other specs" table in `design.md`.

**Phasing.** Phase 1 is the shared pipeline plus food photos. Phase 2 is trip photos (album, feed posts, the recap photos section, and the friend-gated recap share of Requirement 15). Phase 3 (other attach points) is future work and out of scope. Reporting, blocking, and the admin queue ship in Phase 1 because food photos are public from day one. The read-only "Photos from guests" strip on the experience page (Requirement 27, decision D24) is also Phase 1: it reads the restaurant-wide food photo list that Requirement 11.2 already provides and adds no upload path.

**Not changed by this spec.** `trip-expenses` keeps its rule that the receipt photo used for OCR is never uploaded or stored. The receipt scanner is not an attach point.

**My Photos (added later; Requirement 29, decision D36; additive).** A user's own photos are scattered across food logs, the dish and restaurant galleries, the Trip Album, and the Trip Activity feed, and the only own-photo read today (`GET /me/photos`, Requirement 4.11) is scoped to one target. Requirement 29 adds one read route, `GET /me/photos/all`, and one screen, the My_Photos_Screen, in the Vault tab (`CollectionStack`), that lists every photo the signed-in user owns, newest first, in a thumbnail grid with the existing status chip, the existing full-screen viewer, and the existing delete. It is a plain paginated read of rows that already exist: no new table, column, index, migration, config variable, or worker. It depends on Phase 1 of this spec (own-photo reads, status chip, viewer, delete, status polling) and, for what an owner may see once their account is deleted, on `account-deletion` (Requirement 28). It changes no existing requirement.

## Assumptions and Decision Points

The following product decisions are written into the requirements below as decided behavior. Each is an explicit decision point the user can veto.

- **D1 (trip summary photos) - DECIDED by the owner: option B, an in-app share to Friends.** What exists today (verified in code): the Trip summary "Share" button (`TripSummaryScreen` `handleShare`) calls React Native `Share.share` with plain text built by `formatSummaryShareText`; there is no link, no token, no recipient list, and nothing server-side. `GET /trips/:id/summary` is Trip-member-only, and members already see the recap in the app. The in-app friend Sharing service (`Sharing_Service`) has no trip payload today. So the OS text share cannot carry photos, and a person who receives it has no app access to the Trip's photos. **Decision:** a Trip member can send a Trip recap to Friends through the existing Sharing_Service, as a new share payload kind modeled on the existing `pinShowcase` pointer payload (Requirement 15). The OS `Share.share` plain-text action stays exactly as it is. There are no links, no public or tokenized URL, no deep-link, domain, or fallback-page work, and no option C. The recap gains a photos section (a highlights strip plus "View all in album", Requirement 26); the recipient opens a read-only recap in the app from their inbox. Options considered, kept for the record:
  - **A (members-only recap photos, with an optional deep link): superseded.** Members-only photos in the recap remain true for members (Requirement 26) and are the baseline. A deep link into the recap was considered and is **not built**: it needs app linking configuration and, for recipients without the app, a domain and a fallback page, and it is **unverified** whether linking is configured (a search of `apps/mobile/app.config.ts` and `apps/mobile/src/navigation/*.tsx` for a URL scheme, `linking` prop, associated domains, or intent filters found none; nothing else was checked).
  - **B (CHOSEN): friend-gated pointer share.** A new share payload kind, `tripSummary`, sent only by a Trip member to Friends (the recipient must be a Friend of the sender, through the existing atomic recipient check), modeled on the existing `pinShowcase` pointer payload: the payload is a **pointer** (trip id, trip name, sharer display name, and the `includePhotos` flag; the sharer's id is read from `shares.sender_id`, D33) and **not a snapshot**; the recap and its photos are read live through a friend-gated route keyed on the share row (the gate re-checks on every read that the recipient is still a Friend of the sharer, D25), with presigned GET URLs minted per request after the gate passes. It needs a migration widening `shares_payload_kind_chk` and `shares_experience_payload_chk` as `0038` did (part of migration `0063`). There is **no per-photo opt-out flag**: all Trip photos are treated the same, so a recap share shows the recap highlights (Requirement 26.1) drawn from every approved Trip photo of every crew member. **Accepted trade-off (owner decision): any member who shares a recap exposes crew members' photos to the sender's Friends, and the other members do not get a say.** The uploader can still delete their own photo at any time, which removes it everywhere (one lifecycle, Requirement 1.6). The inbox card opens a read-only recap. Requirement 15 carries the acceptance criteria and is built in Phase 2.
  - **C (REJECTED by the owner for this release): tokenized public link.** No link, public URL, token-in-URL route, or unauthenticated route is created (Requirement 15.7, Requirement 26.4). It would need its own requirement and a decision that moderation is mature enough; none is written.
  - **D (not chosen): OS share of an individual photo.** The copy leaves the app: it cannot be revoked and escapes moderation and block.
  Decided details inside option B: **D21** the highlight rule is a pure function (one photo per Trip day, the earliest photo of that New York day by capture time `sort_at`, chronological, capped at 6; the day field was revised from upload day to `sort_at` by D29); **D23** there is no per-photo opt-out flag (all Trip photos are treated alike). Still open inside option B: **D22** whether a sender can revoke a share (recommended: out of scope, because the existing Sharing service has no sender revocation).
- **D2 (feed posts).** The Trip feed is system-generated today. A member "post" is a net-new feed item type `member_post` carrying 1 to 4 photos and an optional caption (Requirement 14).
- **D3 (album).** The album is a view over every Trip photo (feed-attached, day-attached, or standalone). One photo has one owner and one lifecycle. Deleting a feed post deletes its photos.
- **D4 (harm check on trip photos).** The harm check runs on every upload, including trip photos. Trip photos are not given the food/people/text screen.
- **D5 (URL lifetime).** Read URLs are presigned GET URLs valid for 1 hour (`PHOTOS_GET_URL_TTL_SECONDS=3600`).
- **D6 (thumbnails).** The client uploads a 320 px thumbnail as a second object so the server never processes images. The harm check runs on both objects.
- **D7 (blocking).** A minimal block feature is included. A block hides photos between the two users on every photo surface. It does not unfriend and does not affect other features (a broader block is a recommended follow-up).
- **D8 (byline, revised).** The photo is public; the person's name is not. A public food photo shows the uploader's display name and avatar preset (both read from `profiles`, not `users`) **only to the uploader's Friends** (the same Friend relation `friend-food-ratings` uses: an accepted `friendships` row). Every other signed-in user sees the photo with no name, no avatar, and no user id, and the photo carries only the park-calendar day of upload (D18). Reporting and blocking do not need a name: report is keyed by the photo id, and blocking accepts a photo id so the server resolves the uploader (Requirement 17.1). This keeps the posture of `friend-food-ratings` (R3, friend-gated: strangers get nothing; and R5, private fields are never exposed), which deliberately hides "named person X ate dish Y" from strangers; the user's decision that food photos need not be private covers the image, not the identity. Veto option: show the named byline to every signed-in user. That would be a product choice (not a requirement of reporting or blocking) and would differ from `friend-food-ratings`; it would need the owner's explicit confirmation before task 7.1 starts. Trip photos always carry the uploader byline, because only Trip members, who already know each other, can see them.
- **D9 (food photo needs a log).** A food photo is always attached to one of the uploader's own food logs.
- **D10 (departed members).** Photos uploaded by a member who later leaves or is removed from a Trip stay in the album. The organizer can delete them.
- **D11 (trip auto-hide).** Trip photos auto-hide at 1 report (the audience is a small known crew); food photos auto-hide at 3.
- **D12 (reads need a session).** The food photo read routes require a signed-in session. The existing public `GET /experiences/:id/food-items` stays public and unchanged.
- **D13 (rejected retention).** A rejected photo stays visible to its uploader with the reason for 24 hours, then it is deleted.
- **D14 (upload key versus serving key).** The client uploads to an upload key. Moderation reads that exact object version. Only on approval does the server copy it, bound to the moderated ETag, to a serving key that no presigned PUT URL ever covers. Nothing is served from the upload key to anyone but its uploader. This closes the "overwrite after approval" hole that a reusable presigned PUT URL would otherwise leave (Requirements 3.8, 4.9, 8.10, 8.11, 9.9).
- **D15 (EXIF policy).** The server scans every uploaded object for an Exif segment. The default is to log and count it (`PHOTOS_EXIF_POLICY=log`), not reject, because the client strips metadata and a false reject would be hostile. The operator can flip it to `reject` after the real-device check in Requirement 2.10 (Requirements 2.7, 2.8).
- **D16 (summary-share notice).** Because any member's recap share can show every crew member's photos (D1, D23), the Album shows a one-time notice to every member saying so, and the share composer states that the recap includes photos from all crew members (Requirement 15.10). The only control a member has over their own photos is deleting them.
- **D17 (admin approve of needs_review).** An operator approval of a `needs_review` photo records the pass verdicts and an operator-override marker, so the rule "approved implies pass verdicts" still holds literally (Requirement 8.12).
- **D18 (public timestamp precision).** The public food photo shows the park-calendar day of upload, not a second-level timestamp, so even a Friend who sees the name does not learn when someone was at a restaurant beyond the day (Requirement 10.5).
- **D19 (who may delete others' trip photos) - DECISION POINT.** Recommended: the uploader deletes their own, and an organizer can delete any Trip photo (`delete_any_trip_photo`, Requirement 12.5). Alternative: uploader only, with members using Report. The recommendation exists because an organizer is accountable for the Trip and a departed member's (D10) photos stay in the Album, where the organizer is the only crew member who can remove them if the uploader does not. A departed member keeps the right to delete their own photos (decision D30: the route authorizes on `owner_id`, never on current membership). Choosing uploader-only drops `delete_any_trip_photo` from the permission matrix, Requirements 12.5 and 14.7 (organizer clause), and the organizer delete controls.
- **D21 (recap photo highlights rule) - DECIDED by the owner as a pure function; the day field was REVISED by D29.** The recap highlights are one photo per Trip day that has photos, chronological, capped at `PHOTOS_RECAP_HIGHLIGHT_MAX` (default 6), where a photo's day is the America/New_York calendar day (`wdwClock`) of its `sort_at` (the client-declared capture time when valid, otherwise the request time, Requirement 13.3), and the photo chosen for a day is the earliest approved photo of that day by `sort_at` (ties by id). The function is pure and has its own property test (Requirement 26.6, Property 38). **Revision (D29):** an earlier draft grouped by upload day (`created_at`). That is replaced, because a family that uploads the whole trip at the end would otherwise get one or two highlights, and because the Album groups by the same `sort_at` day (Requirement 13.6). The owner did not pick upload day deliberately (when asked what the highlights should be, they said they did not know), so this is a default, not an overridden decision. One interpretation point stays an assumption, not a decision: when more than `PHOTOS_RECAP_HIGHLIGHT_MAX` days have photos the first `PHOTOS_RECAP_HIGHLIGHT_MAX` days in chronological order are kept (the alternative is the last ones).
- **D22 (sender revokes a share) - OPEN DECISION POINT.** Verified in code: the existing Sharing service (`apps/api/src/services/sharing/routes.ts`) offers `POST /me/shares`, `GET /me/inbox`, `POST /me/inbox/:shareId/open`, `DELETE /me/inbox/:shareId` (recipient soft delete), `GET /me/shares` (the sender's sent list), and the sender-gated reactions list; it has **no sender-side revoke or unsend**. Recommendation (owner's): out of scope for this release, so no revoke route, no `photo_access_revoked_at` column, and no "Stop sharing" control are built. Consequences to accept: once a Friend has a recap share, they can read it (and its photos, minted fresh per request) until they delete it from their inbox, the sender **unfriends them** (D25: the recap read re-checks the Friendship on every call), the sender leaves the Trip, or the Trip is deleted. A sender who wants to cut access can unfriend the recipient or ask the recipient to delete the share; there is still no per-share unsend. If the owner later wants revocation it is an additive change to the Sharing service (a new column and route) plus one more gate in Requirement 15.3.
- **D23 (no per-photo opt-out flag) - DECIDED by the owner.** There is no "keep out of the shared recap" flag, column, route, or control. All Trip photos are treated the same. An uploader's only control is deleting their own photo (anytime, removes it everywhere). Accepted trade-off: any member sharing a recap exposes crew members' photos to the sender's Friends (D1, D16).
- **D20 (starting numbers) - DECISION POINT.** The face and text thresholds (`PHOTO_VISION_MAX_PROMINENT_FACES` 0, `PHOTO_VISION_MAX_TEXT_FRACTION` 0.25, `PHOTO_VISION_MIN_FOOD_CONFIDENCE` 0.6), the per-user and global caps (20 per day, 2000 per day, 4 per food log, 300 per Trip, 4 per feed post), the 1-hour read-URL lifetime (D5), and the auto-hide thresholds (D11) are starting values taken from a 7-photo planning test and cost reasoning, not measured. All are environment variables. Requirement 24 and task 12.2 re-measure the vision thresholds on a labeled set before launch.
- **D24 (experience-page guest photo strip) - DECIDED by the owner.** Requirement 11.5 places food photo strips and a gallery only on each dish row in `MenuScreen`; Requirement 11.2 already gives the server restaurant-wide lists, but no requirement put that data on a screen. Decision: add a read-only "Photos from guests" strip on the experience page (`ExperienceDetail`) for food experiences (Requirement 27), showing the newest `PHOTOS_EXPERIENCE_STRIP_MAX` (default 8) restaurant photos with the dish name as a text label. It has no empty state and no add control: adding a photo stays per dish on the menu, which avoids a dish-picker flow. Photos of rides and attractions themselves are not part of this decision and remain Phase 3. Verified in code (read-only): there is no user-submitted-location detail screen in `apps/mobile` (locations appear only inside sheets and lists), so the strip is not applicable to locations today (Requirement 27.14). Assumptions the owner did not spell out: the strip lives in the Today lens only (the experience page has two lenses); "restaurants, bars, lounges, and quick-service" are all category `Restaurant` in the catalog (the `ExperienceCategory` enum has no bar or lounge category; this was verified in the enum and in `gating.ts`, not against catalog data); the shared photo viewer keeps its own-photo Delete (Requirement 23.3), which does not make the strip itself editable.

The following decisions came from the final independent review (`.agents/tasks/photo-uploads/final-review.md`). Each is a default the owner approved to write into the spec; the "Veto" sentence says what changes if the owner overrules it. M1, M5, M8, and M9 (D26, D29, D30, D31) are defaults the owner has **not** explicitly confirmed one by one.

- **D25 (recap read re-checks the Friendship) - default, closes review finding H1.** The existing Sharing service checks the Friendship only when a share is created; `removeFriend` does not touch `shares` or `share_recipients`, and the Inbox only checks `stillFriend` on the client. A pointer share that reads live data would therefore keep serving a crew's photos (with freshly signed URLs) to someone the sharer later unfriended. Decision: `GET /me/inbox/:shareId/trip-recap` and every recap-photo access (including report and block with a `shareId`) re-check, on every call, that the recipient is currently a Friend of the sharer (one `friendships` primary-key lookup through `canonicalPair`, the sharer being `shares.sender_id`); failure answers the same collapsed `validation_failed` as a deleted share, with no figure and no URL (Requirements 15.3, 15.14). The mobile `InboxScreen` reuses its existing `stillFriend` pre-check for the tripSummary card (task 22.4). *Alternative:* trust the creation-time check and the client pre-check only. *Veto consequence:* an unfriended person keeps live access until they delete the share, the sharer leaves the Trip, or the Trip is deleted; Requirements 15.3 and 15.14, Property 21's Friend dimension, and the unfriend-after-send test of task 21.3 are dropped.
- **D26 (recap superlatives carry the display name, never an id) - default, closes M1.** `TripSuperlativeDTO` has `memberId` and `memberDisplayName`; the OS text share already prints `memberDisplayName` ("Group MVP: <name>"). Decision: `TripRecapDTO.superlatives` is `ReadonlyArray<{ id, title, description, icon, experienceName?, value?, memberDisplayName? }>`, never `memberId` or any user id, and `memberDisplayName` is allowed because the text share already exposes it, so a recipient learns no more than the text share shows (Requirement 15.16). The recap carries no per-member contribution counts or ratings. *Alternative:* drop `memberDisplayName` and show only titles and descriptions. *Veto consequence:* the projection allowlist loses `memberDisplayName`; the sentinel test then asserts the display name is absent everywhere.
- **D27 (a recap whose Trip is gone or whose sharer left is unavailable, not stale) - default, closes M2 and N3.** Decision: when the Trip no longer exists or the sharer is no longer a member, the route answers `200` with `recap: null` and `photos.access: 'unavailable'`, and the card shows "This recap is no longer available" (Requirement 15.15); no figure and no URL is returned. `unavailable` wins over `includePhotos = false`: once access is gone the sender's photo choice no longer matters. *Alternative:* answer the collapsed `validation_failed`. *Veto consequence:* the card cannot distinguish "gone" from "deleted by me"; `TripRecapDTO.recap` becomes non-nullable and the route throws instead.
- **D28 (the recap route survives the photo kill switch) - default, closes M3.** Decision: the recap route is registered by the Sharing side, not by the photo plugin, and works when `PHOTOS_ENABLED=false`, returning the figures with `photos.access: 'unavailable'` (Requirements 15.17, 20.9). It is the one exception to Requirement 20.2. When the `FeedPhotoPort` is absent the trips feed route drops `photoIds` and returns `member_post` items with an empty `photos` array (Requirement 14.14). *Alternative:* let the route disappear with the kill switch. *Veto consequence:* a user could send a recap card the recipient cannot open while photos are off.
- **D29 (the highlight rule groups by `sort_at`, not by upload day) - default, closes M5.** See D21. *Alternative:* group by `created_at` (upload day). *Veto consequence:* Requirements 13.7, 13.8, 15.5, and 26.6, Property 38, and tasks 5.5, 19.4, and 21.3 revert to `createdAt`, and a trip uploaded in one sitting yields one highlight.
- **D30 (an uploader may always delete their own Trip photo) - default, closes M8.** The route authorizes on `owner_id`, never on current Trip membership, so a member who left or was removed can still delete their own photos; reading the Album, uploading, and posting still answer `trip_forbidden` for them (Requirements 12.5, 12.6, 12.11). This is the only control a departed uploader keeps over photos that stay in the Album and in recap shares (D10, D23). *Alternative:* deny deletion once the uploader is no longer a member. *Veto consequence:* a departed member has no control at all over their photos; D19's rationale and Requirement 12.11 are reversed and the organizer becomes the only remover.
- **D31 (only current Trip members' reports count toward auto-hide on Trip photos) - default, closes M9.** With the Trip threshold at 1 and recap recipients allowed to report (Requirement 16.8), any Friend of any member could hide a crew member's photo from the whole Trip with one report. Decision: for a Trip photo, a report counts toward the auto-hide threshold only when the reporter is a current member of that Trip; a report that arrives with a `shareId` is recorded as a Photo_Report and appears in the admin queue (Requirement 18.1) but does not by itself hide the photo (Requirement 16.9). *Alternative:* a separate higher threshold for recipient reports. *Veto consequence:* Requirements 16.4 and 16.9 and Property 13 revert to counting every distinct reporter, and the griefing path returns.
- **D32 (reportable through a share only if it is a current highlight; widened by D35) - default, closes M10.** The stricter rule is already what the design and tests describe and prevents enumerating photo ids through a share (Requirements 16.8, 15.5). *Alternative:* any approved Trip photo is reportable with a valid `shareId`. *Veto consequence:* photo ids of non-highlights become probeable by a recipient. **Widened by D35 (2026-10-19):** with the recipient album a recipient can open any approved Trip photo of the share's Trip, so a report or a photo-id block with a valid `shareId` is accepted for any such photo, not only a current highlight (Requirement 16.10); the probing concern is met by the same gate (current Friendship, live share, `includePhotos`, sharer still a member, the photo belongs to that share's Trip, `approved`, not blocked) and by the album itself already exposing those ids to that recipient. *Veto consequence of the widening:* see D35.
- **D33 (the recap's sharer is `shares.sender_id`) - default, closes N5.** One source of truth: the route and the gate read the sharer from `shares.sender_id`; the payload carries no `sharerId` (nothing reads it; the inbox DTO already has `senderId`) (Requirement 15.20). *Alternative:* keep `sharerId` in the payload and assert it equals `sender_id`. *Veto consequence:* the payload has six keys again and a test asserts equality.
- **D34 (recap aggregate totals, parks count and catalog fields of top-rated rows) - DECIDED by the owner (2026-10-19).** The shared recap screen was bare next to the owner's own Trip Summary, so the owner approved two things: (1) the recap allowlist gains the Trip-wide aggregates `totalCompletionsCount`, `totalRatingsCount` and `parksExploredCount` (the number of distinct parks in the summary's `parkBreakdown`), and each top-rated row gains the public catalog fields `park`, `category` and `imageUrl` (catalog data about the Experience, not user data), present only when the Experience has them; (2) the recap screen reuses the Trip Summary's presentational components (hero card with progress ring, stat tiles, award card, top-rated row) (Requirements 15.21 to 15.24). **Still forbidden, unchanged:** `memberId` or any user id, `actor_id`, `perMember` and anything derived from it (`logEntryCount`, `confirmedTagCount`, `totalCompletedCount`, `topRatedExperienceName`, `topRating`, member avatar presets), per-member ratings or counts, `experienceId`, and a Member Contributions section. Superlatives keep the D26 projection (a display name is allowed, never an id). Accepted trade-off: a Friend who is not on the Trip now also learns the Trip-wide totals and park count, which the plain-text OS share does not print. *Alternative:* keep the minimal five-key payload and the plain recap screen. *Veto consequence:* Requirements 15.21 to 15.24 and Property 43 are dropped, the allowlist and Property 40 revert to the five-key and three-key lists, `TripRecapScreen` returns to the plain text lines, and task 22.7 is reverted.
- **D35 (recipient photo album) - DECIDED by the owner, option B (2026-10-19).** A shared recap showed at most `PHOTOS_RECAP_HIGHLIGHT_MAX` highlight photos (one per New York day), so a Friend who opened a recap of a trip with many photos could see only a handful of them. Decision: the recipient of a `tripSummary` Share gets a read-only, paginated "View all photos" album of **every `approved` Trip photo** of the share's Trip, through the same share and the same gates as the recap (a non-deleted recipient row, a current Friendship re-checked on every call, `includePhotos` true, the sharer still a Trip member, the Trip existing; Requirements 15.25 to 15.30), excluding uploaders blocked either way with the recipient. Each item is exactly the six `SharedSummaryPhotoDTO` fields; it names no uploader. Report and "Block uploader" from the album work through the `shareId` for any approved Trip photo visible through the share (Requirement 16.10), which widens decision D32 (a share-reported photo is still recorded and queued for the admin but never counts toward auto-hide, D31). Accepted trade-off: a Friend of any member who shares a recap can now see every approved crew photo, not only the highlights, and the crew gets no per-photo say (D23 and D16 already accept this exposure; D35 widens how much of it is visible). The only control an uploader has is still deleting their own photo. *Alternative:* keep the highlights-only recap. *Veto consequence:* remove the route `GET /me/inbox/:shareId/trip-recap/photos`, the `TripRecapAlbumScreen` and the "View all photos" action, the `RecapPhotoPort` album methods and the `TripRecapPhotosDTO`/`tripRecapPhotosQuerySchema`; revert the widened report/block eligibility of Requirement 16.10 and 16.8 to "a current highlight" (D32); drop Requirements 15.25 to 15.33 and 16.10, Properties 44 and 45, and tasks 21.6 and 22.8; the recap goes back to highlights only.
- **D36 (My Photos: one screen for everything the user uploaded) - DEFAULT, written as decided behavior; the owner can veto each point below.** Verified in code (read-only): the only own-photo read is `GET /me/photos?targetKind=&scopeId=&cursor=` (`myPhotosQuerySchema` requires both `targetKind` and `scopeId`; an existing route test pins that omitting `scopeId` is `validation_failed`), consumed by `useMyPhotos(targetKind, scopeId)` in `FoodLogPhotos`; there is no list of all of a user's photos. Decisions:
  - **Where it lives.** The Vault tab: the bottom tab whose navigator name is `Collection` and whose tab bar label is "Vault" (`MainTabs` in `apps/mobile/src/navigation/RootNavigator.tsx`), whose stack is `CollectionStack` (`apps/mobile/src/navigation/CollectionStack.tsx`, params type `CollectionStackParamList`) with the Vault main screen registered as `CollectionHome` (`CollectionScreen`, `apps/mobile/src/screens/collection/CollectionScreen.tsx`). The My_Photos_Screen is a NEW route `MyPhotos` in that stack, reached from an entry control in the `CollectionScreen` header. No new bottom tab (the tab set is fixed by the repo conventions), and nothing in `ProfileScreen.tsx` (its default export is not a registered route). *Alternatives rejected:* a fifth Vault segment (the segmented control already holds four, and the segments are content views of the Vault, not photo management); a button inside the Food segment only (trip photos are not food); the You & Crew screen (it is identity and account settings). *Veto consequence:* the entry control moves; the route name and the API are unchanged.
  - **Endpoint.** A NEW route `GET /me/photos/all`, not an extension of `GET /me/photos`. Making `targetKind` and `scopeId` optional would change a strict schema that an existing test pins and would give one route two meanings; a sibling route leaves `GET /me/photos` and its tests untouched. *Alternative rejected:* optional parameters on the existing route. *Veto consequence:* make both parameters optional in `myPhotosQuerySchema` and update the pinned validation test.
  - **What an owner may see of their own photos: the spec is NOT silent, and this follows it exactly.** Requirement 4.11 returns the owner's non-`deleted` Photos "with status and, for a `rejected` Photo, its reason"; Requirement 8.9 and D13 keep a `rejected` Photo visible to its owner with its reason for 24 hours and then delete it; Requirement 23.2 shows `hidden` as "under review"; Requirement 9.9 mints no URL for a `rejected` Photo and an owner-only thumbnail URL for `uploading`, `pending`, and `needs_review`. The My_Photos list is exactly that set. A photo an operator removed is `deleted` and simply disappears, as it does from every other owner view today; no notice is added. *Veto consequence:* none to the API; hiding `rejected` or `hidden` from the grid would be a client filter and a new requirement.
  - **Independent of current Trip membership (default).** The list reads `owner_id` only, exactly as `GET /me/photos` does, so a member who left a Trip still sees their own Trip photos here and can delete them, which is the only control they keep over photos that stay in the Album (D10, D30, Requirement 12.11). This owner-scoped list is a deliberate exception to the read rule of Requirement 12.6, which concerns the shared Album and other members' photos, not an owner's own photos. *Alternative:* list only photos of Trips the user is still a member of. *Veto consequence:* a departed member could no longer find their photos in the app to delete them, which reverses D30; the repo query would join `trip_memberships`.
  - **Blocks and account deletion.** A block hides each user's photos from the other user (Requirement 17.3); it never hides a photo from its own owner, so the list has no block filter. An account in `pending_deletion` has no session (`account-deletion`), so it cannot call the route, and a purge sets the user's Photos to `deleted` (Requirement 28.1), which the list excludes.
  - **Polling.** The screen polls only photos whose status is `pending`: the pipeline is the only thing that changes a `pending` photo, whereas `needs_review` and `hidden` change only by operator action and `uploading` only by this device's own confirm. This keeps a screen that holds many `hidden` photos from polling forever (the existing `isFinalPhotoStatus` treats those two as non-final). The other states refresh when the list refetches (Requirement 29.17).
  - **Pagination and `created_at` ties (verified in code, read-only).** `PhotoService.initiate` reads `this.clock.now()` once per call and passes it as `createdAt` to the only `INSERT INTO photos` (`insertUploading` in `photos/repo.ts`), so every stored `created_at` is a whole millisecond and a batch of up to 10 photos shares one value. Rows therefore tie on `created_at`, and the `(created_at, id)` tiebreak handles them: a millisecond `Date` round-trips exactly through the driver, so the raw keyset comparison used by `listMine` and the galleries is exact. The SQL `now()` fallback in that statement is the only source of sub-millisecond values and no production path uses it. The new route therefore mirrors `listMine` (raw `created_at DESC, id DESC`, cursor from the last row's `created_at` and `id`, which also keeps the `photos_owner_idx` ordering) and no existing read is changed or in need of a follow-up. The tie-heavy tests of tasks 25.2 and 25.4 stay, to guard the tiebreak.

## Glossary

- **Photo**: A user-uploaded JPEG (plus a thumbnail) stored in object storage, with a metadata row in the `photos` table.
- **Photo_Service**: The API service in `apps/api/src/services/photos/` that owns the Photo lifecycle.
- **Photo_Target**: The thing a Photo is attached to, identified by `target_kind` and `target_id`. Implemented kinds: `food_log`, `trip`.
- **Target_Adapter**: A per-kind module that authorizes upload and view, resolves the Photo's listing scope, and reports whether the target still exists.
- **Scope**: The id a Photo is listed under (`scope_id`): the `food_item_id` for a `food_log` Photo, the `trip_id` for a `trip` Photo.
- **Upload_Key**: The object key (`u/<photoId>/full.jpg` or `u/<photoId>/thumb.jpg`) the client uploads to with a presigned PUT URL. Moderation reads it. It is never served to anyone but the uploader.
- **Serving_Key**: The object key (`p/<photoId>/full.jpg` or `p/<photoId>/thumb.jpg`) the server copies a moderated object to on approval. Only the server writes it, and all viewer reads use it.
- **Object_Store**: The port for object storage. The adapter targets Cloudflare R2 through the AWS S3 SDK.
- **Harm_Moderator**: The port for the harm check. The adapter calls the OpenAI Moderation API.
- **Vision_Checker**: The port for the food screen. The adapter calls the Gemini API.
- **Vision_Fields**: The structured JSON the Vision_Checker returns (description, `is_food_or_drink`, `food_confidence`, `prominent_face_count`, `minor_face_visible`, `any_person_part_visible`, `large_text`, `text_fraction`, `text_seen`).
- **Vision_Verdict**: The accept/reject decision computed in our code from Vision_Fields. The model's own verdict is never used.
- **Moderation_Pipeline**: The in-process asynchronous job that runs the harm check and, for food photos, the food screen, and then sets the Photo status.
- **Photo_Status**: One of `uploading`, `pending`, `approved`, `rejected`, `needs_review`, `hidden`, `deleted`.
- **Photo_Sweep**: The housekeeping pass driven by the Photo_Cron.
- **Photo_Cron**: The external cron-job.org job calling `POST` or `HEAD /internal/photos/sweep/run` with the `x-cron-secret` header.
- **Album**: The Trip photo view: all non-deleted Trip-target Photos the viewer may see, ordered by `sort_at` descending.
- **Trip_Post**: A Trip feed item of type `member_post` carrying photos and an optional caption.
- **Summary_Share**: An in-app Share of kind `tripSummary` sent by a Trip member to Friends. Its payload is a pointer to the Trip; the recap and its photos are read live through a friend-gated route keyed on the share row, which re-checks the Friendship on every read (decisions D1 and D25).
- **TripShareFacts**: The structural port through which the Sharing service reads Trip role, Trip name, membership, and the Trip summary without importing the trips repo or the database pool (Requirement 15.18).
- **Summary_Strip**: The highlights strip in the photos section of the Trip Recap (`TripSummaryScreen`, and the recipient's read-only recap), computed by the pure `selectRecapHighlights` function of decisions D21 and D29: one photo per America/New_York day of `sort_at`, chronological, capped at `PHOTOS_RECAP_HIGHLIGHT_MAX`.
- **Photo_Report**: A user report against a Photo.
- **Block**: A row in `user_blocks` where one user blocks another.
- **Admin_Panel**: The existing HTTP-Basic server-rendered `/admin` UI.
- **Upheld_Count**: The number of Photos of one uploader that the operator has removed, stored in `photo_upload_strikes` independently of the Photo rows so that it survives the Photo_Sweep.
- **Mobile_App**: The React Native + Expo client in `apps/mobile`.
- **Trip_Role**: `organizer` or `member`, per `apps/api/src/services/trips/permissions.ts`.
- **Friend**: An accepted `friendships` row between two users, as used by `assertOwnerOrFriend` and `friend-food-ratings`.
- **Upload_Prefix_Expiry**: The age, in days (`PHOTOS_UPLOAD_PREFIX_EXPIRY_DAYS`, default 7), after which the bucket lifecycle rule deletes any object under the `u/` prefix.
- **Experience_Photo_Strip**: The read-only "Photos from guests" strip on the experience page (`ExperienceDetail`, Today lens, dining section) showing the newest `PHOTOS_EXPERIENCE_STRIP_MAX` restaurant-wide food photos, each with a dish-name label (Requirement 27).
- **Restaurant_Photos_Sheet**: The sheet opened by "See all" on the Experience_Photo_Strip: the restaurant-wide food photo gallery, paginated with the keyset cursor (Requirement 27.4).
- **Restaurant_Food_Photo**: An item of the restaurant-wide gallery read (`GET /experiences/:id/food-photos`, `GET /locations/:id/food-photos`): a `FoodPhotoDTO` plus the additive `foodItemName` (Requirement 27.11). The dish gallery keeps the plain `FoodPhotoDTO`.
- **Recap_Album**: The read-only, paginated list of every approved Trip photo that a Summary_Share recipient can open from the recap through `GET /me/inbox/:shareId/trip-recap/photos` (decision D35, Requirements 15.25 to 15.30). It is separate from the Summary_Strip (at most `PHOTOS_RECAP_HIGHLIGHT_MAX` photos).
- **My_Photos_Screen**: The mobile screen (`MyPhotosScreen`, route `MyPhotos` in `CollectionStack`, reached from the Vault main screen) that lists every Photo the signed-in user owns, newest first, in a thumbnail grid (Requirement 29, decision D36).
- **My_Photos_Page**: One page of the `GET /me/photos/all` read: a `PhotoPageDTO<MyPhotoDTO>` of the caller's own non-`deleted` Photos across every target kind, with an opaque keyset cursor (Requirement 29.3 to 29.9).

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Generic Photo model and attach points

**User Story:** As a developer, I want one photo pipeline with a polymorphic target, so that new attach points can be added later without reworking storage, moderation, or cleanup.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Photo_Service SHALL store each Photo as one `photos` row identified by a UUID, with `target_kind`, `target_id`, `scope_id`, `owner_id`, `status`, an object key for the full image, and an object key for the thumbnail.
2. THE Photo_Service SHALL accept exactly two `target_kind` values in this release: `food_log` and `trip`, and SHALL reject any other value with `validation_failed`.
3. THE Photo_Service SHALL authorize upload and view through a Target_Adapter per kind, so that the pipeline, moderation, reporting, and sweep code contain no per-kind authorization.
4. THE Photo_Service SHALL keep provider names (Cloudflare, R2, OpenAI, Gemini, Google) out of domain modules, and SHALL reference them only in adapters and in environment configuration.
5. THE Photo_Service SHALL NOT store image bytes in Postgres.
6. THE Photo_Service SHALL give every Photo exactly one owner and one lifecycle, such that deleting a Photo anywhere makes it unavailable everywhere it was shown.
7. THE Photo_Service SHALL store all timestamps as UTC `TIMESTAMPTZ`.

### Requirement 2: Client image preparation

**User Story:** As a user, I want my photo shrunk and cleaned before it leaves my phone, so that uploads are fast, cheap, and do not carry my location.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user picks a photo, THE Mobile_App SHALL re-encode it as JPEG with the long edge at most 1280 px, quality 0.75, never upscaling and preserving aspect ratio.
2. WHEN a user picks a photo, THE Mobile_App SHALL also produce a JPEG thumbnail with the long edge at most 320 px.
3. THE Mobile_App SHALL produce its upload bytes by re-encoding through `expo-image-manipulator`, so that EXIF metadata including GPS coordinates is not present in the uploaded bytes.
4. THE Mobile_App SHALL compute the target dimensions in a pure function that is independent of the native module.
5. IF re-encoding fails, THEN THE Mobile_App SHALL show a retryable error and SHALL NOT start an upload.
6. THE Mobile_App SHALL show the prepared photo as a preview and require an explicit confirmation before the upload starts.
7. WHEN the Moderation_Pipeline reads a full or thumbnail object, THE Photo_Service SHALL scan its first `PHOTOS_EXIF_SCAN_BYTES` (default 65536) bytes for a JPEG APP1 segment that begins with `Exif\0\0`, in a pure module, and SHALL count each hit in `photo_usage_daily.exif_present` and log it at `warn` without image data.
8. WHERE `PHOTOS_EXIF_POLICY` is `reject`, THE Photo_Service SHALL reject a Photo whose full or thumbnail object contains an Exif segment with reason `invalid_image` without calling any provider; WHERE it is `log` (the default), THE Photo_Service SHALL NOT change the Photo's outcome because of an Exif segment.
9. WHEN a user picks a photo from the library, THE Mobile_App SHALL read the capture time from the picker asset's metadata (the EXIF `DateTimeOriginal`, requested through the picker's `exif` option) before re-encoding and SHALL send it as the optional `takenAt` field; WHEN the capture time is unavailable or the photo was taken with the in-app camera, THE Mobile_App SHALL omit `takenAt` or send the current time respectively. THE Mobile_App SHALL NOT send any other EXIF field, and SHALL NOT upload the original file.
10. THE project SHALL include a manual real-device verification task for iOS and Android that uploads a GPS-tagged photo through the app and inspects the stored Upload_Key object for an Exif segment, and SHALL NOT record a result that was not actually observed.

### Requirement 3: Upload initiation and direct-to-storage upload

**User Story:** As a user, I want to upload without the API handling my image bytes, so that uploads work even when the server is cold or small.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an authenticated user calls `POST /photos/uploads` with a target, and 1 to 10 items each declaring content type, byte size, thumbnail byte size, width, and height, THE Photo_Service SHALL authorize the target through its Target_Adapter, create one `uploading` Photo row per item, and return for each item the photo id, a presigned PUT URL for the full object, and a presigned PUT URL for the thumbnail object.
2. THE Photo_Service SHALL issue presigned PUT URLs that expire after 900 seconds and that pin the content type to `image/jpeg`.
3. THE Photo_Service SHALL generate object keys from the photo id only and SHALL NOT include any user-supplied text in a key.
4. IF an item declares a content type other than `image/jpeg`, a full byte size above the configured maximum, a thumbnail byte size above its maximum, a non-positive size, or dimensions outside 1 to 4096, THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL reject the request with `photo_upload_invalid` and SHALL create no rows.
5. THE Mobile_App SHALL upload the bytes with an HTTP `PUT` directly to the presigned URL, SHALL NOT send the API bearer token to the storage host, and SHALL NOT route image bytes through the API.
6. THE Photo_Service SHALL NOT accept image bytes in any API request body.
7. WHERE a request is made by a user who is in `photo_upload_bans`, THE Photo_Service SHALL reject it with `photo_uploads_banned`.
8. THE Photo_Service SHALL presign PUT URLs only for Upload_Keys (`u/<photoId>/full.jpg` and `u/<photoId>/thumb.jpg`) and SHALL NEVER presign a PUT URL for a Serving_Key, so that no client-held URL can write an object that is served to other users.
9. THE Mobile_App SHALL obtain the exact bytes it will send by reading each prepared image (full and thumbnail) into a `Blob` with `await (await fetch(fileUri)).blob()`, SHALL declare `byteSize` and `thumbByteSize` in the initiate request as the `size` of those two Blobs, and SHALL send those same two Blobs as the bodies of the two `PUT` requests, so that the declared sizes equal the sent sizes.
10. THE Mobile_App SHALL set only the `Content-Type: image/jpeg` header on each storage `PUT` and SHALL NOT set `Content-Length` by hand, because the HTTP stack derives it from the Blob body and the presigned URL signs the declared length (Requirement 3.2).
11. IF the storage SDK's presigner cannot sign `content-length` (the adapter test of task 6.2 observes this), THEN THE Object_Store adapter SHALL sign `content-type` only, THE confirm-time HEAD size check (Requirements 4.2 and 5.4) SHALL remain the authoritative size enforcement, and the wording of Requirement 3.10 and the design SHALL be amended to say the URL signs the content type only; correctness SHALL NOT depend on the storage service enforcing the signed length.

### Requirement 4: Confirmation and asynchronous moderation start

**User Story:** As a user, I want to confirm my upload and get an immediate answer, so that the app does not hang while checks run.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the owner calls `POST /photos/confirm` with 1 to 10 photo ids, THE Photo_Service SHALL verify each object with a storage HEAD request and respond `202` without waiting for moderation.
2. WHEN confirming a Photo, THE Photo_Service SHALL require that the full object and the thumbnail object both exist, that each has content type `image/jpeg`, and that each byte size is within its configured maximum.
3. IF an object is missing at confirm, THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL leave that Photo `uploading` and report the per-item result `incomplete` for it in the `202` body (no error code is thrown for a partial batch).
4. IF an object has the wrong content type or exceeds the maximum size at confirm, THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL move the Photo to `deleted` so the Sweep removes the object, and report the per-item result `invalid` for it in the `202` body.
5. WHEN confirm succeeds for a Photo, THE Photo_Service SHALL set the Photo to `pending` and enqueue it for the Moderation_Pipeline in the same process.
6. THE Photo_Service SHALL make confirm idempotent: confirming a Photo that is already past `uploading` returns its current state and starts no second Moderation_Pipeline run.
7. WHEN the owner calls `GET /photos/:id`, THE Photo_Service SHALL return the Photo's current status and, for a rejected Photo, the rejection reason code and message.
8. WHEN the owner reads a Photo that is `pending`, whose lease has expired, whose `next_attempt_at` has passed, and that has attempts remaining, THE Photo_Service SHALL re-enqueue it for the Moderation_Pipeline (opportunistic resume), so that a Render sleep mid-job does not wait for the next cron tick.
9. WHEN confirming a Photo, THE Photo_Service SHALL record the ETag of the full Upload_Key object and of the thumbnail Upload_Key object returned by the storage HEAD request on the Photo row (`full_etag`, `thumb_etag`).
10. THE Photo_Service SHALL provide `GET /me/photos/status?ids=<comma-separated photo ids, 1 to 10>` returning the owner's `MyPhotoDTO` for each id the caller owns, omitting ids the caller does not own, and SHALL apply the same opportunistic resume as Requirement 4.8 to each returned `pending` Photo, so that a client can poll many Photos with one request.
11. THE Photo_Service SHALL provide `GET /me/photos?targetKind=&scopeId=&cursor=` returning the caller's own non-`deleted` Photos for one target kind and scope (for example one Trip, or one food item), newest first, as `MyPhotoDTO` with status and, for a `rejected` Photo, its reason, using keyset pagination with an opaque cursor, so that the Mobile_App can show the owner's pending, rejected, and approved photos together after an app restart.
12. WHEN the Mobile_App loads the owner's photos (Requirement 4.11) and the result contains `uploading` Photos for which no upload is in progress on this device (for example after an app restart between the storage `PUT` and confirm), THE Mobile_App SHALL call confirm for them (idempotent, Requirement 4.6) and SHALL show the retry state for any item the response reports as `incomplete`, so that a restart does not strand a completed upload until its row expires.

### Requirement 5: Quotas, limits, and abuse controls

**User Story:** As the app owner, I want hard limits on uploads, so that cost and abuse stay bounded.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Photo_Service SHALL cap each user at 20 photos per park-calendar day (America/New_York, via `wdwClock`), counted per item of each initiate request (a request with 5 items consumes 5), configurable with `PHOTOS_DAILY_UPLOAD_CAP`, and SHALL reject a request that would exceed the cap with `photo_quota_exceeded`.
2. THE Photo_Service SHALL reserve the daily quota atomically with a single conditional upsert, so concurrent requests cannot exceed the cap.
3. THE Photo_Service SHALL cap a food log at 4 non-deleted Photos (`PHOTOS_MAX_PER_FOOD_LOG`) and a Trip at 300 non-deleted Photos (`PHOTOS_MAX_PER_TRIP`), and SHALL reject beyond the cap with `photo_target_full`. THE Photo_Service SHALL serialize initiate requests per target by taking a transaction-scoped advisory lock keyed on the target id before counting, so concurrent initiates cannot exceed the cap.
4. THE Photo_Service SHALL reject a full object larger than 1 MiB (`PHOTOS_MAX_BYTES`, default 1048576) and a thumbnail larger than 65536 bytes (`PHOTOS_MAX_THUMB_BYTES`), at request time on the declared size and at confirm time on the HEAD size.
5. THE Photo_Service SHALL allow only `image/jpeg`.
6. THE Photo_Service SHALL cap a single initiate or confirm request at 10 items (`PHOTOS_MAX_BATCH`).
7. WHEN a user deletes a Photo, THE Photo_Service SHALL NOT refund the daily quota.
8. THE Photo_Service SHALL expose `GET /photos/config` returning the effective kill-switch flags and limits plus the caller's remaining daily quota, so the Mobile_App can hide controls that cannot work.
9. WHERE an operator adds a user to `photo_upload_bans`, THE Photo_Service SHALL reject that user's new uploads with `photo_uploads_banned` while leaving the user's existing photos governed by the normal rules.
10. THE Photo_Service SHALL cap total uploads across all users at 2000 per park-calendar day (`PHOTOS_GLOBAL_DAILY_CAP`), counted per item in `photo_usage_daily.uploads` and reserved atomically in the same transaction and with the same conditional-update technique as the per-user cap (Requirement 5.2), and SHALL reject a request that would exceed it with `photo_uploads_disabled` and `details.reason` equal to `global_cap`, so that spend has an automatic ceiling even when many accounts each stay under their own cap. WHEN either cap leaves no remaining allowance, THE `GET /photos/config` `remainingToday` SHALL be 0 so the Mobile_App hides the add-photo controls.
11. THE Photo_Service SHALL return every `photo_uploads_disabled` error with `details.reason` set to `kill_switch` (when `PHOTOS_ENABLED`, `PHOTOS_FOOD_ENABLED`, or `PHOTOS_TRIP_ENABLED` is false for the request) or `global_cap` (Requirement 5.10), and THE Mobile_App SHALL show the message "Photo uploads are turned off right now" for `kill_switch` and "Photo uploads are full for today, try again tomorrow" for `global_cap`.
12. FOR the per-target cap of Requirement 5.3, THE Photo_Service SHALL count a Photo toward the target's non-deleted total in every status except `uploading` rows older than `PHOTOS_PUT_URL_TTL_SECONDS` (900 s, past which their PUT URLs are dead), so that abandoned initiates do not block a target for the full `PHOTOS_UPLOAD_EXPIRY_MINUTES`; a `rejected` Photo still counts until its owner deletes it or it is deleted at the end of its 24-hour retention (Requirement 8.9), and the Mobile_App SHALL offer Delete on a rejected Photo so the owner can free the slot immediately.

### Requirement 6: Harm check

**User Story:** As the app owner, I want every uploaded image checked for harmful content before anyone sees it, so that the app is not a channel for abuse.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Moderation_Pipeline processes a Photo, THE Photo_Service SHALL run the harm check on the full object and, unless `PHOTO_HARM_CHECK_THUMB` is false, on the thumbnail object, for every `target_kind`.
2. THE Photo_Service SHALL send the image to the Harm_Moderator as base64 image content and SHALL read only `flagged`, `categories`, and `category_scores` from the response.
3. THE Photo_Service SHALL compute the harm verdict in a pure module: a Photo is flagged when the provider's `flagged` is true or when any `category_scores` value is at or above `PHOTO_HARM_SCORE_THRESHOLD` (default 0.5).
4. IF the harm verdict is flagged, THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL set the Photo to `rejected` with reason `harm`, and SHALL NOT run the food screen.
5. IF the Harm_Moderator returns a transport error, a timeout, an HTTP 429, or an HTTP 5xx, THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL treat the outcome as retryable and SHALL NOT approve or reject the Photo.
6. IF the Harm_Moderator returns HTTP 400 and the error body matches the recorded allow-list of image-decoding errors (`PHOTO_HARM_INVALID_IMAGE_CODES`, populated only from the live probe in Requirement 24.4), THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL reject the Photo with reason `invalid_image`; IF the HTTP 400 error body matches no allow-list entry (including while the allow-list is empty), THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL treat the outcome as retryable with `last_error_code` `harm_bad_request`, SHALL NOT reject the Photo, and SHALL log at `error`, because a 400 on an object that already passed the JPEG-signature check and the storage HEAD check most likely means our own request is wrong.
7. WHEN the object bytes do not begin with the JPEG signature `FF D8 FF`, THE Photo_Service SHALL reject the Photo with reason `invalid_image` without calling any provider.
8. THE Photo_Service SHALL persist the harm verdict as soon as it is known, so a retry does not repeat a completed harm check.
9. WHEN the Harm_Moderator returns `harm_bad_request` on `PHOTO_HARM_BAD_REQUEST_ALERT_COUNT` (default 5) consecutive calls in one process, THE Photo_Service SHALL log a distinct `error` event `photos.harm.bad_request_streak`, so that a mismatched request shape or a retired model alias is noticed before photos expire.
10. IF the Harm_Moderator returns HTTP 401 or 403, THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL treat the outcome as retryable with `last_error_code` `harm_auth_error` and log at `error`; IF it returns any other non-2xx status not covered by Requirements 6.5 and 6.6 (for example 404 for a retired model, 413, or 422), THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL treat the outcome as retryable with `last_error_code` `harm_http_<status>` and log at `error`, because a provider failure never decides a photo.

### Requirement 7: Food screen and Vision_Verdict

**User Story:** As a user browsing dishes, I want food photos to be photos of food, without people's faces or screenfuls of text, so that the dish pages are useful and safe.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Photo with `target_kind` `food_log` passes the harm check, THE Photo_Service SHALL call the Vision_Checker with the full image and the structured-output schema containing description, `is_food_or_drink`, `food_confidence` (0 to 1), `prominent_face_count` (integer), `minor_face_visible`, `any_person_part_visible`, `large_text`, `text_fraction` (0 to 1), and `text_seen`.
2. THE Photo_Service SHALL NOT invoke the Vision_Checker for a Photo whose `target_kind` is `trip`.
3. THE Photo_Service SHALL compute the Vision_Verdict in a pure module from Vision_Fields and thresholds, and SHALL NOT use any verdict the model itself returns.
4. THE Vision_Verdict module SHALL reject when `food_confidence` is below 0.6 (`PHOTO_VISION_MIN_FOOD_CONFIDENCE`) or `is_food_or_drink` is false, with reason `not_food`.
5. THE Vision_Verdict module SHALL reject when `prominent_face_count` is greater than `PHOTO_VISION_MAX_PROMINENT_FACES` (default 0, so one clearly visible face rejects at the default) or `minor_face_visible` is true, with reason `face_visible`.
6. THE Vision_Verdict module SHALL reject when `text_fraction` is above 0.25 (`PHOTO_VISION_MAX_TEXT_FRACTION`), with reason `too_much_text`.
7. THE Vision_Verdict module SHALL accept a photo in which `any_person_part_visible` is true and no other rule trips, so a hand, arm, or legs alone never cause a rejection.
8. THE Vision_Verdict module SHALL NOT use the boolean `large_text` in its decision.
9. WHEN more than one rule trips, THE Vision_Verdict module SHALL return all tripped reasons and a primary reason chosen by the fixed precedence `face_visible`, then `not_food`, then `too_much_text`.
10. THE Photo_Service SHALL generate the rejection message shown to the user from our own copy table keyed by the primary reason, and SHALL NOT display any free text produced by a model.
11. IF the Vision_Checker returns an HTTP error, HTTP 503, HTTP 429, a timeout, or a response that does not validate against the Vision_Fields schema, THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL treat the outcome as retryable and SHALL NOT reject the Photo.
12. THE Photo_Service SHALL persist the Vision_Fields (description and `text_seen` truncated to 200 characters) for operator review, and SHALL never return them in a user-facing DTO.
13. THE Photo_Service SHALL persist the Vision_Verdict as soon as it is known, so a retry does not repeat a completed food screen.

### Requirement 8: Moderation lifecycle, retry, and needs_review

**User Story:** As the app owner, I want checks that survive a sleeping server and a flaky provider, so that good photos are not lost and bad photos are never shown.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Photo_Service SHALL set a Photo to `approved` only when the harm verdict is pass and, for `food_log` Photos, the Vision_Verdict is pass (an operator approval of a `needs_review` Photo records both verdicts as pass together with the operator-override marker, Requirement 8.12), and SHALL enforce this invariant in the database with CHECK constraints in addition to the status machine.
2. WHILE a Photo is `uploading`, `pending`, `rejected`, or `needs_review`, THE Photo_Service SHALL make it visible only to its owner.
3. THE Photo_Service SHALL claim a Photo for processing with a lease (`PHOTOS_MODERATION_LEASE_SECONDS`, default 180) in a single conditional update, so that the confirm path, the opportunistic resume, and the Sweep cannot process the same Photo concurrently. THE Photo_Service SHALL renew the lease with a conditional update before the harm step (which makes up to two harm calls, the full image and the thumbnail), before the vision step (which makes up to three attempts), and before the publish step (Requirement 8.11), so that the lease only has to cover the longest single step (Requirement 8.14).
4. WHEN the Moderation_Pipeline claims a Photo, THE Photo_Service SHALL increment `attempts` in the same conditional update, so that a process that dies mid-job still consumes an attempt.
5. WHEN a retryable outcome occurs, THE Photo_Service SHALL record a `last_error_code`, set `next_attempt_at` to now plus `min(60 s × 4^(attempts−1), 6 h)`, and set `lease_until` to NULL in the same conditional update, so that the first retry waits only for its backoff and not for the remainder of a live lease.
6. IF a retryable outcome occurs while the Photo's `attempts` has already reached `PHOTOS_MODERATION_MAX_ATTEMPTS` (default 6) without a final verdict, THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL set the Photo to `needs_review` immediately in the same step, without waiting for `next_attempt_at`; and IF a Photo is found due for processing with `attempts` already at the maximum (for example after a process death), THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL set it to `needs_review` instead of processing it again, using a conditional write that also requires `lease_until` to be NULL or in the past, so that a live final attempt can never be moved to `needs_review` from under itself.
7. THE Photo_Service SHALL run at most `PHOTOS_MODERATION_CONCURRENCY` (default 3) Moderation_Pipeline jobs at once in one process, holding excess jobs in a bounded in-memory queue and leaving overflow `pending` for the Sweep.
8. THE Photo_Service SHALL permit only these status transitions: `uploading→pending`, `uploading→deleted`, `pending→approved`, `pending→rejected`, `pending→needs_review`, `needs_review→approved`, `needs_review→rejected`, `approved→hidden`, `hidden→approved`, `approved→deleted`, `rejected→deleted`, `hidden→deleted`, `pending→deleted`, `needs_review→deleted`; and no transition out of `deleted`.
9. WHEN a Photo becomes `rejected`, THE Photo_Service SHALL keep it visible to the owner with its reason for 24 hours (`PHOTOS_REJECTED_RETENTION_HOURS`), and then set it to `deleted`.
10. WHEN the Moderation_Pipeline reads an Upload_Key object, THE Photo_Service SHALL compare the object's ETag with the ETag recorded at confirm (Requirement 4.9), and IF they differ (the object was overwritten after confirm), THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL reject the Photo with reason `invalid_image` and SHALL NOT use any verdict computed on a different object version. THE Photo_Service SHALL reuse persisted partial verdicts (Requirements 6.8, 7.13) only while the object's ETag still equals the recorded ETag.
11. WHEN a Photo passes all required checks, THE Photo_Service SHALL, before writing `approved`, server-side copy the full and thumbnail objects from their Upload_Keys to their Serving_Keys conditional on the recorded ETags, SHALL verify the copied objects' ETags equal the recorded ETags, and SHALL delete the Upload_Key objects; IF the conditional copy fails because the source changed, THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL, subject to Requirements 8.13 and 8.17 (it deletes Serving_Key objects only when the final conditional write succeeded or the re-read status is neither `approved` nor `hidden`), delete any copied Serving_Key objects and reject the Photo with reason `invalid_image`; IF the copy fails for a transient storage error, THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL treat the outcome as retryable. THE Photo_Service SHALL write `approved` only after the Serving_Key objects exist and are verified.
12. WHEN an operator approves a `needs_review` Photo, THE Photo_Service SHALL perform the same ETag check and Serving_Key copy as Requirement 8.11 and then record `harm_verdict` as pass, `vision_verdict` as pass for a `food_log` Photo, and `approved_by_operator` as true, so that no Photo is ever `approved` without pass verdicts.
13. IF a Moderation_Pipeline job's final conditional status write affects zero rows, THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL re-read the Photo's current status and SHALL delete the Serving_Key objects that job wrote only when the current status is neither `approved` nor `hidden` (for example `deleted` or `rejected`).
14. THE Photo_Service SHALL require `PHOTOS_MODERATION_LEASE_SECONDS` to be at least the longest single step bound plus a 10-second margin, where the step bound is the maximum of twice `PHOTO_HARM_TIMEOUT_MS` and twice (three times `PHOTO_VISION_TIMEOUT_MS` plus 2000 ms), and SHALL fail startup with a `ConfigError` when it is not (with the defaults the step bound is 124 seconds and the minimum lease is 134 seconds).
15. IF an Upload_Key object that the Moderation_Pipeline or an operator approval needs is missing (a storage "not found" on read or copy), THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL reject the Photo with reason `invalid_image` without retrying and without calling any provider, because the upload cannot be recovered and the owner can upload again; the rejection SHALL be a conditional write, so a job that loses a race to a job that already published the Photo changes nothing (Requirement 8.13).
16. WHEN a Photo has been in `needs_review` and its age since `created_at` exceeds `PHOTOS_NEEDS_REVIEW_MAX_AGE_HOURS` (default 120), THE Photo_Sweep SHALL set it to `deleted` (Requirement 19.3), so that a `needs_review` Photo is always resolved before the lifecycle rule could remove its only objects (the config relationship is Requirement 8.18).
17. WHEN the re-read of Requirement 8.13 shows the current status is `approved` or `hidden`, THE Photo_Service SHALL leave every Serving_Key object in place and exit without error, because another job has already published the Photo; a zero-row write therefore never removes the objects behind a served Photo.
18. THE config loader SHALL require `PHOTOS_NEEDS_REVIEW_MAX_AGE_HOURS` plus 24 hours to be no greater than `PHOTOS_UPLOAD_PREFIX_EXPIRY_DAYS` × 24.
19. THE Photo_Service SHALL contain every detached job (a Moderation_Pipeline job started by confirm, by the opportunistic resume, by the queue pump, or by the Photo_Sweep's resume step, and the asynchronous Photo_Sweep itself) in its own top-level `try`/`catch`, implemented in the runner and sweep code rather than delegated to the `Scheduler` implementation, so that no rejection can escape as an unhandled rejection (which ends the Node 22 process and drops every in-flight Photo). WHEN a detached job throws, THE Photo_Service SHALL log `photos.job.crashed` at `error` with the photo id (or `sweep`) and an error code only (no image data, URLs, keys, or provider bodies), SHALL leave the Photo `pending` so that its lease expires and the next trigger resumes it (the attempt was already counted by the claim, so repeated crashes escalate to `needs_review` through Requirement 8.6), and SHALL release the sweep's single-flight guard.
20. THE Photo_Service SHALL fence lease renewal to the claim that owns it: every `renewLease` call SHALL be conditional on the `attempts` value returned by that job's own claim, so that a stale job whose lease expired and was re-claimed by a second job renews zero rows and exits (Requirement 8.13 then governs its cleanup), and so that Requirement 8.3's exclusivity holds for renewals as well as for claims.

### Requirement 9: Reading photos

**User Story:** As a viewer, I want photos to load fast and securely, so that galleries are quick without exposing the storage bucket.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Photo_Service SHALL serve image bytes only through presigned GET URLs to a private bucket, and SHALL NOT proxy image bytes.
2. THE Photo_Service SHALL mint presigned GET URLs that expire after 3600 seconds (`PHOTOS_GET_URL_TTL_SECONDS`) and SHALL mint them only for Photos the requesting viewer is allowed to see, and only for Serving_Keys except as Requirement 9.9 allows.
3. THE Photo_Service SHALL return in each read DTO a `thumbUrl` and a `url`, and SHALL NOT return object keys or the bucket name.

5. IF a viewer requests a Photo they are not allowed to see, THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL respond `photo_not_found`, so that existence is not revealed.
6. THE Mobile_App SHALL cache photo lists with a stale time shorter than the URL lifetime (30 minutes) and SHALL refetch on an expired URL.
7. THE Photo_Service SHALL ensure that presigned URLs and their query strings are not written to application logs.
8. THE Photo_Service SHALL respond with `Cache-Control: private, no-store` on every route that returns a presigned URL, and SHALL never return a presigned URL from a publicly cacheable route.
9. THE Photo_Service SHALL mint a GET URL for a Photo's Serving_Key only when the Photo is `approved` or `hidden`; WHILE a Photo is `uploading`, `pending`, or `needs_review`, THE Photo_Service SHALL mint a GET URL for its thumbnail Upload_Key for the owner only (and no full-image URL); WHILE a Photo is `rejected`, THE Photo_Service SHALL mint no URL for anyone; and THE Photo_Service SHALL never mint an Upload_Key URL for a viewer other than the owner, except that the Admin_Panel MAY mint a GET URL with a 300-second TTL (`PHOTOS_ADMIN_URL_TTL_SECONDS`) for the thumbnail Upload_Key and the full Upload_Key of a `needs_review` Photo, because such a Photo has no Serving_Key yet and, for a food Photo, the food screen never completed, so the operator needs the full image to decide. The key selection SHALL be one pure function of the Photo's status and the viewer kind (`owner`, `other`, or `admin`).

6. WHEN a Photo linked to a post is deleted, THE Photo_Service SHALL render its slot as `removed` and SHALL keep the post.
7. WHEN a post is deleted by its author, or by an organizer through `delete_any_trip_photo`, THE Photo_Service SHALL delete the post's Photos and the post's reactions and comments in one transaction, after loading the post with `WHERE id = :feedItemId AND trip_id = :id AND type = 'member_post'` (Requirement 14.13). THE Photo_Service SHALL ensure that a `member_post` item with no slot visible to a non-author viewer is omitted from that viewer's feed, where a slot is visible to a non-author only when its state is `ready` or `checking` (states `removed` and `unavailable` are not visible), and where the author always sees their own post.

THE Mobile_App SHALL show every Trip member a dismissible one-time notice in the Album (acknowledgement stored per device) stating that any member can share the trip recap with their friends and that the recap can include photos from all crew members, and that deleting a photo removes it everywhere; THE notice SHALL NOT offer a per-photo opt-out, because none exists (Requirement 12.7).

### Requirement 16: Reporting and auto-hide

**User Story:** As a user, I want to report a photo that should not be there, so that it is hidden and reviewed quickly.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user who is allowed to see a Photo calls `POST /photos/:id/report` with a reason from `inappropriate`, `shows_person`, `not_food`, `spam`, `other` and an optional note of at most 300 characters, THE Photo_Service SHALL record one Photo_Report.
2. THE Photo_Service SHALL reject a report on the reporter's own Photo and a report on a Photo the reporter cannot see with `photo_not_found`.
3. THE Photo_Service SHALL store at most one Photo_Report per reporter per Photo, and a repeat report SHALL succeed without creating a second row.
4. WHEN the count of distinct `open` reports that count toward auto-hide on an `approved` Photo reaches the threshold for its kind (`PHOTOS_REPORT_AUTOHIDE_FOOD` default 3, `PHOTOS_REPORT_AUTOHIDE_TRIP` default 1), THE Photo_Service SHALL set the Photo to `hidden` with `hidden_reason` `reports`. For a food Photo every distinct open reporter counts; for a Trip Photo only a reporter who is a current member of that Trip counts (Requirement 16.9, decision D31).
5. WHILE a Photo is `hidden`, THE Photo_Service SHALL show it only to its owner (with an "under review" note) and to the Admin_Panel.
6. WHEN an operator dismisses the reports on a Photo, THE Photo_Service SHALL mark them `dismissed` so they no longer count, and a restored Photo SHALL need new reports to be hidden again.
7. THE Photo_Service SHALL apply the existing per-user mutation rate limit to the report route.
8. WHERE the reporter (or a viewer blocking by `photoId`, Requirement 17.1) can see the Photo only as a Summary_Share recipient, THE report route and the photo-id block route SHALL accept an optional `shareId` and SHALL treat the Photo as visible only when every condition of Requirement 15.3 holds for that share (including the current Friendship, Requirement 15.14) **and** the Photo is an `approved` Photo of that share's Trip that the recipient can see under Requirement 15.27 (originally only a current recap highlight, Requirement 15.5, decision D32; widened to every such Photo by decision D35 and Requirement 16.10); a Photo of another Trip, a Photo that is not `approved`, a Photo of a blocked uploader, and any Photo without a `shareId`, SHALL answer `photo_not_found`.
9. WHEN a report on a Trip Photo arrives through a `shareId`, or comes from any reporter who is not a current member of the Trip, THE Photo_Service SHALL record it as a Photo_Report (one per reporter per Photo, Requirement 16.3) so that it appears in the admin review queue (Requirement 18.1), and SHALL NOT count it toward the auto-hide threshold of Requirement 16.4; WHEN a current Trip member reports an approved Trip Photo THEN it SHALL count and the Photo SHALL be hidden at the threshold.
10. WHEN a report (`POST /photos/:id/report`) or a photo-id block (`POST /me/blocks` with `{ photoId, shareId }`) arrives with a `shareId`, THE Photo_Service SHALL accept it for any `approved` Trip Photo whose Trip is the share's Trip, that is not the caller's own, and whose uploader has no Block with the caller in either direction, provided the gate of Requirement 15.26 holds for that share on this call (non-deleted recipient, current Friendship re-checked, `includePhotos` true, sharer still a member, Trip existing); THE Photo_Service SHALL otherwise answer `photo_not_found` and record nothing; a report accepted this way SHALL be stored and listed for the admin but SHALL NOT count toward the auto-hide threshold (Requirement 16.9, decision D31); a block accepted this way SHALL return no user id (Requirement 17.1).

### Requirement 17: Blocking

**User Story:** As a user, I want to stop seeing a person's photos and stop them seeing mine.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Photo_Service SHALL provide `POST /me/blocks` accepting exactly one of `userId` or `photoId` (so a viewer can block the uploader of a photo without ever learning who the uploader is), `DELETE /me/blocks/:blockId`, and `GET /me/blocks`. WHEN `photoId` is given, THE Photo_Service SHALL require that the viewer is allowed to see that Photo and that it is not the viewer's own, and otherwise respond `photo_not_found`, and SHALL resolve the uploader on the server without returning the uploader's user id. THE `GET /me/blocks` response SHALL carry each block's own `blockId` and creation time, and SHALL include the blocked user's display name and avatar preset only when the blocker is currently a Friend of that user, and `null` for both otherwise.
2. THE Photo_Service SHALL reject blocking oneself with `validation_failed`, and SHALL make blocking an already-blocked user succeed without a second row.
3. WHILE a Block exists between two users in either direction, THE Photo_Service SHALL hide each user's Photos from the other on every photo surface: dish galleries, the Album, feed post slots (rendered `unavailable`), and Summary_Share reads.
4. THE Photo_Service SHALL NOT change friendships, friend requests, shares, or Trip membership as a result of a Block.
5. THE Mobile_App SHALL offer "Report" and "Block uploader" on any photo that is not the viewer's own, SHALL send the photo id (not a user id) when blocking from a photo, and SHALL provide a Blocked users list with unblock under You & Crew that shows the label "Photo uploader" and the date when the name is null.

### Requirement 18: Admin review queue

**User Story:** As the operator, I want to review reported and stuck photos and remove bad ones, so that the app meets app-store content rules.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Admin_Panel SHALL provide `GET /admin/photos` behind the existing HTTP-Basic gate, listing Photos with open reports, Photos in `hidden`, and Photos in `needs_review`, oldest first.
2. THE Admin_Panel SHALL show each listed Photo's thumbnail and a link to its full image using presigned GET URLs minted at render time with a 300-second TTL (`PHOTOS_ADMIN_URL_TTL_SECONDS`), taken from the Serving_Keys for `approved` and `hidden` Photos and from the Upload_Keys for `needs_review` Photos as Requirement 9.9 allows, together with its kind, the uploader's display name (the operator sees the real name even where the public DTO shows none), report reasons and counts, and the stored Vision_Fields.
3. THE Admin_Panel SHALL escape every user-sourced string with `escapeHtml`.
4. THE Admin_Panel SHALL provide POST actions `approve` (applies to `needs_review` Photos and follows Requirement 8.12), `restore` (applies to `hidden` Photos), `remove`, `dismiss-reports`, and `ban-uploader`, each a single-statement or single-transaction write (apart from the storage copy in `approve`) that works only in response to the operator's request.
5. WHEN the operator removes a Photo, THE Admin_Panel SHALL, in one transaction, set it to `deleted`, mark its open reports `upheld`, and increment the uploader's `upheld_count` in `photo_upload_strikes` by exactly one (once per removed Photo, not once per report), and SHALL show the uploader's `upheld_count` from that table. THE `photo_upload_strikes` row SHALL be independent of the Photo row and its reports, so that the Photo_Sweep's hard-delete of the Photo and the cascade of its `photo_reports` rows never reduce the count.
6. WHEN the operator bans an uploader, THE Admin_Panel SHALL insert a `photo_upload_bans` row.
7. THE Admin_Panel SHALL respond to every `/admin/photos` route with `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`.
8. THE Admin_Panel SHALL show a Photo usage section with the last 14 days of `photo_usage_daily`.
9. THE Admin_Panel SHALL enforce same-origin on every `POST /admin/photos/*` route: IF the `Sec-Fetch-Site` header is present and is not `same-origin`, THEN THE Admin_Panel SHALL respond `403` and change no state; ELSE IF the `Origin` header is present and its host does not equal the request's `Host`, THEN THE Admin_Panel SHALL respond `403` and change no state; a request carrying neither header (a non-browser client) is allowed, because the HTTP Basic gate still applies. This protects against cross-site form posts that carry the browser's cached Basic credentials.
10. WHERE `PHOTOS_ENABLED` is false, THE Admin_Panel SHALL still list the queue from the database without thumbnails (showing "Photos are disabled; thumbnails unavailable"), SHALL allow the `remove`, `dismiss-reports`, `restore`, and `ban-uploader` actions (database writes only), and SHALL refuse `approve` with an explanatory message because it needs the Object_Store.
11. THE Admin_Panel SHALL show a "Ban uploader" control on the removal confirmation page (beside the uploader's `upheld_count`), and SHALL accept `ban-uploader` for a Photo in any status including a `deleted` Photo that has not yet been swept, so that the operator can act on a removed Photo that has already left the queue list.
12. THE Admin_Panel SHALL obtain presigned GET URLs and the `approve` action only through an `AdminPhotoPort` (`presignGet(key, ttlSeconds)` and `operatorApprove(photoId)`) supplied through `AdminRoutesOptions.photos`, and THE API composition root SHALL supply that port only WHERE `PHOTOS_ENABLED` is true, so that its absence selects the degraded mode of Requirement 18.10.

### Requirement 19: Storage cleanup, sweep, and cron

**User Story:** As the app owner, I want deleted photos truly removed from storage and stuck jobs retried, without a background worker.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Photo_Service SHALL provide `POST` and `HEAD /internal/photos/sweep/run` gated by the `x-cron-secret` header compared with `PHOTOS_CRON_SECRET`, returning `202` immediately and performing the sweep asynchronously in-process, with a single-flight guard.
2. IF the secret is missing or wrong, THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL respond `unauthorized`.
3. WHEN the Photo_Sweep runs, THE Photo_Service SHALL, in order: resume due `pending` Photos; set `uploading` Photos older than `PHOTOS_UPLOAD_EXPIRY_MINUTES` (default 120) to `deleted`; set `rejected` Photos older than `PHOTOS_REJECTED_RETENTION_HOURS` to `deleted`; set `needs_review` Photos older than `PHOTOS_NEEDS_REVIEW_MAX_AGE_HOURS` to `deleted` (Requirement 8.16); reap orphans; delete bucket objects for `deleted` Photos; hard-delete the rows whose objects were deleted; prune expired counters and Vision_Fields.
4. THE Photo_Service SHALL reap orphans by setting to `deleted` every non-deleted Photo whose `owner_id` is null or whose target no longer exists, as determined by the Target_Adapter, so that cascade deletions of users, trips, food logs, or food items cannot leave objects behind.
5. THE Photo_Service SHALL delete the full and thumbnail objects under both the Upload_Key and the Serving_Key prefixes (four keys) of a `deleted` Photo before hard-deleting its row, and SHALL treat deleting an already-absent object as success. THE project SHALL document a bucket lifecycle rule that expires any object under the `u/` prefix after `PHOTOS_UPLOAD_PREFIX_EXPIRY_DAYS` days (default 7, the Upload_Prefix_Expiry), as a backstop for objects written to an Upload_Key after its row was removed; the 7-day value is deliberately longer than the longest legitimate life of an upload object (a `needs_review` Photo, Requirement 8.16).
6. IF deleting an object fails, THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL keep the row, increment `delete_attempts`, log the failure at `warn`, and retry on the next sweep.
7. THE Photo_Service SHALL bound each sweep step by `PHOTOS_SWEEP_BATCH` (default 200) rows and the whole sweep by `PHOTOS_SWEEP_TIME_BUDGET_MS` (default 25000).
8. THE Photo_Service SHALL make the sweep idempotent so that running it twice in a row produces the same final state as running it once.
9. THE Photo_Service SHALL expose a `deleteAllForUser` operation that sets all of a user's Photos to `deleted`, as the extension point for a future account-deletion feature.
10. THE Photo_Cron SHALL be a dedicated job separate from the keep-alive pinger, running at most 4 times per day (suggested crontab `5 7,13,19,0 * * *` in America/New_York).
11. THE Photo_Service SHALL include, in each sweep, the `needs_review` expiry of Requirement 8.16 as its own idempotent step, so that a Photo never waits for an operator longer than `PHOTOS_NEEDS_REVIEW_MAX_AGE_HOURS`.
12. THE Photo_Service SHALL include, in each sweep, an idempotent step that deletes the two Upload_Key objects (`u/<photoId>/full.jpg` and `u/<photoId>/thumb.jpg`) of every `approved` or `hidden` Photo whose `served_at` is older than one hour and whose `upload_cleaned_at` is null, and then sets `upload_cleaned_at`, so that a failed best-effort delete after approval does not leave an upload object waiting for the lifecycle rule, and SHALL treat an absent object as success.
13. THE Photo_Sweep's resume step SHALL start at most `PHOTOS_MODERATION_CONCURRENCY` Moderation_Pipeline jobs per sweep and SHALL check the remaining `PHOTOS_SWEEP_TIME_BUDGET_MS` before starting each one, because a started job cannot be interrupted and may take up to the lease-sized step bound (Requirement 8.14); Photos left over wait for the next trigger. THE `POST` and `HEAD /internal/photos/sweep/run` route SHALL return `skipped` with reason `in_flight` when a sweep is already running (the same reason string the Trip booking runner uses).

### Requirement 20: Configuration, kill switches, and secrets

WHERE `PHOTOS_ENABLED` is false (the default), THE Photo_Service SHALL register no working photo routes beyond `GET /photos/config` reporting `enabled: false`, SHALL NOT require any storage or provider variable, and SHALL NOT break the Render build. The one exception is the Sharing-side recap route `GET /me/inbox/:shareId/trip-recap`, which stays registered and serves figures with `photos.access` of `unavailable` (Requirements 15.17, 20.9), and its album sibling `GET /me/inbox/:shareId/trip-recap/photos` (Requirement 15.30), which stays registered the same way and answers `unavailable` with no photo.
3. WHERE `PHOTOS_ENABLED` is true, THE config loader SHALL require the storage endpoint, bucket, access key id, secret access key, `PHOTO_HARM_API_KEY`, and `PHOTOS_CRON_SECRET`, and SHALL require `PHOTO_VISION_API_KEY` when `PHOTOS_FOOD_ENABLED` is true, and SHALL fail startup with a `ConfigError` naming each missing variable.

THE config loader SHALL, WHERE `PHOTOS_ENABLED` is true, fail startup with a `ConfigError` when `PHOTOS_MODERATION_LEASE_SECONDS` violates Requirement 8.14 or when `PHOTOS_NEEDS_REVIEW_MAX_AGE_HOURS` violates Requirement 8.18. WHERE `PHOTOS_ENABLED` is false, THE Sharing-side recap route SHALL return the recap figures with `photos.access` of `unavailable` and no photo, and its album sibling `GET /me/inbox/:shareId/trip-recap/photos` (Requirement 15.30) SHALL return `access` of `unavailable` with no photo, and THE trips feed route SHALL drop `photoIds` and return `member_post` items with an empty `photos` array (Requirement 14.14), so that disabling photos never leaves a recap card that cannot be opened and never leaks a raw photo id.

### Requirement 21: Cost visibility and observability

**User Story:** As the app owner, I want to see what photos cost and when something breaks.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Photo_Service SHALL maintain a fixed-shape `photo_usage_daily` row per day counting uploads, upload bytes, harm calls, vision calls, vision prompt tokens, vision output tokens, rejections, `needs_review` transitions, and Exif segments found (`exif_present`).

3. IF the Vision_Checker returns HTTP 404 for the configured model, THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL log at `error` with the code `vision_model_unavailable`, try `PHOTO_VISION_FALLBACK_MODEL` once, and treat a failure of both as retryable.

**User Story:** As a user, I want one screen that lists every photo I have uploaded, so that I do not have to look through my food logs, the dish and restaurant galleries, the Trip Album, and the Trip Activity feed to find a photo, see whether it passed the checks, or delete it. This requirement is additive: it adds one read route and one screen, depends on Phase 1 of this spec (Requirements 4.10 to 4.12, 9, 23) and on `account-deletion` (Requirement 28), and changes no existing requirement or criterion.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Mobile_App SHALL provide the My_Photos_Screen as a new route `MyPhotos` (no params) in `CollectionStack` (`apps/mobile/src/navigation/CollectionStack.tsx`, `CollectionStackParamList`), the stack of the bottom tab whose navigator name is `Collection` and whose tab bar label is "Vault" (`MainTabs` in `RootNavigator.tsx`). THE Mobile_App SHALL NOT add a bottom tab, and SHALL NOT add the screen, its route, or its entry control to `ProfileScreen.tsx` (whose default export is not a registered route), to `YouAndCrewStack`, or to any other stack.
2. THE Mobile_App SHALL reach the My_Photos_Screen from one entry control in the header of the Vault main screen (the `CollectionHome` route, `CollectionScreen`), shown on every Vault segment (Pins, Food, Lists, Park Stats), with the accessibility role `button`, the accessibility label "My Photos", and a touch target of at least 44 by 44 points. WHERE `GET /photos/config` reports `enabled: false`, fails, or is still loading, THE Mobile_App SHALL render no entry control (the same hide-when-disabled rule as Requirement 23.7). The per-kind switches (`foodEnabled`, `tripEnabled`) SHALL NOT hide it, because existing photos stay readable when a kind's uploads are off (Requirements 10.9, 12.8).
3. THE Photo_Service SHALL provide `GET /me/photos/all` returning a `PhotoPageDTO<MyPhotoDTO>` of the caller's own non-`deleted` Photos across every target kind (`food_log` and `trip` today; a kind added later under Requirement 25.1 appears with no change, and its cell gets the generic accessibility label "Photo, opens viewer" of Requirement 29.18), newest first, and SHALL require a signed-in session (`unauthorized` otherwise). THE route SHALL be an additional route: `GET /me/photos` of Requirement 4.11, its schema, and its behavior SHALL stay unchanged.
4. THE Photo_Service SHALL return only Photos whose `owner_id` equals the session user id. THE route SHALL accept only the optional query keys `cursor` and `limit` and SHALL answer `validation_failed` for any other key (including `userId`, `ownerId`, `targetKind`, and `scopeId`), so that no parameter can select another user's Photos or narrow the list. THE response SHALL NOT contain any other user's Photo, any uploader name, avatar, or user id, or any Friend or Block information.
5. THE Photo_Service SHALL return every one of the owner's Photos in the statuses `uploading`, `pending`, `approved`, `rejected`, `needs_review`, and `hidden`, each with its `status` and, for a `rejected` Photo, its `rejectReason` and `rejectMessage` (Requirements 4.11, 23.2). A `rejected` Photo SHALL stay in the list until its owner deletes it or it is deleted at the end of its 24-hour retention (Requirement 8.9, D13). THE Photo_Service SHALL never return a `deleted` Photo, whether it was deleted by its owner, removed by an operator, deleted with its Trip or food log, or purged with an account.

WHILE an account is in `pending_deletion` it has no session and cannot call the route (`account-deletion`), and WHEN the account purge sets the user's Photos to `deleted` (Requirement 28.1) the list no longer contains them; this requirement adds no account-deletion behavior of its own.

A member who has left or been removed from a Trip therefore still sees their own Trip Photos in the list and can delete them (Requirements 12.6, 12.11, D30), and existing Photos stay listed while `PHOTOS_FOOD_ENABLED` or `PHOTOS_TRIP_ENABLED` is false (Requirements 10.9, 12.8). IF `limit` is out of range or not an integer, or `cursor` is malformed, THEN THE Photo_Service SHALL answer `validation_failed` and read nothing. Following `nextCursor` from the first page SHALL return every listed Photo exactly once, including Photos that share a `created_at` (a batch upload inserts up to 10 rows in one transaction).
9. THE Photo_Service SHALL return each item as the existing `MyPhotoDTO` with its fields unchanged, SHALL mint its URLs with the owner key selection of Requirement 9.9 (`approved` and `hidden`: serving-key `url` and `thumbUrl`; `uploading`, `pending`, and `needs_review`: an owner-only thumbnail URL and a null `url`; `rejected`: both null) and the lifetime of Requirement 9.2, SHALL NOT return object keys or the bucket name (Requirement 9.3), SHALL respond with `Cache-Control: private, no-store` (Requirement 9.8), and SHALL NOT write a presigned URL to a log (Requirement 9.7).
10. THE Photo_Service SHALL serve the list with one indexed read per page and no write, and SHALL add no table, column, index, migration, configuration variable, Redis command, or background work (the hosting constraints of `docs/hosting.md`). WHERE `PHOTOS_ENABLED` is false, THE Photo_Service SHALL NOT register the route (Requirement 20.2).
11. THE Mobile_App SHALL render the list as a grid of 3 columns in the order the server returned it (newest first, without re-sorting), each cell a square thumbnail (`thumbUrl`) with the existing `PhotoStatusChip` for that photo's status directly beneath it, so that every status, including a `rejected` photo's reason, is visible as text and an icon (Requirement 23.2). WHERE a photo has no readable image (`uploading`, where the uploaded object may not exist yet, or `rejected`, which has no URL), THE Mobile_App SHALL render a neutral placeholder in place of the image.
12. WHEN the user taps a cell, THE Mobile_App SHALL open the existing `PhotoViewer` for that photo, in every status (so a `rejected` photo can be deleted to free its slot, Requirement 5.12), as the user's own photo: with the existing Delete control and its confirmation (Requirement 23.3, which calls `DELETE /photos/:id`), and without Report or "Block uploader" (Requirement 17.5 offers them only on a photo that is not the viewer's own, and Requirement 16.2 refuses a report on one's own photo). WHERE the tapped photo has no readable image (`uploading`, whose uploaded object may not exist yet, or `rejected`), THE Mobile_App SHALL pass the viewer no image URL, so that the viewer shows its "Photo unavailable" placeholder with Delete available rather than a broken image. WHEN the delete succeeds, THE Mobile_App SHALL close the viewer, SHALL show the list without that photo, and SHALL refresh every other photo query that could show it (the existing photo-query invalidation of `useDeletePhoto`).
13. WHILE the list contains a photo whose effective status is `pending`, THE Mobile_App SHALL poll the batched `GET /me/photos/status?ids=` route under Requirements 4.10, 23.4, and 23.10 (one request per tick however many photos are pending, chunked at 10 ids, every 3 seconds for the first 60 seconds and then every 15 seconds), SHALL show the polled status on the chip, and WHEN a photo reaches a final status SHALL refresh the list without the user leaving the screen. THE Mobile_App SHALL poll only `pending` photos: `uploading`, `needs_review`, and `hidden` are never polled (D36). THE Mobile_App SHALL prefer the polled status (and the polled reject message) over the list status only while the list item's status is `pending`; once the list reports any other status, the chip and the reject message SHALL show the list values, so a stale polled value from earlier in the session never overrides a fresher list read (for example a photo polled to `approved` and later auto-hidden shows "Under review" after a refresh). WHEN the loaded list contains `uploading` photos for which no upload is in progress on this device, THE Mobile_App SHALL call confirm for them (Requirements 4.6, 4.12), sending each photo id at most once per screen visit (an id already sent during this visit SHALL NOT be sent again, including when a refetch returns the same photo with a different thumbnail URL), and SHALL refresh the list after a confirm only when at least one result is `accepted`, `already`, or `invalid`. WHEN that confirm reports a photo as `incomplete`, THE cell SHALL stay in `uploading` and THE Mobile_App SHALL NOT show the retry chip state of Requirement 4.12 on this screen (this matches `FoodLogPhotos`, which also does not consume `retryPhotoIds`); THE Mobile_App SHALL retry the confirm for that photo on the next visit to the screen or on pull-to-refresh, and SHALL NOT retry it on every list load.
14. WHEN the user scrolls to the end of the loaded list and a `nextCursor` exists, THE Mobile_App SHALL request the next page with that cursor. IF a later page request fails, THEN THE Mobile_App SHALL keep the already loaded cells and show an inline "Couldn't load more photos" message with a Retry control.
15. WHEN the first page contains no photos, THE Mobile_App SHALL show an empty state with the title "No photos yet" and a one-line explanation that photos added to food logs and trips appear here, and SHALL offer no add-photo control.
16. IF the first page request fails, THEN THE Mobile_App SHALL show an error state with a Retry control and no placeholder or cached cells, and Retry SHALL request the first page again. THE screen SHALL support pull-to-refresh, which refetches the list.
17. THE Mobile_App SHALL cache the list with a stale time shorter than the URL lifetime (Requirement 9.6) so that opening the screen refetches an old list, and WHEN a thumbnail fails to load THE Mobile_App SHALL refetch the list at most once per screen visit (not once per failing cell, and not again when a thumbnail still fails to load after that refetch) to replace expired URLs. THE Mobile_App SHALL re-arm that single refetch only on pull-to-refresh or when the screen regains focus after more than the stale time. A mutation elsewhere that already invalidates the photo queries (an upload, a delete, a block change, a status poll reaching a final status) SHALL refresh this list as well.
18. THE Mobile_App SHALL build the screen's styles with `makeStyles` and `useAppTheme` and SHALL contain no raw colour literal (Requirement 23.5); SHALL give each cell the accessibility role `imagebutton` and a label that names the photo kind and the action (for example "Food photo, opens viewer" or "Trip photo, opens viewer", and "Photo, opens viewer" for any other target kind); SHALL NOT rely on colour alone for status (the chip carries a text label and an icon); and SHALL label the loading indicator, the empty state, the error state, and every control.
19. THE My_Photos_Screen SHALL NOT offer filters, sort options, search, albums or collections, multi-select or bulk delete, sharing of a photo, any other user's photos, caption editing, or uploading; these are non-goals of this requirement (the upload and caption flows stay where Requirements 3, 10, 12, and 14 put them).

## Out of Scope
