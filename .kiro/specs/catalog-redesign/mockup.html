💡 Reality Check: Verified Data & Redesign Principles
Direct response to user review: Aligning strictly with what the database & API provide, preserving the complete filtering system, and clarifying architectural boundaries.

✅ 1. Real Data We Actually Have

Every piece of information shown in this revised mockup is 100% verified against our models:

  • From ExperienceDTO: name, category, land, worldShowcaseCountry, priceTier ($, $$, $$$), heightRequirement (e.g. 44" min), groupedFacets / physical considerations (Thrill, Dark, Big Drops), mealPeriods, imageUrl.
  • From User State: useCompletedExperiences() (visited checkmark) and useFavoritedExperiences() (favorite heart).
  • From ResortDTO: tier (Deluxe, Moderate, Value, DVC), transportationModes (Monorail, Skyliner, Boat), featurePool, recreation, transitTimes.
  • From ParkLiveSnapshotDTO: Integer average park wait time across 4 parks (as already computed on HomeScreen's ParkWaitPulse).

❌ 2. What Was Speculative & Has Been Removed

We deliberately removed data that does NOT belong on static catalog list rows:

  • NO per-ride live waits on catalog rows: Catalog is a 5-minute cached reference directory (GET /catalog). Per-ride live wait times update every minute and belong strictly in LiveWaitsScreen and LiveWaitCockpit on detail view. Sourcing live waits for 150+ items on browse would break offline caching and hammer the server.
  • NO live Lightning Lane return windows: Return windows fluctuate second-by-second and live in live services, not the catalog DTO.
  • NO live showtime clocks ("Next show 2:15 PM"): Belongs in LiveDetail on the detail screen.

🔍 3. Complete Filtering System Restored

Nothing from the existing powerful filter architecture is lost:

  • Category Tabs: All, Rides, Dining, Shows.
  • Dedicated Favorites Toggle: Restored right beside the tabs so users can instantly narrow any destination to their saved items.
  • Filters Modal Trigger: Multi-select sheet for Lands, Price Tiers, Facets, and Festivals.
  • Dynamic Quick Chips: Automatically derived from active tab items (e.g., Thrill Ride, Quick Service, Table Service, Height 40"+).
  • Collapsible Land Accordions: Retained, but with cleaner visual hierarchy, clear count badges, and rich card tags.

🏨 4. Complete Resorts Ecosystem Restored (Zero Regressions)

Full preservation of the 258 resort experiences without returning to the old 30-accordion clunkiness:

  • Full Experience Partition (258 Total): 32 Resort Hotels, 104 Dining & Lounges, 85 Recreation & Activities, and 37 Sub-Destination experiences.
  • First-Class Sub-Destinations: Dedicated showcase cards for Disney's BoardWalk & Promenade, ESPN Wide World of Sports Complex, and Property-Wide Recreation & Sports (Mini-Golf, Championship Golf, Marinas).
  • Direct Navigation to Existing Resort Screen: Tapping any hotel card navigates directly to the app's existing production ExperienceDetailScreen (with ResortGuideSection and PassportAndLoreLens). The on-property Recreation & Resort Amenities section is enriched in place with full verified activities (pools, boat rentals, cabanas, fitness/spas, campfires).
  • Completion & Favorites Parity: Every hotel, dining venue, and recreation activity supports the green visited checkmark and favorite heart toggle.
