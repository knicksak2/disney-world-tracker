How it fits together. Screen 1 is the Overview hub — one screen tall, no endless scroll: a hero completion ring, the gold percentile brag, and four tappable highlight cards. Tapping a card pushes a focused detail screen (2–4) with a native back arrow; the tab bar stays visible.

Rings vs bars. Rings are the headline visual — the big hero on the hub and the small "at a glance" ring on Coverage. Comparisons use ranked progress bars sorted most→least complete, so 6 parks or 11 categories stay scannable. Coverage uses a lens switcher (Parks · Categories · Areas · Lands) so you see one dimension at a time.

Ratings shows a big average + stars, a 1–10 distribution histogram (peak bars in gold), highest/lowest hero cards, and per-park/category averages — or a friendly unlock state below the 3-rating threshold. Interests reuses the ranked-bar pattern for Disney's themed facets.

Per-resort completion (new). Screen 5 shows the new Resorts lens — "what you've done at each resort" (dining, recreation, spa, etc.), ranked most→least complete. This is powered by a small additive backend dimension (byResort, grouping experiences by their resort_id). It's kept separate from the aggregate Hotels visited (stayed) bar at the top — two complementary stories: which hotels you've stayed at vs. how much you've done at each.
